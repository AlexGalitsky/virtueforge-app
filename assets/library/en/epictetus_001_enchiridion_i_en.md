**The Discourse I**

§ 1. From the things that exist, some are within our control, while others are not. All that is within our power is our thoughts, desires, and choices - in a word, everything that belongs to us. Everything that does not belong to us is beyond our control.

§ 2. That which lies within our control is free from obstacles and impediments, whereas that which is outside of our control is weak, servile, burdened, and alien.

§ 3. Therefore, remember: if you consider yourself free but actually submit to the whims of others, you will suffer hardships, misfortunes, and upheavals, and you will blame the gods and men for your fate. But if you only consider what is yours to be yours, and recognize that everything else is as it truly is, no one can force you, no one can hinder you, and you will not criticize or blame anyone, nor will you commit any act against your will. You will have no enemies, for you will be invulnerable.

§ 4. As you strive for greater goals, remember that you should not pursue them without sufficient motivation. Some of these goals are best left aside, while others must be temporarily set aside. But if you wish to attain your goal and also possess power and wealth, you cannot achieve the latter, for you will be striving for the former alone. At least, you will not attain that which is the source of freedom and happiness.

§ 5. So, strive to silence all unpleasant fancies: "You are merely a mental construct, and you are not what you seem." Then, investigate it and test it according to your own principles. First and foremost, ask yourself whether this vision belongs to what lies within our control or outside of it. And if it belongs to the latter, then have the following thought in mind: "It has no bearing on me."
