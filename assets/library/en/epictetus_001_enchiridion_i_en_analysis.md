### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the Enchiridion, a philosophical work by Seneca written for beginners in Stoicism. It emphasizes the importance of distinguishing between what is within our control and what is not.

### Essence
- The focus of the Enchiridion is on teaching individuals to differentiate between what they can control and what they cannot.
- The texts underscore the importance of recognizing the limits of one's power and making judgments accordingly about what is within our control and what is not.
- Seneca stresses the significance of considering the impact that things beyond our control can have on us.
