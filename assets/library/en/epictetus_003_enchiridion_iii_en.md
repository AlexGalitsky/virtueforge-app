# The Art of Embracing the Mundane
---
In every subject that captivates the soul, brings benefit, and inspires love, remember to remind yourself of its nature, starting with the smallest matters. If you love a pot, say to yourself: "I love a pot." For if it were to shatter, it would not disturb you. If you love your child or wife, say that you love humanity. For if they were to pass away, it would not disturb you.
