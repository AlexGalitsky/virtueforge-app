### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the Enchiridion, an encyclopedia of Stoicism written by Aristo Kirkheuer. It emphasizes the importance of acknowledging and accepting one's own feelings and thoughts.

### Essence
* Accepting and being aware of one's own feelings and thoughts.
* Starting with the examination of the most insignificant objects to understand their impact on us.
* The importance of distinguishing between loving things and loving people.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of humility, as it underscores the importance of recognizing one's own feelings and thoughts rather than suppressing or manipulating them.

### Question to Ponder
How can I differentiate between loving things and loving people?
