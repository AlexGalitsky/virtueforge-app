Whenever you embark on a task, remind yourself of its nature. If you're about to take a bath, imagine the chaos that ensues – people splashing water on each other, pushing and shoving, arguing, and stealing from one another. This will help you approach your task with greater self-awareness, saying to yourself: "I want to bathe while preserving my original intention." Similarly, apply this mindset to every endeavor. In doing so, if something were to hinder your progress, you could say: "I intended not only to bathe but also to maintain my original intention. However, I won't be able to do that if I start complaining about the situation."
