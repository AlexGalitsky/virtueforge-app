### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the Enchiridion, a book by the Stoic philosopher Seneca, in which he offers advice on living and ethics. In this passage, Seneca emphasizes the importance of considering the potential consequences of one's actions and striving to maintain a natural intent.

### Core Message
* View each situation as a unique event rather than part of a broader context.
* Imagine the possible outcomes of your actions to avoid angry emotions.
* Aim to preserve natural intent, resisting external influences.

Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtues of tolerance and the ability to manage one's emotions. It requires one to be capable of viewing situations from multiple perspectives and avoiding angry emotions to maintain one's integrity.
