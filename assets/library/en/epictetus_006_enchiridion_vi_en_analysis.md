### Philosophical Context
In this text, Epicurus emphasizes the importance of distinguishing between personal virtue and external recognition. He stresses that one should be fair to oneself and not boast about the virtues of others.

### Essence
* Personal virtue is the use of mental representations that align with human nature.
* Pride is when one speaks of their own virtues rather than external accolades.
* In this sense, pride is a positive emotion when it is tied to personal virtue.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: The use of mental representations makes you who you are, not external recognitions.

### Connection to Virtue
Pride is a key aspect of personal virtue when it is linked to the use of mental representations. This aligns with the idea that one should take pride in their own well-being.

### Question to Ponder
What is the difference between pride in external recognitions and pride in one's own virtue?
