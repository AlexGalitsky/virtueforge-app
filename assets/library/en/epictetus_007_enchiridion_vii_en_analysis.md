### Philosophical Context
This passage is part of the book "Enchiridion" by Epicurus, written in the 1st century BCE and dedicated to teaching Stoicism.

### Essence
- In this text, Epicurus likens life to a sea voyage and emphasizes the importance of constant vigilance in one's affairs and not straying from them without cause.
- He underscores the necessity of being prepared for any event, regardless of how adverse it may be, and not deviating from one's duties.
- It is crucial to be cautious and not depart from one's "ship" (one's life) without reason.
