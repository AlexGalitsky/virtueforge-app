### Philosophical Context
In this text, Seneca emphasizes the importance of accepting situations as they are, rather than demanding that they change to align with one's desires.

### Essence
* Accepting events as they are.
* Not making claims against fate.
* Taking responsibility for one's life and its outcomes.
* Strengthening inner happiness and tranquility.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is connected to the virtue of patience, as it underscores the importance of accepting situations as they are, rather than demanding that they change to suit one's wishes. This requires a person to be tolerant and adaptable to changing circumstances.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us has never demanded from life what we wanted?
