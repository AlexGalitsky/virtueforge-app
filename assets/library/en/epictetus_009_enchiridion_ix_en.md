**The Enchiridion IX**

# The Obstacle is Not the Enemy

A disease is an obstacle to the body, but not to the mind, unless the mind itself does not want it. A limp is an obstacle to the legs, but not to a wise choice. Say this to yourself in every case. For you will discover that it is an obstacle to something else, not to you.

Remember that in every circumstance, look for an opportunity to use your own ability. When you see a beautiful young man or woman, you will gain self-control. When you are offered a challenge, you will gain courage, and when you suffer hardship, you will gain patience. And the one who cultivates this habit will not be swayed by his fancies.
