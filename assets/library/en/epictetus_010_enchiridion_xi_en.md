Never say you have lost something, but say you have given it away. Your son has died? He was given back. Your wife has passed away? She was given back. A plot of land was taken from you? It was given back. However, the one who takes is dishonorable. But what concern is that to you, if he has demanded it back with his own hands? Be concerned about how long he will give it back to you, and treat him as a guest would treat a hotel guest.
