### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the Enchiridion, a Stoic guide to living and ethics written by Epictetus. It emphasizes the importance of making decisions and taking action based on the situation at hand, rather than blaming others for one's problems.

### Essence
- The ability to perceive situations as they are, rather than as personal losses.
- By giving ourselves to the benefit of others, we are essentially giving back what was given to us.
- We should not concern ourselves with how others return to us what we have given.
- We must care for what we have as we would care for guests in a hotel.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is connected to the virtue of tolerance and the ability to see things from another perspective. It requires us to be able to make decisions and act based on the situation, rather than blaming others for our problems.
