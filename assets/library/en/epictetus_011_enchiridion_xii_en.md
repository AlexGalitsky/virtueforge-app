Enchiridion on Living a Virtuous Life
=====================================

### Section 1

To be successful, cast aside such reasoning: "If I do not provide for my wealth, I will not have enough to eat; and if I do not discipline my son, he will become wicked." For it is better to die with an empty stomach, free from worry and fear, than to live in abundance without peace. It is better that your son becomes wicked than you suffer.

### Section 2

Therefore, begin with small things: does your olive oil leak? Is your wine stolen? Remind yourself that such a price is paid for indifference, that such a price is bought for tranquility, and that nothing is given for free. When you call your servant, keep in mind that he may not hear you, or if he does, he will do nothing as you wish. It is no great benefit to rely on him for your peace of mind.
