### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the book "Enchiridion" (Handbook) by Epicurus, written in the 3rd-4th centuries BCE. In this text, Epicurus emphasizes the importance of patience and humility in achieving success.

### Essence
* Patience and humility are necessary for achieving success.
* One should not strive to appear knowledgeable, but rather focus on internal matters.
* If someone considers you significant, do not believe them, as this is merely an illusion.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: To succeed, one must endure and humble oneself, not striving to appear knowledgeable.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of humility, which is a crucial aspect of Epicurean philosophy. Humility allows individuals to remain impartial and tolerant towards others, which is a key element in achieving success.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us does not strive to appear knowledgeable or significant to others, only to find that this is an illusion?
