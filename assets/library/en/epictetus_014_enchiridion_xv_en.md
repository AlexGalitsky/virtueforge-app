Remember how to behave at a banquet. A dish passed around the table has come to your side? --- Reach out with your hand, taking only what is fitting. People passing by you? Don't delay. Those who have not yet offered it? Do not strain forward in your desire. Wait until the dish comes to you. Handle children, wives, public office, wealth--- and you will become worthy of dining with the gods. If you do not touch it, you will reject what has been placed before you with disdain, and you will even share their power. For, by doing so, Diogenes, Heraclitus, and others like them earned the name of gods.
