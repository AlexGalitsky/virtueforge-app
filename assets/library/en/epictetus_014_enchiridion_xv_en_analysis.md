### Philosophical Context
This text is attributed to Diogenes, a prominent Stoic philosopher, who emphasizes the importance of humility and courtesy in everyday life.

### Core Message
- Behave with dignity and humility in any situation, even during a feast.
- Do not obstruct others if they are passing by, and avoid actions that may lead to anger.
- Wait for the right moment or circumstance before taking action.
- Similarly, apply these principles in dealing with children, spouses, public service, and wealth.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: The Stoic philosopher Diogenes underscores the importance of humility and courtesy in daily life, urging adherence to simple rules of conduct in any situation.

### Connection to Virtue
Applying these principles can foster the development of the virtue of humility, a key aspect of Stoicism. Humility allows individuals to interact with others with dignity and avoid anger or conflict.

### Question to Ponder
Who among the people we frequently encounter in our daily lives might be the most unpredictable and require the most humility?
