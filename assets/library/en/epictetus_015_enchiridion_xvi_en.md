Whenever you see someone crying from grief over the loss of a child abroad or the loss of their possessions, do not let your imagination entice you and do not think that this person is being afflicted by external misfortunes. Instead, have in mind the thought: "This person is being oppressed not by what is happening, but by their own opinion about what is happening." Do not consider it a difficult task to talk to them or, if the situation arises, to cry with them together. However, be cautious of crying within yourself.
