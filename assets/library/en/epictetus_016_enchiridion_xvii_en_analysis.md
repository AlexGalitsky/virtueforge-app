### Philosophical Context
This passage from Epictetus's Enchiridion underscores the importance of adaptation and acceptance of one's role in life. It reflects Stoicism, which emphasizes the importance of external behavior and utility.

### Essence
* The actor must fit the role that is offered to them.
* It is crucial to perform the given role well, rather than choosing it for oneself.
* Accepting the role is a key aspect of decision-making in life.
* The role does not define the person, but the person must fit into it.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of honesty and underscores the importance of adaptation and acceptance of one's role in life, which is a key aspect of this virtue.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us has not played a role that we did not like? How can we learn from this role and make it our own?
