### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the book "On Virtue" (Enchiridion) by the Athenian philosopher Nicotarch, written in the 1st or 2nd century BCE. In this text, Nicotarch emphasizes the importance of critical thinking and the realization of the impact of all situations on oneself.

### Core Message
* Critical Thinking: Analyzing bad thoughts and not getting carried away by them.
* Perspective: Seeing well-being in every event, even those that seem negative.
* Self-Control: Deriving benefit from all situations that arise.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Critical thinking and the awareness of well-being should be the foundation for problem-solving.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is connected to the virtue of humility (represented in VirtueForge as Chastity), as it underscores the importance of awareness and control over one's thoughts and actions.

### Question to Ponder
Can you ever let your imagination run wild with negative thoughts, and if so, how do you bring yourself back to reality?
