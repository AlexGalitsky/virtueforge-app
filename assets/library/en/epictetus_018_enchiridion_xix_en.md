# The Path to Invincibility

§ 1. You will be invincible if you do not participate in competitions that are beyond your reach.

§ 2. When you see someone deserving of honor and power, or someone who is renowned for other reasons, beware of being swayed by their example, lest you think them happy. For if the essence of good lies within our control, then there is no room for envy or resentment. Rather, strive to be free from the desire to emulate others, to aspire to positions of power, or to seek fame. The only path to this freedom is to despise everything that lies beyond our control.
