### Philosophical Context
This text is part of Seneca's *Enchiridion*, a philosophical work in which he outlines his principles of virtue and humility. In this passage, Seneca emphasizes the importance of recognizing the limits of one's power and avoiding envy and jealousy.

### Essence
- To be invincible, one must abstain from contests where victory is unattainable.
- Upon encountering individuals who have attained greater power or fame, it is crucial not to be swayed by their perceptions and to consider them happy, but instead to focus on what is within our control.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Maintaining freedom and humility is linked to the principle of not envying or being jealous of others, but instead focusing on what is within our control.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us is truly free from envy and jealousy?
