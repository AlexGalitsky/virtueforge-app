### Philosophical Context
In this text, Seneca emphasizes the importance of keeping death constantly in one's thoughts as a means to curb selfish desires and prevent excess.

### Essence
- Death should be ever-present in a person's mind to prevent them from thinking about base things and desiring more than is necessary.
- The presence of death helps to restrain selfish desires and keep a person from excess.
- This requires constant presence and focus on death so that a person can better manage their thoughts and actions.
- Seneca underscores the importance of restraining selfish desires and preventing excess, enabling one to live a more moral and balanced life.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of humility, which includes the ability to restrain selfish desires and prevent oneself from excess. Seneca highlights the importance of humility as a means to achieve a moral and balanced way of life.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us can truly be free from the fear of death if we still think about it?
