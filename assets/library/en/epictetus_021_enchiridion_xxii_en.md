# The Philosopher's Preparation for Mockery

If you aspire to philosophy, prepare yourself for the fact that many will mock you, saying "Where did this person come from? He has arrived at our doorstep as a philosopher" and "Where did we get such hardness?" And you should not be hard. Hold fast to what appears best to you, as if it were placed upon you by God. And remember, if you preserve your convictions, those who previously mocked you will later admire you. But if you yield, you will suffer double mockery.
