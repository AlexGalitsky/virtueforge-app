### Philosophical Context
Stoicism is a philosophical school that emphasizes the importance of reasoning, self-control, and decision-making based on personal convictions. This text is part of the Enchiridion, a work by the Stoic philosopher Marcus Aurelius.

### Essence
* Philosophers often face ridicule and criticism for their beliefs.
* One must be prepared for such reactions and maintain a state of sternness, however unpleasant this may be.
* The state of "sternness" is a necessary condition for preserving personal convictions and making decisions based on them.

### Form of This Version
Prose

### Connection to Virtue
Sternness and the preservation of personal beliefs are linked to dignity and honesty, which are crucial aspects of virtue. As Franklin Weeks notes in relation to chastity, sternness and impartiality are essential for moral growth.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us is willing to uphold their beliefs, even if it means facing ridicule and criticism?
