### Philosophical Context
This text is attributed to the Athenian philosopher Seneca, who was a prominent thinker during the Middle Ages. In the Enchiridion, the fundamental principles of Stoicism and their application to daily life are presented.

### Essence
* External events should not influence one's inner order and peace.
* Self-sufficiency is a key concept that enables one to rely on oneself and not be dependent on others.
* A philosopher should prioritize inner development over external recognition and accolades.

### Form of This Version
Prose with elements of paradigm.

### Connection to Virtue
Seneca's advice reminds us of the importance of self-control and inner strength in achieving virtues such as Chastity and Temperance, as described by Franklin's weeks of Order.

### Question to Ponder
Does everything that makes you self-reliant also make you independent of external opinions?
