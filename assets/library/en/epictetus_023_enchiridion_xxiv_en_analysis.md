### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the work "On the Practice of Virtue" (Enchiridion) by the ancient Greek philosopher Seneca, written in verse. In this text, Seneca critiques the notion that individuals should prioritize their personal interests and well-being over the public good.

### Essence
- Seneca asserts that we cannot be unhappy due to another person's actions.
- Our concern should be focused on what is within our power, rather than what lies beyond our control.
- Virtue and modesty are essential qualities for us to be of service to society.
- Seneca emphasizes the importance of remaining true and modest when making decisions.

### Form of This Version
