Duties are largely determined by one's character. Is he a father? It is implied that one should care for him, yield to his authority, and tolerate his criticism and reproof. Yet, he is not a good father. You are bound to him by nature, not because he is good, but simply because he is your father. Your brother offends you. Preserve your position towards him as you would towards a brother, pay no attention to his words, and only observe the actions that align with your natural inclination. For it is not another person who can cause harm to you if you do not wish it so. You will only feel harm when you begin to think about it. In this way, you will come to understand the nature of duties towards a neighbor, citizen, commander – once you have grown accustomed to recognizing their characteristics.
