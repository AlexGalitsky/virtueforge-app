### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the book "Manual of Virtue" (Enchiridion), an Aristotelian work written in 3 AD, and serves as a guide to ethics and morality for a military leader.

### Essence
* Duties are largely defined by one's qualities.
* One should be yielding and tolerant, disregarding hostile words from others.
* Intentions should align with human nature rather than actions.
* Harm only occurs when we think about harm.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Duties are largely defined by one's qualities, and therefore one should be yielding and tolerant.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is connected to the virtue of tolerance, which is a crucial aspect of Aristotelian ethics.
