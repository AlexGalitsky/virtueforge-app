### Philosophical Context
This text is part of Seneca's "Enchiridion," a work by the Stoic philosopher written in the 1st century CE. It presents principles of virtue and piety, which are central concepts in Stoicism.

### Essence
* Piety towards the gods entails acknowledging their existence and their just governance of the world.
* Actions towards the gods should align with their will, not against it.
* Harm cannot bring joy, and benefit cannot bring pleasure.
* Piety is associated with the proper pursuit and avoidance.
* Making libations and sacrifices is a way to demonstrate piety.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Piety is linked to the proper pursuit and avoidance, which is a key aspect of virtue.
