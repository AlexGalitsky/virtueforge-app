### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the Enchiridion by Marcus Aurelius, a philosophical work in which he compiled his thoughts on virtue and the way of life. The passage discusses divination and oracles and how to properly utilize them in decision-making.

### Essence
- Approach the predictions of oracles with caution and critical thinking.
- Do not rely solely on divinations or external factors for making decisions.
- Use the oracle as an advisor, but do not rely on it indiscriminately.
- It is crucial to exercise sound judgment and critical reasoning, even when consulting oracles.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: When you consult divinations or oracles, remember that the prediction is unknown, and you are seeking it from the seer. Use the oracle as an advisor, but do not rely on it blindly.

### Connection to Virtue
Using an oracle to make decisions can be linked to the virtues of honesty and justice. If you use the oracle as an advisor but also consider common sense and critical thinking, it can help you make well-founded decisions that align with your values.
