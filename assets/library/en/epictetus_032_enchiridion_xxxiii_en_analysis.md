### Philosophical Context
This text is a moral guide written by Seneca, a Greek philosopher and statesman, offering advice on behavior and morality to achieve virtue and happiness.

### Essence

* Adopt a character and type that you will maintain even when alone.
* Keep silent and speak only when necessary.
* Redirect conversations to appropriate topics.
* Avoid swearing, and if you do, refrain from fulfilling your oath.
* Reject feasts with unfamiliar people.
* Limit physical concerns to what is essential.
* Discard all that pertains to fame and pleasure.
* Maintain purity in romantic affairs until marriage.
* Do not defend yourself against negative reviews; instead, respond, "This person is unaware of my other faults..."
* There is no need to frequently attend spectacles and endure difficulties.
* Maintain seriousness and composure during public readings.
* Imagine what Socrates or Zeno would do in your situation.
