When you begin to fantasize about some pleasure, beware and do not be swayed by these fancies, as with all things. Let the pleasure wait, and take a delay for yourself. Then recall the time when you will indulge in this pleasure, and the time when, having indulged, you will regret it and scold yourself. Set against this, consider how, by resisting, you will rejoice and praise yourself. If you think that the time has come to act, look out so that sweetness, pleasantness, and attractiveness, which are characteristic of pleasures, do not overpower you. Compare how much more enjoyable it is to realize that you have conquered all these things.
