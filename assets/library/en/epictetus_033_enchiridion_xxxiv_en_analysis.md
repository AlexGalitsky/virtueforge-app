### Philosophical Context
This passage from the Enchiridion is part of the Stoic philosophical tradition and emphasizes the importance of restraint in relation to pleasures.

### Essence
- Engaging in fantasies and anticipating pleasures can lead to regret and self-criticism.
- Abstaining from pleasures allows one to take joy and praise oneself for it.
- Practicing restraint over pleasures gives one the victory over sweetness, pleasantness, and allure.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Restraint in relation to pleasures is connected to the virtue of temperance, which involves controlling one's desires and emotions to achieve balance and wisdom.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us has not regretted our indulgences?
