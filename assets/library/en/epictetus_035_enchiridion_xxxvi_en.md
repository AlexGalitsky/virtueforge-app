The expressions "day has come" and "night has fallen" hold great significance in distinguishing between separation and union. For instance, if one were to take the greater part of food, although it is essential for the body, it lacks value in preserving modest behavior and communal unity at a banquet. Therefore, every time you dine with another person, remember that it is not only necessary to scrutinize the quality of the offerings for the body, but also to keep shame before the host who has arranged the feast.
