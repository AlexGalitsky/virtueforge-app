### Philosophical Context
In this text, Seneca emphasizes the importance of distinguishing between food for the body and food for society. He underscores that while food is necessary for the body, it does not hold significance for maintaining modesty and social cohesion at a banquet.

### Core
* It is crucial to differentiate between food for the body and food for society.
* Food for the body is a physical necessity, whereas food for society is a social aspect.
* Modesty and social unity at a banquet are essential for upholding social norms and behavior.

### Connection to Virtue
Shame is a key aspect of virtue, as it helps us maintain social norms and behave in accordance with societal expectations. Therefore, preserving shame in the face of a banquet host is crucial for sustaining virtue.
