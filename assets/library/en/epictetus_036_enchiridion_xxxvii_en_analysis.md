### Philosophical Context
This passage is from the Enchiridion, a moral guide in Stoic philosophy written by Epictetus. It emphasizes the importance of considering one's capabilities and limitations when making decisions and taking responsibility for one's actions.

### Essence
* One should be aware of their limitations when exceeding their role or capacity.
* Accepting a role beyond one's capabilities can lead to the inability to fulfill it.
* This underscores the importance of assessing one's abilities and boundaries when making decisions.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtues of honesty (veritas) and self-awareness of one's own limits.

### Question to Ponder
How can I become more aware of my limitations to avoid overstepping my role?
