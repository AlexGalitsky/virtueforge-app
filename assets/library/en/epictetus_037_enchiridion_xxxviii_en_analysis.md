### Philosophical Context
This text is part of the book "On Goodness" (Enchiridion) by Saint Augustine, a philosopher and theologian. The book serves as a guide for living a virtuous life and emphasizes the importance of moral principles in everyday life.

### Essence
* Pay attention to not harming the ruling principle of your mind.
* Maintain this principle in all your actions to minimize risk to yourself.
* Take responsibility for your thoughts and behavior.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
By preserving the ruling principle of one's mind, a person can develop moral qualities such as honesty, justice, and tolerance, which are key aspects of virtue.

### Question to Ponder
How can I ensure that my ruling principle remains untainted by external influences and emotions?
