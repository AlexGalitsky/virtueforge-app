One's possessions are their body, just as the foot is the measure for shoes. If you adhere to this principle, you will preserve moderation, and if you exceed it, you will inevitably fall into decline, like a shoe that has exceeded its proper size. For just as shoes that have grown too large become first gilded, then dyed purple, and finally embroidered, so too do the excesses of one's possessions lead them to be first adorned with gold, then dyed in purple, and eventually embroidered with unnecessary embellishments.
