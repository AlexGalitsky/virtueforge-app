### Philosophical Context
This text reflects Stoic philosophy, emphasizing the importance of moral behavior, restraint, and controlled influence on the environment. Stoics used this text to encourage women to engage in moral conduct.

### Essence
- It is crucial to distinguish between modesty and shame with other qualities.
- Women engaging in sexual activity do not necessarily need to be concerned about their appearance.
- Women possess their own values and dignity.

### Connection to Virtue
The Stoic perspective underscores the importance of distinguishing between modesty and shame, advocating for the encouragement of women to engage in moral behavior that is not contingent upon their appearance. This aligns with the idea that women have their own values and dignity.
