When someone inflicts harm upon you or speaks ill of you, remember that they are acting in accordance with their duty. It is impossible for them to follow your will if it conflicts with theirs. Therefore, if their perception of you appears false to them, the one who has been deceived suffers the harm. For if true union is considered false by someone, it is not the union itself that suffers, but rather the individual who has fallen into error. Based on these principles, you will remain indifferent to your critic. In each instance, respond with: "It seemed so to him."
