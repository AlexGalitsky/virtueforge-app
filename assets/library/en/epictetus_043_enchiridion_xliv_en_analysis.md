### Philosophical Context
This text is an excerpt from Marcus Aurelius's book "On Virtue" (Enchiridion), where he explores the concept of comparison and its impact on human behavior.

### Essence
- Comparing oneself to others can lead to an inappropriate multiplicity of existence.
- Instead, one should focus on their own virtue rather than comparing themselves to others.
- Actions and qualities of a person are not possessions or characteristics that can be attributed to others.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Recognizing one's own worthiness and detaching it from comparisons with others relates to the understanding and practice of virtue as discussed in Franklin Weeks' Order.

### Question to Ponder
Do you compare yourself with others rather than with your own worthiness?
