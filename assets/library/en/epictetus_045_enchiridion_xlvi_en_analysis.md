### Philosophical Context
This text is authored by Socrates in collaboration with Nicodemus, serving as a guide for philosophers emphasizing the importance of applying moral principles in practice.

### Essence
* Do not strive to be a philosopher, but rather follow the teachings presented in these instructions.
* Practical application of moral principles over theoretical discourse.
* It is crucial to approach discussions with simplicity and without self-disdain.

### Form of This Version
paraphrase: follow the teachings presented in these instructions rather than striving to be a philosopher.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of honesty (sincerity), as it underscores the importance of applying moral principles in practice and avoiding self-disdain.
