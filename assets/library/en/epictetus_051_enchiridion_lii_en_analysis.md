### Philosophical Context
This passage from Epictetus' Enchiridion offers a critical assessment of the structure of philosophical reasoning and its connection to the virtue of truth.

### Essence
- In philosophy, it is crucial to distinguish between utility, proofs, and assertions.
- Proofs are necessary for assertions, and assertions are essential for understanding what is true and what is false.
- Truth is a paramount aspect of virtue, as it is linked to the correctness of opinions and actions.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Truth is a key aspect of the virtue of honesty and integrity in dealings with others.

### Question to Ponder
Should one prioritize truth when considering philosophical proofs, or should other values take precedence?
