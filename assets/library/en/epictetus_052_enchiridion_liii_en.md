# The Art of Living LIII

### § 1

Lead me, O Zeus, lead and you, Fate,
To the place that is destined for me.

I will go hunting, but if my desires are not present,
I will go with shame, to where I am appointed.

### § 2

He who yields to necessity,
Is wiser among mortals and the gods.

### § 3

But, Kriton, if it pleases the gods so be it.

### § 4

Anith and Melit can kill me, but they cannot do harm.
