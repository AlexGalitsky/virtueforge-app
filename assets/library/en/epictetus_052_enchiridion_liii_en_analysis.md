### Philosophical Context
This text is from the Enchiridion, a moral treatise written by Seneca, a Stoic philosopher. The text is intended to instruct readers on how to live virtuously and yield to the inevitability of fate.

### Essence
* To live in accordance with the will of God.
* To yield to the inevitability of fate without shame.
* Wisdom consists in yielding to fate, as it does to the gods.
* The honest person may be killed but cannot harm others.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
A clear expression of Stoic philosophy, emphasizing yielding to the inevitability of fate and living in accordance with the will of God. This aligns with the principles of virtue, which advocate living in accordance with one's abilities and circumstances.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us can be wise if they do not yield to the inevitability of fate?
