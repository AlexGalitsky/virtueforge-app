# Friendship

Dear Sencen,

You write that the letter will reveal your friend, and immediately beg not to share with him about "the secret of two".

Define - is he a friend or an enemy?

If it's one, then he shares your soul.

You won't hide anything in your inner depths...

Maybe he's just a "chance lord"?

Firstly, judge: does he deserve friendship?

And if you decide to trust him, as fate dictates.

Doubting makes the unnecessary and treacherous towards oneself.

One is ready to trust everyone who passes by...

Another cannot trust himself...

Who believes in everything - is noble, though,

Risking losing trust.

Who is busy with trivialities - deserves respect?!

Striving for superficiality breeds a weak spirit.

Who conserves every movement...

Is either a sinner or a libertine, dear friend.

As Pomponius wrote in his time:

"There are people who are more pleasant in the darkness,
So much so that they can't see light!"

This is food for the soul and mind!

We need rest if we've done something.

And night is necessary, since your day was harsh,

Who is wise - combines everything wisely,

Seeking advice from nature.

Be healthy.
