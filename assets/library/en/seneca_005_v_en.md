# On the External Form

Lucilius greets Seneca!

I am delighted by your perseverance,
which has achieved considerable success.
Be bold and proceed, undaunted by obstacles.

I must warn you: do not try to be
like everyone else in appearance.

Otherwise, you will create barriers for yourself
in communication with people, my wise friend.

Your uniqueness is hidden:
You are different from the crowd by spirit.

Strive to live better, but remain humble,
and do not seek to impress people with your wealth.

What is the greatest gift of philosophy?

The ability to live among people.

Do not dress in a way that makes you appear arrogant,
but let wisdom illuminate each day!

A philosopher who lives modestly
is not one who lacks humility or courage.

By doing so, you will dispel the darkness
and people will flock to you in large numbers.

You ask: "How will I be different from those
who are ignorant of philosophy?"

For many, your spirit will be endearing,
not your home, tableware, or possessions.

Those who are poor in spirit and wealth
are often envious of what others have.

Eat like silver, but let gold belong to your soul,
my dear friend. Hecaton, the wise one, has discovered
the remedy for fear: "To stop fearing,
simply forget all your hopes in an instant:
They create a chain that crushes you."

Hopes are a source of anxiety and worry,
and forward-thinking thoughts can be overwhelming.

But foresight is a wonderful opportunity,
and unfulfilled expectations can torment the soul.

Danger drives a wild animal to flee.
But fear will pass, and it will find its place soon enough.

What matters in past or future losses?
Your life belongs to you alone today.

Be healthy.
