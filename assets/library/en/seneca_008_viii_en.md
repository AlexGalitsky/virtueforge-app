# Letter VIII (Advice to Posterity)

Dear Seneca,

"How do I spend my time?" - I am not idle,
I do not close my eyes until late at night,
I do not cease my diligent work.

I have abandoned my own affairs... Here you will say with great subtlety:

"For what are you sitting up all night?"

I answer: These nocturnal vigils are for posterity,
I hope to help them in some way.

Just as they compose valuable remedies,
So I spend the night writing down words of counsel
For people and states, not "for glory" or with pure thoughts.
I write to them about that starry road
That I have found myself, through wandering and straying.

I regret having discovered it so late in life,
But I believe it will be useful to them.

I cry out: Do not rely on hopes:
They are the beginning of your downfall!

And greed, and foolish ignorance,
Will catch you off guard like a wild animal...

They think their gain is in their pocket...
But when the forests are cut down sharply,
The voice says: "Fortune, dear friend, do not deceive me!"

This is what lies at the bottom of faith in miracles...
For indulgence to the body is not luxury:
It is simply forgetting about the soul.

To live with a thatched roof is no worse than to live with gold.
Warmth is not found in a luxurious house,
But in a humble one.

Whoever has created something without flaw
Has wasted his time and effort.
There is greatness in one's own mind,
But those who deny it are deceiving people.
When I return to my posterity on this Monday,
I will be more grateful than when the idler
Added his hand to the list of rewards.

It is time to end. I remember Epicurus:
"Philosophy is free, and its slaves are many!"
A philosopher (and also in literature)
Is great because he has freed himself from struggle.
Poets say a lot of things like this,
But it would be better if philosophers could say them
And their thoughts could soar above the chains...
"Everything that is given to us can be taken away"

Your words have wisdom in them.

My duty remains the same as when I took it,
I will pass it on to you later.

Be healthy.
