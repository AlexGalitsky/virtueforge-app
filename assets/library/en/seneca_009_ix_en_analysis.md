### Philosophical Context
This text is a letter from Sotion to Seneca, emphasizing the importance of friendship and relationships in the life of a wise person.

### Core Message
* Wisdom and friendship are intertwined: one who is wise will always find a friend.
* Friendship is not a benefit to be obtained from others, but rather something that arises from within.
* A sage does not need friends for happiness, but they can help him become stronger and braver.
* Folly does not appreciate the value of friendships, whereas wisdom allows one to be truly happy, not just in words but in deed.

### Connection to Virtue
Wisdom and friendship are linked to the virtues of honesty and justice. Sotion underscores the importance of being truthful and honest in relationships, rather than merely seeking advantage or recognition.

### Question for Reflection
How can I be wiser and braver in my relationships with others?
