Title: Who Is Your Best Friend?

---
# Who Is Your Best Friend?

Lucilla greets Seneca!

Do you want a friend? Be one to yourself.

You possess the image of a great Man,

You are the best who can save you from woe.

While walking, Crates, a disciple of Stypon,

Saw a young man alone:

"Being alone is perilous,
There are reasons for it."

Being alone is foolish:
In them arrogance and anger grow like yeast...

A sin of the soul, among noisy companies,
They were forced to hide and be ashamed and afraid.

It seems they have no face:
No one dares to question them...

But (foolishness has no cure),
He reveals himself in all his glory.

Ask for health for your soul from God,

Then ask for it for your body.

And know that you are asking for very little,
For God, for the soul, there is strength.

Live with people under the watchful eye of God,

Mend your way and ask for the blessings of the righteous.

I believe that you will rise high,

If God is with you.

Be healthy.
