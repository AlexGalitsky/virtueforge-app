### Philosophical Context
Letter X is Seneca's sermon on the importance of friendships and the importance of self-care rather than care for external people.

### Essence
- In the letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of inner development and self-care.
- He asserts that being a friend to oneself is the primary condition for achieving virtue.
- Seneca also underscores the importance of prayer and petitioning God for the health of one's soul.
