**Letter XI (On the Spiritual Father)**

Lucillia greets Seneca!

Yesterday, my young friend came to me. I understood that he is making progress:

He has suddenly brightened up in conversation.

Shyness is a good sign for youth,
It will remain with them forever.

What is born with fortune lives within us until we die.

Those who speak before an audience often freeze or blush,
Their hands tremble like on the fifth day of a harvest,
Their faces pale, as if they have been struck by lightning.

Others become tough and resolute when they blush,
Like beets that have turned red.

Sulla blushed... and became terrifying,
By calming down cities with his sternness.

Fabius in the senate was only more excellent,
And not always a rosy complexion - a sign of shame.

A rosy complexion is innate to us,
It cannot be overcome by a wise spirit.

We cannot change the weather,
Whether we blush or not, it's one of two things.

To make an actor cry on stage,
He leans his head against the wall,
And whispers quietly - "wall, penitence"

But he can't always blush.

Always keep in mind, before your eyes,

An example of a good guardian.

Strive for him, and give him an examination:

Live and act, correct your mistakes.

If you wanted to say something bad,
Ask him, like a son before his father.

Oh, happy is the one who becomes a worthy example
In thoughts, he becomes someone worthy of praise.

Only "on the straight line" can we correct
The inaccuracies of the drawn contours.

Search for that whose name you can glorify,
And - you will be glorified yourself.

Be healthy.
