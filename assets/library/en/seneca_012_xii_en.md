# Letter XII (On the Expectation of Death)

Dear Seneca,

I recently left town...

What did I see: all the orchards, like cripples,
And they were my own that I planted.

My estate has lost its appearance:

The stones are crumbling, the roof is leaking.

And it's not because of a lack of management,
He says, "The estate has recovered."

I built it too, full of strength...

Even if I couldn't return the days that have passed,

It wouldn't be wrong to feel blood in anticipation of death

I breathe now deeper and more evenly.

What is this old man sitting in the shadows near the door?

I don't want someone else's corpse...

"You gave me a doll," he may say. Perhaps I can believe it?

That, son of a slave... looks like an elder than his age.

I am old, but old age is a pleasure,

The last fruits are sweeter,

And children are like a beautiful vision,

When they leave childhood, their traces remain.

To the one who drinks, the last cup is sweet,

Which completes the journey to the bottom,

Only the previous ones leave scratches.

Like a pleasant breath before drowning.

You will say: "Death is pleasant?! - Impossible...

Yes, how can you worship it?!

All ages are truly enjoyable when life rushes by at an angle.

Everyone should know that death's hour is near,

And young men and mature men:

It's not determined by age on this list,

The one who... has lived his life.

He doesn't look as old as the others, so dry,

That you can't believe in him even for a day more,

Day by day, their account is measured by fortune's whim

Death takes away with its fingers.

"One day is equal to everyone else,"

Said the cloudy Heraclitus.

A day will come, and everything falls into someone,

And all that was suffering - no longer suffers.

Live your day as if it were your last,

"Having lived life" - say this before falling asleep...

It's not so scary, since immortality is just a myth...

And in the morning... God will help bring back life.

I'll add one more: "Living in need is bad,

But there's no need to live for you."

Only striving with God's blessing

To overcome your own need.

You will say: "This is an Epicurean phrase..."

On what does this crown, laurel, mean to you?

All the truth that exists in literature

Is mine, and I'm willing to share it.

Be healthy.
