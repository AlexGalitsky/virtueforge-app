# Letter XIV (On Dangers)

Lucilius greets Seneca,

I agree - the body should be preserved...

Do not become a slave to his care,
Dignity throws him into the fire.

Poverty, pain, and violence are terrifying,
The last is the most frightening,
It comes, even if we beg for it

Do not touch it, and shudder at its sight:

Imagine it, (let us only dream of it),

Like a lavish procession, with chains and whips,

It throws us into the darkness of a dungeon cell

With hopes, wives, and children.

The arsenal of means to torture is in his hands,

A palae can defeat a victim:

And victims leave all attempts at resistance,

In fear of harm.

Who should we be afraid of more:

Government, senate, or people?

To avoid the wrath of those in power,

You must, like a sailor navigating a stormy sea.

How to escape from the threat of the underworld?

Do not wish for the same as they do.

Do not be rich, so that at nightfall

You will not meet robbers behind a wall.

Avoid envy, hatred, and contempt.

What? - only wisdom can give advice.

Moderation will reward you with insight,

How to equalize these evils:

To engage in philosophy is fitting,

And to dive into the depths of ideas.

I do not believe that this name will become

A curse, not worthy of respect among people.

But to pursue it must be done quietly:

Take - Mark Caton, who intervened in the war...

Philosophy was his profession, but he ended up with a bitter illness:

After Pompey's defeat, he went down into the depths.

You will say: Only the best can win?

And the worst gets the glory of victory.

Is this your business? Often, by chance,

It is possible to separate success from misfortune.

Can I say that there will be no sorrow

For those who follow these rules?

Dangers are like a ship in open sea,

More dangerous than being in harbor when the time comes.

A person's enterprise is restless and impatient,

Risks everything to get a basket of gold.

There may be an innocent victim,

And idleness does not save from danger...

Whoever is wise, sees the plan at first

And knows the path, without leaving the gate:

When the ship sailed away from the shore,

Fortunes were already decided.

"Only one will find joy in possessing wealth

Only that person who fears goodness."

Receive this slogan as a daily payment,

Again, praise someone else's.

Be healthy.
