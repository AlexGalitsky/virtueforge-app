### Philosophical Context
This text is part of Seneca's book "Virtues and Morality," written in 1-2 CE in Latin verse.

### Essence
- In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of restraining one's emotions and desires to maintain freedom and dignity.
- He warns of various dangers that may threaten people, particularly in relation to power and wealth.
- Seneca also underscores the importance of wisdom and balance in life.

### Form of This Version
prose

### Connection to Virtue
Corresponds to the principle of chastity as described in Franklin's Week Order. Seneca stresses the need for control over one's own desires and passions to preserve moral integrity.

### Question to Ponder
How can one balance the pursuit of success with the risk of becoming its victim?
