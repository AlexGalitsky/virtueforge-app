### Philosophical Context
Letter XV by Seneca is written in verse and addresses the theme of health, which was significant in Greek philosophy. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of health not only in a physical sense but also in terms of spiritual well-being.

### Essence
* Health is not merely physical but also pertains to the soul.
* Muscles are worthless without the mind's diligence and rest.
* The body needs training, but the mind requires no effort.
* Time is a value to be considered in movement.
* Life is a quest for beauty and understanding of the world.

### Form of This Version
Prose

### Connection to Virtue
Health is linked to the virtue of health, a crucial aspect of Seneca's philosophy. This aligns with the principles of Franklin Weeks Order, which underscores the importance of physical activity and a healthy lifestyle in achieving virtue.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us truly seeks health and virtue rather than merely material comforts?
