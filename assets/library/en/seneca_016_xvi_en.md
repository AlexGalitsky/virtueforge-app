# On the Road to Happiness

Lucilius greets Seneca!

You know that wisdom is the foundation of happiness.

If all knew from A to Z,

You would already be happier.

But, what's obvious needs development...(Don't argue with me?- I'm right.)

Your gentle pursuits are transforming your will into a beautiful character.

I hope I'm not wrong about you. And I wish for this too...

For there is no easy way to believe in oneself,

Until one has sorted out their affairs.

Wisdom is not a pleasant day without boredom,

But the strength and moderation of the soul.

Her counsel - more important than any science

For those tasks that need to be solved.

Someone might say: "What's the point of it all?"

The chance, fate, or divine power rule everything."

But wisdom teaches you not to give up to fate,

To endure the uncertainty, and understand it.

It's not time yet to know what's within our power,

And there's no need to rush with a solution.

The soul's striving - your path to happiness,

Until it becomes... a state of the soul.

A gift: "If you're guided by your spirit,

You can never be poor."

And if you listen to the crowd's noise,

Say goodbye to wealth forever."

Yes - Epictetus... Nature craves little,

But humans crave infinitely, whatever they have.

Everyone thinks that excess is not a problem...

They don't know the shame of it.

The nature of things is right in front of us,

And the falsehood of opinions is an endless ocean...

The path to the goal is on the road where you're walking.

Blind wandering will kill you sooner.

When, after 100 days, you have only

More days left until your goal than ants,

Know that your goal was not born from nature,

But in it, you can see the way out of excess.
