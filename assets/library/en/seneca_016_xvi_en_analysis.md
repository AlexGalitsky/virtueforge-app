### Philosophical Context
Letter XVI by Seneca is part of his philosophical work "Consolation," in which he elucidates the principles of Stoicism and emphasizes the importance of wisdom, balance, and humility in achieving happiness.

### Core Themes
- Wisdom is the key to happiness.
- Wisdom requires development and practice.
- It enables one to transform the will of a person into a beautiful character.
- Wisdom teaches one not to yield to fate but to focus on inner drive.
- The path to one's goal is a journey where one measures oneself.

The corresponding principle of Stoicism is the virtue of frugality, which includes humility, moderation, and the renunciation of excess. Seneca emphasizes the importance of humility and moderation in achieving happiness.
