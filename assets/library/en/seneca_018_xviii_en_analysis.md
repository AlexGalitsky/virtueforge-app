### Philosophical Context
This text is part of Seneca's philosophical letters to Lucilius, in which he offers advice on life, morality, and virtue. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of patience, resilience, and humility in achieving well-being.

### Essence
- Seneca advises Lucilius to adhere to a strict diet and wear austere clothing to demonstrate virtue in her life.
- He compares those who are not afraid of their fate to soldiers who display valor before battle.
- Seneca underscores the importance of humility and patience in achieving well-being.
- He warns Lucilius that joy and wealth can be fleeting, but humility and resilience can lead to long-term well-being.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Seneca advises Lucilius to adhere to a strict diet and wear austere clothing to demonstrate virtue in her life, and he emphasizes the importance of humility and patience in achieving well-being.

### Connection to Virtue
Seneca links humility and resilience to virtue, a key concept in Epicurean philosophy. He suggests that Lucilius should follow Epicurean principles to achieve well-being and happiness.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us can be "defeated by fortune" if we cannot control our fate?
