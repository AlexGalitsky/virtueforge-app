# Letter XIX (On Leisure)

Lucillia greets Seneca!

I welcome any of your letters.

Try to remove all distractions:

* We are imprisoned by our duties.

It is not a cry for idleness, but for leisure:

* To spend one's life at sea,
* To find a harbor for death without fear,
* Where you can quietly lay your head down.

Now you have no choice:

* You are already famous by fate's decree.

Your light shines brightly, even if it plunges into the depths of hell,

Because it is the work that you have done.

There are those with whom you cannot live in solitude!

And there are those who will wait for you, grieving!

You will understand that losing others is less painful than...

Losing yourself.

What are you looking for? The fulfillment of all your desires?

The chain of endless longing is endless and unending...

It's better to have a knife at your throat with a firm hand,

Than to be trapped in an endless cycle of suffering under their yoke.

Is it not better... to have enough, but also to be content together?

While you are still blinded by the world's powerlessness,

Everyone is ready to be blinded along with you.

So what is the way out? Think for yourself how you want...

Your destiny is now in your hands.

The more success you achieve, the greater your honor

But the less sleep you get, haunted by fear.

"Lightning strikes at the highest points"

That's a saying of the Muses... 

He ended badly, in soft blankets,

But he could have been a model of eloquence...

I will conclude again with the words of Epicurus:

"Look at who you eat and drink, then - what you eat."

In solitude, there is a field for culture,

Among your "friends", there are many fools.

When you do good for others,

You are more likely to find hatred in them.

Our charitable acts bring us less benefit,

And instead we get envy, anger, and lies.

Until you have a mind, listen to the wise:

Do not give your kindness and blood to everyone,

You will be disappointed less in the morning,

And you will sleep peacefully.

Be healthy.
