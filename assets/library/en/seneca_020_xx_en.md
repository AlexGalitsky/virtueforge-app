# The Letter XX (On Word and Deed)

Lucillia greets Seneca!

Is your health good? I am glad to hear from you.

Philosophy is not acquired in a library,

Its wisdom is recognized by deeds.

The mark of wisdom is the unity of word and deed,

 Remain yourself with all, regardless.

You ask: "Who was able to do this?"

Few... Do not boast of my humility.

Look around - you will find contradictions:

Is your home, clothes, table equal?

Do you speak to everyone with the same tone?

The difference is given by steadfastness.

No one knows firmly what they want,

And if they know - there is no struggle in it.

In the morning, they want what they wanted at night

They have doubted and even condemned themselves...

Wanting and rejecting always the same thing

Is a mark of wisdom, from which comes trouble.

It cannot be unjust or wrong to attract us always.

Most people have desires like dough,

Striving achieves the highest peaks!

Let's assume this place is not a peak...

But knowing about it will make you alone.

"What should I do with my entourage?"

Their poverty can offend me...

Who will stay with you in poverty?

That person loves!

Is poverty not lovely and easy to bear

Because, with it, there is security?

Nature has given us very little,

No one is born rich.

We nurse our first milk from the breast,

And sleep, hiding behind a small cloak.

So we begin... Further - the kingdoms are narrow,

We lack gold, tents...

Why?!- Nature's secret is unknown...

Who was born rich?

Be healthy.
