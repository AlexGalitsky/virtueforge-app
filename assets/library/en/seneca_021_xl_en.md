# Letter XL (On Light and Shine)

Dear Seneca,

Do you think you are striving for people?

The shine of this life is like the sparkle of snow that has fallen on city streets.

A former life, a new one; different as the shine - starlight.

Shine - reflection of good and bad:

The shadow has fallen... Where is the shine? - The trail has disappeared...

My Epicure wrote to Idomenaeus:

"Are you seeking fame? You will find it in my letter

You will find recognition, far more worthy than any achievement."

He was not wrong... Who would have known Idomenaeus,

When his king had long been forgotten?

Philosophers are always less powerful than kings,

But their spirit is stronger and breaks through the world.

Time will erase all, but some - instantly.

Others will live on for centuries.

If I am honest with you, Lucretius,

I will present your likeness to posterity today.

A single fate - a Nirvana similarity,

While he lives, his honor is complete.

The cost of great gifts only grows more expensive.

The more it grows, the farther it reaches.

Perhaps Idomenaeus will repay you,

For mentioning him in my letter:

"Do not add Pylades' money; enough is enough!

You have already exceeded..."

Not just money... also - pleasures,

And honors, and long years...

Desires, like mirages, lure us all to an unknown destination.

There are people who, reading Epicureanism,

Will seek to justify their vices.

In every place, I see charity.

A modest appetite is not enough;

Excess brings suffering.

Order must be established first,

And then it will be harder.

Be healthy.
