### Philosophical Context
This text is Seneca's response to a letter from Lucilius, in which he expresses his philosophy on virtue and happiness. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of seeking the brilliance and light in life, rather than merely striving for fame or material well-being.

### Essence
* The brilliance of life reflects both good and bad.
* Virtues include modesty, restraint, and caution.
* Desires can lead us to unknown places and result in vices.
* Good health is necessary for the pursuit of brilliance and light.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: The brilliance of life is a reflection of both good and bad, and achieving this requires modesty, restraint, and caution.

### Connection to Virtue
The desire to justify vices can lead to further excess. Therefore, modesty is a necessary condition for seeking brilliance and light in life.
