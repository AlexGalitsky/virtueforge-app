# Letter XXII (On Liberation from Tasks)

Dear Seneca,

How to escape from your troublesome tasks?

A doctor cannot prescribe medicine in the pharmacy,
only based on patient records.

Just like a gladiator, only on the arena,
he decides, having seen the enemy's movements.
He tightens his grip, as if strangling.

You can give general advice to friends or heirs,
but you cannot specify how to do this and that.

Don't say it until you have seen it with your own eyes,
and don't miss a fleeting opportunity to escape.

Even from the last drop of strength,
it is better to leave life than to live in misery.

Whatever you decide, put an end to your delusions:

Break free from the tangled web.

But even the most timid prefers to fall,
rather than being stuck on the endless rope.

If you are busy with something, limit yourself to it,
and don't think that you are a prisoner of tasks.

Stop, do not become carried away by a wandering wind,

Your fate will change if you stop now.

Don't delay, try to choose the right moment,
but if you see that your time has come and gone,

then don't hesitate – boldly take a step forward.

It's a simple choice: either now or never.

You can say: "What am I hearing? Are you a stoic,
throwing away burdens that are heavy on my chest?"

I will respond: "Not always is it necessary to work hard,
sometimes it's better to abandon this weight."

When tasks are uncertain and strange,
and you do them only for the sake of tasks,

there is no need for a burden.

It's better to be idle, even if it's late or early.

And when it comes to fruits, what about the emptiness in the pantry?
Or leaving the field before harvest time?

It looks like taking on a daring lover,
either by throwing away something precious and dear,
or by holding onto it too tightly.

People cling to slavery rather than freedom,
because that's all they know.

I call upon the stoic brotherhood,

to free you from your usual trap.

God sends us abundant hardships,
according to our request – trials, a fiery furnace.

Whoever bears the weight – it's heavy going into the water,
but whoever throws it away gets a breath of air again.

"Does everyone leave life as soon as they are born?"
No, only those who have isolated themselves from sin die well.

We were born to live without fear,
without desires and treachery.

A blessed person is one who dies with a peaceful face,
quietly leaving behind the body that ruled.

Like children, old people fear death,
not knowing life, they leave behind their evil deeds,

and as they depart, they cry out: "Save me! Believe in me!
I want to live!"

Those who are consumed by life's thirst
have not done any good in life.

Their fear is the fire that haunts them.

But if you add one more year to your life,
it's better to do something good now.

Be healthy.
