**Letter XXIII (On Joy)**

Lucilla greets Seneca!

What is the deceit of this present spring?

The Easter grass beneath the snow?

We must not speak of it.

I will tell you how to learn to think,

Not seeing joy in emptiness.

Who can calculate their own joy,

Will never be crushed by that.

Learn the art of joy. Losing

Unnecessary sweet pleasures,

You may find that joy, which lies far from all.

What delights the crowd - weak and wrong,

Like a grain of gold in the depths of a bucket.

Wealth of life is probably in the depth

And will reward the seeker fully.

Believe: true joy is rough,

Who holds pleasures captive,

Who does not suffer loss of blood,

Will have joy forever and everywhere.

The path to pleasures - on the edge of precipice,

Has fallen - and suffering surrounds...

To avoid it - there are measures, but not easy

To notice happiness in a well-planned design.

What is joy? - In clear conscience, charm,

Disdain for a luxurious collar.

We have joy - a smooth course of a peaceful life,

Gliding along a strict path.

The river of fate carries people, scaring

With its current and turbulent wave.

Few are those who, by themselves, prepare,

Always firm in their intention one.

Who strives to live each day with the first,

Only fears dying soon.

On the ship that has been pushed away from the dock,

Cannot look up at heaven's sky.

Almost no one of us knows the truth,

Having lived in a better world than this.

We end our lives, not starting life,

Not experiencing blessings.

Be healthy.
