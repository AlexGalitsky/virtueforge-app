### Philosophical Context
Letter XXIII is one of the most renowned and engaging works of Stoic philosophy, penned by Seneca to his student Lucilius. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of seeking joy in life rather than in possessions and external sources.

### Essence
- Stoicism underscores the significance of inner well-being and the pursuit of genuine joy.
- True joy is robust and enduring, not weak and ephemeral.
- A clear conscience and charisma are key factors in achieving joy.
- Life should be lived from the first day, not burned up at the end.

### Connection to Virtue
Joy is a crucial aspect of virtue, as it is linked to a clear conscience and charisma. Stoicism stresses that joy should be based on inner well-being rather than external sources.
