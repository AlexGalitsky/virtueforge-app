# The Letter XXIX (On Marcullion)

Lucilius salutes Seneca.

You ask: How is our Marcullion?

His visits have become very rare

A word of truth is a sharp edge.

What's the point in speaking to the deaf?

His vices? Be careful with your words

Speaking about nothing is pointless.

Your influence may undermine.

An arrow rarely misses its mark,

Unless, as you say, it's not called an archer.

The art of wisdom resides among the common people,

It never strays from its path.

I see no fault in Marcullion,

And I try to extend my hand to him,

Although his great success with vice

Makes it difficult for anyone to save him.

He can mock everyone on earth,

Including himself and us, philosophers of all schools.

He will pose a question and answer it himself,

Then find a way to wound each one.

He will bring shame to the reputation of Ariston,

Who could heal with words alone,

And remember Scaurus (laughter will reach the stone,

The peripatetic will mock from his roots "to wander")

He will ridicule all jesters-philosophers,

And stare at me in their eyes.

Let him tease me with his custom,

But I'll say, and tears will flow...

I'll prove to him that he values success

And thereby falls in price.

Perhaps my small help will last for a day,

But rest from vice is every day.

Until I'm busy healing him,

Be strong, don't fear fate's threat.

One life belongs to many generations,

And death is one question, like a moral test.

"I did not want to be liked by the crowd

The people do not love what I know,

And I don't know what they love." - Bravo!

I'm drunk with your idea, Epicyrus,

A foolish man knows how to win the hearts of the masses,

Using a shameful trick...

Here, as everywhere, nature's law reigns:

Low love leads to low paths.

People will love you if you acknowledge them,

But above all, be true to yourself!

Flattery is a vice that kills,

And chokes the virtue of noble fame with its dust.

Imagine: you're walking among crowds

Who sing and clap for you...

I'll feel sorry for him: his path is loved by many,

But he's corrupted himself.

Do you like yourself?

Be healthy.
