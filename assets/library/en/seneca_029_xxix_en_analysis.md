### Philosophical Context
This text is a letter from Epicurus to Seneca, in which he expresses his concern about the vice of Marcelinus and its impact on philosophy. Epicurus emphasizes the importance of healing and preserving virtue in the face of vice.

### Essence
- Marcelinus is an angry individual who mocks all philosophers.
- The vice of Marcelinus may undermine the influence of philosophy.
- Philosophy is the art of wisdom that does not err in anyone.
- Virtue perishes in flattery.
- The good of meager fame is but smoke.
