# The Letter XXX (On Death)

Lucilius salutes Seneca.

Under the weight of years, death beckons,
like ancient shipwrecks scattered by the wind.
But our Basse remains calm and serene,
full of life in a lifeless body,
unfazed by the prospect of mortality,
for one who stands firm in spirit advances with courage into the forest.

Any fate can offer hope,
but old age leaves none.
The hour arrives, and we depart together,
uniting in the path for the wise and the foolish alike.

While we live, we must act,
for all men are mortal, and fortune is not kind to any of us...

But death stands apart from evil (beyond its reach).
Death is above even fear of wrongdoing.
A story about death deserves faith,
from those who have died and been reborn.

The measure of mortality is known only to the dead,
the undertaker's cold cross.
For childhood, youth, and old age,
only death remains.

The path is irreversible, but our souls must endure exhaustion...

Death is inevitable, like the law of nature,
Is it fair to blame the law for what is?
Eras will pass, nations will change...
But only death can be changed.

As I approach old age with a slow pace,

I say thank you, God, for this life,
which has been flawed and imperfect.
I have drawn the line without wavering hand.

If you seek courage, who seeks death?
Who waits calmly for it?

The first ones are consumed by anxiety without food,
while the second prepare for flight...
I confess that Basse is dear to me,
a trustworthy guide and friend.
I am joyful, though we are over forty,

like a sprinter completing his final lap.

We must not confuse consequence with cause:

There is no need to hasten to leave life behind,
but let us accept death: it passes all things...

It pains me more to cherish life,
for when death draws near, we suffer.
Though death has been present throughout our lives,
it is only two steps away.

Would it be better to look at lists of diseases?
For in them we often find our enemy.

But you can hate me even more...

Because I have wasted precious time.
It is better to fear death than to anticipate it.

Do not forget about death.

Be healthy.
