# On Self-Trust

Dear Seneca,

I asked many fellow travelers:

How do you live and what are the fortunes of your companions?

In response, I am met with silence. Who is this Lucilius?

I rejoice! Keep to yourself, lest the passing crowd impede your journey.

Life is short, and people are not alike,
Each driven by their own motive.

Hurry, my dear friend, hurry faster
As if enemies were closing in on you.

Seize the moment – complete your path
By the time of your death, guard your time well.

Having completed your journey with great haste,
You may wait for the outcome until the last evening,
And enjoy a pure and unblemished bliss,
Knowing that all is left to you.

To belong to yourself is the ultimate goal.

May no footstep approach you,

Your parents have bequeathed to you much
That was taken from others.

To be free – leave your service behind,
Conquer the temptation of ambition.

Trust in yourself, for our friendship's sake,
Discard necessity.

Be healthy.

—
