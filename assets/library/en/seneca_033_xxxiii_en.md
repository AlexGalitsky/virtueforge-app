# The Letter XXXIII (On the Search for Truth)

Dear Seneca,

You love the sayings of wise men,
But wisdom is not a drop, but a river
That flows from the traditions of our fathers.

Do not be taken in by the slender trunk
Of a tree that offers shade,
For it is the grove where you find delight.

No one praises the slender leg of a beautiful maiden,
Whose beauty is embodied in her form.

The works of poets and historians are filled with great ideas.

My Epicurean friend is pleasant to me alone:
In the valley, the stones are visible from afar.

Do not ask for just my quotes,
Like weights on sale at a distant market.

Our sayings are filled with gold,
That you cannot consume by yourself.

Zeno, Cleanthes, Chrysippus, or Panetius
Are all wise men, truly!

Their thoughts are rare and precious:
"Only one poor man considers himself a sheep among sheep."

Do not try to cut beyond wisdom:
In the intertwining of each thread,
You will ruin the unity and find only your own soul.

Quotes are like colorful prisms:
They give us light, free from shackles.

Uninitiated people should prefer aphorisms
With elegance and poetic form.

Let children learn a little in school
What they can understand.

And those who have grown up, I will ask you: 
Do you still nod at someone else's opinion?

Stand up straight, cast off your crutches:
That is what he said, but it was just him saying...

Find out for yourself what he meant!
Look around you!

Who speaks only of others' words,
Is like a beggar who cannot see the light.

He may be a good scribe,
But never a philosopher or poet.

One who follows someone else's path
Thinks that the way is already laid out.
But following someone else's trail, he will find nothing but dust.

(Have you ever walked among mushrooms?)

I am not afraid to leave the main road,
I will see the path and pave it myself!

I respect my ancestors with strictness,
And I surrender to their doubts.

Go boldly in search of truth with a cat,
Break through the thicket, scatter the fog,
What you cannot find will be left for our descendants.

Truth is open to all.
Be healthy.
