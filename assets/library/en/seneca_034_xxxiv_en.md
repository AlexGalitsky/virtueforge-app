# Letters to Lucilius
## Letter XXXIV (On Success)

Dear Socrates,

The turmoil in my heart is still there, as it was in youth. You have surpassed yourself! Where are you? - In the chase.

The crowd has long since fallen behind.

Just like a shepherd delights in his flock when he sees a new lamb,
A gardener enjoys the first fruit of his labor,
A teacher finds joy in their pupil's progress,

When something solid emerges from clay.

I encouraged and spurred you on as we walked,
Teaching you to solve riddles,
And I believed that I would find the continuation.

You are mine! It was not for nothing that I wrote so many letters.

There is still half the journey... "What else is there?" - ask.

When you become brave and independent,

No one will be able to spoil it for you.

Do not give in to evil,
And do not make a small concession,

Although they tried to pull you in six different directions.

One word, one action,

Then you will be happy.

Be healthy.

—
