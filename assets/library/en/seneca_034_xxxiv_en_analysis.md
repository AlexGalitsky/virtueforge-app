### Philosophical Context
This text is a greeting from Lucilia to Seneca, expressing her joy and pride in his accomplishments. Written in verse, it is replete with metaphors and comparisons that underscore the importance of virtue and personal growth.

### Essence
* Seneca has achieved significant success, which fills Lucilia with joy.
* Lucilia greets Seneca as her teacher and guide, who helps her unravel mysteries and achieve fulfillment.
* Seneca must be bold and independent to avoid evil and not make even the slightest concession.
* Virtue and health are key aspects of happiness.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
Seneca's successes are tied to his virtue, as he attains fulfillment only when he is bold and independent. This emphasizes the importance of personal growth and development in achieving success.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us is willing to make even the slightest concession to avoid evil?
