# On the Joy of Being Together

Dear Seneca,

I need a friend, not just someone to stand by my side.

You love, but in love - there are rivers of sorrow,
Only in closeness - all the joys of love.

I imagine (I tremble and stumble,
Like a girl in dreams, in sleep),
How we will live together, soul to soul...

And strength returns to me.

It's pleasant to dream of love and seeing each other,
But happiness is scarce apart and far away.

Only now - living joy

Giving love - warmth in my hand.

Hurry to me, but only be true to yourself,

Believe in your desires, fleeting evenings:

Their changes mean only losses:

Blessed are the unshakeable.

Be healthy.
