### Philosophical Context
Letter XXXV from Seneca is addressed to Lucria, who expresses a desire to be with him and finds joy in proximity. This text reflects the philosophical idea that happiness and joy can be found not in dreams or distance, but in the present moment.

### Essence
* Love and joy are associated with closeness and presence.
* Dreams and visions cannot replace the pleasure of the present moment.
* Giving love means offering warm and steadfast support.
* Bliss lies in the certainty of one's convictions.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to the virtue of truth (veritas), as Seneca urges Lucria to be true to herself and her love. This requires one to be honest and sincere in their relationships.

### Question to Ponder
How can we distinguish genuine pleasure from dreams or distance?
