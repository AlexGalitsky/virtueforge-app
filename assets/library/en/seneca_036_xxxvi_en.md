# The Dangers of Ambition

Dear Seneca,

I approve of what my friend did:

He left a prominent position forever,
Preferring anonymity and leisure.

It is not in positions that we find our true good...

Whoever seeks happiness, wealth, or power
Lives like a warrior: three years for every year,
And never has the chance to enjoy himself fully.

Do not believe that he who is most needed is happy:
Those who drink from the cup of life are pouring mud.

Even if one is called idle, let him be blessed
Who walks alone on his path.

Let the young man seem gloomy to us,
Let some people seem empty...

The years will pass, and courage in our thoughts
Will show itself in words both wise and simple.

What seemed good at first may turn out bitter

Due to its subtlety. If it is cultivated with patience,

It becomes refined. The stubbornness and determination that are like medals

Are inseparable from each other.

He who sees his fate from birth will not be stopped
By the height of the wall.

It is time for him to learn!

"Is there no time in old age?" Know your honor:

Laughing at someone who tries, with a creak,
To climb down from his dignity.

You cannot take back what you have said.

Do not be ashamed to give credit where it is due,

But do not deceive a good hope.

The spirit above all worship and rewards,

Even if it is covered in gold dust.

But he will not lose the joy of life,
Even if everything falls apart, alas...

For in him, there is a love for himself
And an awe that has been with him since birth

A step towards death... He needs to believe in his awakening

After death. Nothing remains forever in nature,

What has died has entered the cycle:

Day, evening, night, and again it shines like...

Winter - after summer, and vice versa...

There is no fear of death in children or the mad,

Is not reason one of the gifts?

(Rhyme for Zoilus:) Be wise!

Do not be more foolish than an infant.

Be healthy.
