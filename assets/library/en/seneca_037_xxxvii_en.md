# How to Become Free?

Lucilius greets Seneca!

I have heard the majestic phrase:

You promised to be a good man,
And that is the guarantee of benevolence.

Like a gladiator who swears an oath:
"I give myself to be bound and killed."

But he has the right to appeal to mercy...

You took the path without the right to defend yourself,
And only your standing should die.

Since we were born, death is our reward,
Or perhaps by fate's decree... How can one look at it?

"How to become free?" - That which is inevitable,
Is to prepare to overcome in battle.

Then you will be free and untroubled,
Only "Strength lays down its own path."

You are able to rule only with reason,
And foolishness is in yielding to passions.

I ask: do not get caught up in a popular phrase,
And do not pay attention to fleeting news.

The desires of people (how can they see them?)
Carry the current, swept by the wind.

And what? "How did I end up here?" - I am ashamed...

For the foolish, everything is accidental.

Be healthy.
