Title: The Value of Instructional Letters

---
# The Value of Instructional Letters

Senecca is correct that the value of letters lies in their frequency,

In their relentless, laborious run,

As they approach the point of collision.

The depth of reasoning often results in a lot of noise,

But the essence of philosophy is counsel.

It's not advisable to offer advice publicly:

No one will hear a confidential response.

You can inspire people with your desire,

But learning requires quiet words.

For wise individuals, the substance of words is noble,

While loud shouting is characteristic of beasts.

Just as seeds planted in fertile soil

Grow...thorny rose bushes, cedar, and grapes.

So too, an instructional letter (do not dismiss it),

Will bring forth growth many times over.

And that soul, filled with happiness,

Will rejoice as it rises above the rooftops.

A little wisdom is said to be unspoken.

Be healthy.
