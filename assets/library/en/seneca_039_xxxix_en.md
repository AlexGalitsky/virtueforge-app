# The Letter XXXIX (On the Outcomes)

Dear Seneca,

You requested a concise summary and notes in order, like marking each stalk in a field.

Know this: the benefit of ordinary explications is no less than the outcome for an apprentice.

The outcome is useful to recall without strain all that I spoke today.

If you asked for it, I will give you both - but what are references for? I do not understand...

Let those who lead others to answer be guided by their own insight,

Only he who has never led anyone else can claim to have an answer.

I will write about everything you want, in my own way, as I understood it:

And all around there is a multitude of other prophecies,

Foretellings for each day and hour.

There are many predictions from philosophers in their sayings - wisdom's sonnets -

But where the soul did not reach, with you, dear friend, perhaps we will be together.

Oh, blessed spirit! You have an aspiration to surpass your humble station.

And by following the path of guesses and doubts,

You may see the fruits of great deeds.

Who in fortune's dealings does not complain?

Can overcome chance and misfortune,

Overcome failure and defeat,

And despise popular acclaim.

Like a flame that has consumed the underbrush,

It will no longer spread on the earth.

Our spirit, unceasingly troubled,

Will rise above the thrones of kings.

Greatness is only pretended by great men,

Moderation is beyond its limits:

What in excess revives life,

Is loss and corruption in excess.

Who in self-indulgence betrays his own honor

Can overcome stronger enemies, I can forgive them:

Their suffering - the horrors of malicious revenge -

No one could avenge them endlessly.

The brilliance of pleasure is but an illusion,

Let us recognize that it is false.

There is no truth without faith and moderation,

No remedy for those who are corrupted by vice.

We measure necessity in its utility,

Excess does not fit our nature...

They already do not disturb us, but rather

Command us.
