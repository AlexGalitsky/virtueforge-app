### Philosophical Context
This text is part of Seneca's "Notes on Stoic Ethics," where he summarizes his philosophical thoughts and offers guidance on living a virtuous life.

### Essence
* Seneca emphasizes the importance of self-awareness, the pursuit of goodness, and balance in life.
* He asserts that one must learn to manage both fortune and misfortune, and avoid succumbing to pleasures and vices.
* Seneca also underscores the importance of faith and moderation in life.

### Form of This Version
Prose

### Connection to Virtue
Seneca ties his thoughts on balance and self-management to the concept of virtue, advocating that one should strive for goodness and balance in their life.

### Question to Ponder
Who can truly master themselves?
