# The Letter XLI (On the Divine)

Lucillia salutes Seneca!

You write: Cultivate your spirit...

Well said, there is no need to seek external guidance.

For on high, God listens to our prayers,

Why ask for what we can find within ourselves?

For God is with us, in us, and in our hearts.

We do not need to search for Him outside;

Look for Him within yourself.

And as you approach Him alone,

You see the darkness and light around you...

Without God, who can lift themselves above fortune?

Only He can give an answer to all things.

When walking through a dense and quiet forest,

Where the sunlight is hidden by thick foliage,

It inspires us (it's hard to explain here)...

What is in the forest is the manifestation of God's presence.

The sources of rivers, deep caves,

Dark and opaque lakes... 

This awakens the sanctity of our faith,

With a sense of the sacred that will burst forth.

If you see someone

Fearless, calm, like God,

Looking at all with no fear of the ages...

Can you not understand why you cannot grasp it?

What you see is not the threshold of the grave,

But the height of power has been gained!

What divine force has descended upon humanity,

Upon its fragile body and face...

A high soul is untouched by emotions,

Its desires and fears are insignificant.

And in this, there is no doubt about the manifestations

Of God's beginnings in their words.

Like a sunbeam coming to earth,

Their spirit lives on through eternity,

Just like the source of the river.

It does not depart from its origin.

Our soul is not alien to it, but connected...

To the divine - the foundation of many faiths.

And lifts us out of the dirt and filth of our flesh,

And gives an example to many in life.

The soul within itself keeps good from eternity,

It does not boast outwardly or superficially.

What is more foolish than human glory?

Than what can be shared with it?

In the arena, tamer of exhausted lions,

With their shining beauty...

But the wild lion far surpasses

The relaxed one with his golden mane.

One has a beautiful home and family,

He receives many gifts,

He is abundantly fruitful (and often, where he does not sow),

But himself, he is worth nothing.

What is man? - The soul. And in it? - Only reason.

And the highest good for him is to fulfill

What no one can do immediately,

But when worthy - to bring a conclusion.

A crowd, caught up in general joy,

Carries them there where all are captivated by sin.

Live with nature - and you will avoid the hangover.

You are a rational man.

Be healthy.
