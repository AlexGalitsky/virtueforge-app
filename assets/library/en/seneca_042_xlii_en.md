# The Virtues of Humility and the Corruptions of Power

Dear Seneca,

You believe that someone who has achieved greatness is like a child of virtue? Such individuals are rare, even in every age.

It's almost as if you're an exception. Lord.

If only he knew what it meant to be virtuous, he wouldn't have tried to emulate it.

What's wrong with his lamentations about evil? Even Judas regretted betraying Christ.

Does he not despise the unbridled power of those who wield it? He would have done the same if he could.

The flaws of many are hidden because of their weakness. They cannot show the depth of their depravity, which they've been harboring for a long time.

Just like winter, snakes do not fear proximity when they're frozen solid, like a log.

You claimed that someone was in your power.

I said: it's in your hands with the pen!

He disappeared? He was just a shade of gray, responding to evil with hatred.

Those who threaten will issue threats. Those who pursue will bear the burden.

A poet will grasp the truth of life through his inner voice when it pierces through the stone.

We can see our own foolishness in that we value things based on their monetary worth.

Giving up one's home for a long debt is not pleasing to anyone.

It's more convenient to value ourselves than our possessions.

There are values that are more treacherous than any evil blade.

The ants, crawling over someone's lips:

When they didn't belong to us, we would have belonged to them.

We often think about paying back debts and cry like tears of a capful.

Some people get tired of losing things. But if not, they haven't had time to adapt yet.

There is no money in the madness of craving wealth.

We're all ready to defend our homes. But we cannot save ourselves.

Don't think about losses.

Be healthy.
