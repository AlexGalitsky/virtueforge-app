# On the Obvious and the Hidden

Dear Seneca,

You ask: How did I come to know all this?

The rumor knows everything, even from its hiding place,
Through the narrowest of channels.

In the province you are a notable figure,
Though in Rome you are just a distant relative of the hills.

Your descendants are heroes of literature,
And a theme for contemplation among minds.

Why do we build walls? It seems clear enough

To live within them in the peace of the soul?

Alas... We seek not safety in them,

But only a means to be less noticeable in our wrongdoing.

Whoever is fortunate can live well without doors and locks,

So that the one who comes may not catch us off guard,

Though they are neither invited nor known.

He who is honest can live in an open cage.

He who speaks truthfully, we take him seriously.

Do not forget, your conscience is your witness,

And you must consider it.

Be well.
