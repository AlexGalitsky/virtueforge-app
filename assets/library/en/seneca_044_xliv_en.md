**Letter XLIV (On Origin)**

Lucilla greets Seneca!

Are you, a Roman equestrian, poor in origin?

All Romans and Greeks have one common ancestor - God.

You, as a Roman horseman, are entitled to your legs tracing the path of the orchestra,

But there are others, many of them, whose first rows are closed.

Only noble spirit is openly revealed:

Cleantheus, the gardener, poured water with reverence,

Socrates was not renowned from birth,

And Plato acquired strength slowly.

Plato said: "All kings are from slavery,

And no slaves have ancestors without a king."

Neither a king nor a slave who does not recognize brotherhood,

Cannot stand firm at the altar.

What is nobility? In ancient portraits that we pass by barely breathing?

Trust me, my friend, the secret lies not in this:

It is our soul that creates nobility.

Imagine yourself not as a Roman equestrian

There is a path from foot soldiers to a strong farmer

(Crowd does not matter - it's just envy of the gardener):

Only noble path can lead to evil.

Do not get entangled - there is a goal and means in life,

Blessedness is the goal. Let the means be good,

But not always do they align with the neighbor's goal,

And means can overwhelm goals.

Life is a labyrinth...Who rushes through it plods like foolish schoolboys.

Who sees the goal does not dream of emptiness.

Do not mix up goal and means.

Be healthy.
