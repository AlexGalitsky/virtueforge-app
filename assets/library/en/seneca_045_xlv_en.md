# On Truth and Its Similarity

Lucilla greets Seneca!

You lament that there are not enough books...

Why wander through a vast library,

When you have not chosen a book and not delved into it?

You ask for books, but not my counsel?

I am willing to give up everything from my storehouse,

And come myself, as a reward

For recognizing your growth and spirit, and intellect.

I will not consider myself eloquent,

Unless you asked me for my counsel,

How could I be considered beautiful,

Because of that?

If you read my work...let it seem blunt,

There is no truth in it, worth anything...

I am searching for it stubbornly,

Washing away falsehoods year by year.

I believe in the great minds' reasoning,

But know my own rights:

Revealing great discoveries,

But how many have they been able to name?

They got lost in verbal subtleties,

Seeking a second and third meaning in words.

How can one live? How can one die? They said...

I will direct your mind towards this!

What is the similarity between words?

The difference becomes meaningless in synonyms

Wishes are at odds with wishes...

And plans flee from plans...

Just as flattery resembles friendship,

When you find the true path in the depths of your heart.

It captivates our ear, an unarmed one,

And invites our soul to rest.

Here, boldness roams under the guise of happiness,

Moderation stands like a sign of laziness,

Under the mask of caution - cowardice,

And pretends to be your enemy.

Let the most cunning in logical arguments

Prove that you have horns!

Who is so foolish as to seek healing?

From them? - It's a costly lesson...

Those who are skilled in verbal traps,

Are only enjoying themselves,

While the fool cannot even grasp it.

Blessed is he who has discovered gold from eternity,

But blessed is he who has found it within himself.

He who sees humanity,

Knows that anyone is unique.

Who is bold in his judgments and fearless in his actions,

Is struck by fortune, but he is happy...

Like the unassailable walls that rain down hail

Saying: "I lie... Is he telling the truth? Or speaking the truth?"

A meaningless question when everything in life is a lie...

Do not deceive yourself - and you will be justified,

Live your life - and find happiness in it.

What is good - that is necessary for all,

Don't search for it in a broth

Find what is unnecessary, and you'll find meaning.

The fool who cannot pass the threshold of understanding

Finds life's sense in futility.

Check anyone: They think about "tomorrow",

Putting things off until later...

But life rushes by faster than breakfast can be ready,

And the day flies away like a domino.

I will finish, so that your hand holds

My letter... and it has many strophes.

Has my letter illuminated your doubts?

Live, don't plan.

Be healthy.
