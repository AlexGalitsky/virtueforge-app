# Letter XLVI (On the Acquired Book)

Dear Seneca,

I have received the promised book, and I wanted to read it later without any distractions. Last night, I finally opened it and read it from cover to cover.

The book seemed so short to me that it left Epictetus behind. I was amazed by the smoothness of its flight, as if the soul had chosen a courageous path. Perhaps I read it superficially, like food - more with my ears than my mind.

You have chosen a fruitful subject in this book, not one that is cramped or bestowed upon you by chance.

I will read it again and again, and I promise not to lie about it:

There is no point lying because of seven winds.

I will write about the book again soon.

We lie only out of habit.

Be healthy.

—
