# The Letter to the Slaves

Lucilla greets Seneca!

We are all on this earth together,
Sharing the same fate.

Are they slaves? - Fellow travelers in bondage...
Are they slaves? - Neighbors in the palace...
Are they slaves? - Brothers in humility...

They are all human beings, by nature...

I am amused by my own habit:
Having a crowd of slaves around me,
There is much more than enough to go around,
And trying to be thin and toothless...

A slave dare not speak even a word,
Or cough accidentally, or sneeze...
Any noise is punished severely.

They stand all night, without daring to rest...

For that - the masters are useless

They are miserable in their presence...

But the one who is treated well by them
Will not be intimidated by the sword of justice.

"Is my slave a foe?" - We make enemies out of them...

We treat slaves harshly:
We punish - they polish our feet...
And we, they serve us like dogs...

With this needle, sharpened as a thorn,
They cook the finest birds...

Who taught him to be so cruel?
He is more pitiful than anyone else...

This censor, dressed as a woman,
Shaves off every hair...

This censor of guests: flattery is a crime,
Who will invite you tomorrow?

And the one who feeds us - is he not our master?

Who knows how to satisfy his appetite?
Is it not we who are ignorant?

The path of fortune is treacherous and steep,
And its verdict is often severe.

That man, who was once sold by Callicles,
Was not allowed to enter the palace...

One seed is in us, and above us - heaven...
We all live and die like you.

Even those who seem high and mighty...
Will one day be punished by fortune.

I will explain this with a simple example:

"Be humble towards the lower classes,
In proportion to what you want from the upper classes."

"But I am not owned by anyone!"...

While you are young, there will be someone...
When we see our own wrinkles in the mirror,
There is also an image of the master.

Sisyphus, Cressus, Priam's wife, and Hecuba...
Were all punished for their lives.

Plato was sold into slavery - a gift from tyranny...

To share a meal with slaves is proper behavior,
Let the gourmands shake their heads in disgust...

I easily catch them off guard,
While caressing the hands of strangers' slaves

Now it's hard to believe (but don't laugh...
The wisdom of our ancestors: heal yourself with spirit.

The masters used to call themselves "the head of the family",
But slaves were called "household servants".

"What - all the slaves at the table?" - No, those who are better!

It is not the occupations that matter,
But the character...

A fool who does not understand...
Evaluates a horse by its bridle.

Even more foolish is he who tries to find friends
Wherever he goes.

He is a slave! But maybe he is free in spirit?

He is a slave! But show me someone who is not a slave

in terms of greed, in terms of lust...

All are slaves...
And the master is common - fear.

We will trust all slaves with our confidence,
For small mistakes, we will forgive them.

Be yourself - High without high-mindedness,
So that you may not be feared by them, but respected.

"A slave is like a client" - Dreams...

We have been blessed from birth to death
With only love and reverence for God...

Love does not coexist with fear.

Punishing slaves with words - it's their right...
Beating them - that's for the animals.

Punishment is rewarded - better behavior,
But indulgence is punished - silence.

We are acquiring royal habits:

A slight thing, and our anger has no bounds...

The king knows all this, but "in a whisper"...

And the search for evil continues to pursue him.

A good character is happy with itself,
Evil is more changeable than the wind...

What clouds of people follow it...
But does not follow them.

Be healthy.
