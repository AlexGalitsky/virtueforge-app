# The Letter XLVIII (On Sophisms)

Lucillia greets Seneca!

You sent me a long letter,

So long that it's hard to put down between lines and take a seat...

I'd like to think about it and respond later.

Resolving the question is harder than asking:

What one thing can be compared to death,
Another to divine favor?

When two are friends, their thoughts are pure:

Good and evil are intertwined in fate.

Egoists cannot be happy:
They will reap what they sow in return.

This law has been written for centuries,

And binds us stronger than any thread.

Whoever shares with everyone can share with anyone.

Sophists, with their cunning games,

Twist the meaning of words "human":

"Can you share something with someone?"

"What can I give to a human forever?"

"Is every human my friend?" Another will say:

"Important is not what's human, but my loyal friend..."

The first friendship brings bread at dawn,
The second destroys everything around...

I don't understand sophisms,

But I know the direction and goal!

It's worth dissecting them,

Because they're not worth exploring.

"There's a mouse - a word, and we catch it...
Does the mouse get eaten by the word?"

Here's foolishness, but what's the harm?

Have you seen many traps for words in your time?

You haven't seen any harm from words,
So why waste our lives on them?

Philosophers are silly...

Why do we bother with them?

It's not time to play around,
Help is needed:

When someone is already close to death,

Only a fool makes jokes.

You're joking, but it's scary for the sick,
Listen to me!

You're joking and not even hearing:
Come on, help!

Stretching out my hands towards your tower,

Everyone believes that enemies will be defeated.

Take it seriously and let truth guide you:

Through narrow gates of salvation.

Evil will seep in somewhere.

Share what's necessary with what's unnecessary,
Tell me how easy it is to follow the righteous law,

How delicious it is to taste the sweetness of viishni...

Bread of truth - a bite for those who are fighting.

Sophisms are useless and corrupt:

There's no strong weapon in them...

You've seen people trying to hide their fate with a weak reed?

They're just making empty promises,

Whoever believes them will be in trouble.

Like a helper on the court,
Wisdom restores our rights.

Why learn grammar from the beginning?

Why did I join philosophy?
It promised me equality with God!

So, where is that promised wellspring?!

All good things are clear and obvious:

How can we not see it?

Don't waste our lives on nothing,

Throw away your sophisms!

Be healthy.
