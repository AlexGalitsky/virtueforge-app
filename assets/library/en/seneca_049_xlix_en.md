# The Art of Dialectics

Dear Seneca,

As I sit on my old couch, reminiscing about my friends, I am reminded that indifference is a vice. Campaigns, Naples, and Pompeii have brought back the sharpness of my sorrow.

It's been ages since then...and I remembered you when I stumbled upon it again, like finding myself in a treacherous river.

Wasn't everything just too long ago? Recently, it was childhood...and Sotion was by my side.

Here are the trials, here is the loss of enthusiasm,

Here are the forces...the swiftness of time...

How can one divide life into long periods?

What's the point of a lifetime, written on a small piece of paper?

It's childhood, youth, and an imperceptible transition...

And old age is being silvered on the edge.

Time's swift passage, once unnoticed,

Becomes clearer with each passing year.

A line appears, like a dark cloud,

Counting losses becomes easier when it's closer.

As Cicero said, "There's no point in wasting days on lyric poets."

But, alas, lyric poets are powerless,

While dialecticians are complex.

What's the point of struggling with foolish questions?

The wise understand the meaningless circle.

Of course, digging into trivialities is pointless...

For those whose life is a never-ending leisure,

There's no time for the struggles of life

Counting on chance to make sense,

When death and defeat loom near.

I've been labeled as mad by some,

Standing at the edge of victory and defeat,

In the midst of bloody, thunderous battles,

With intricate schemes.

I won't waste my effort on these things,

The final battle stands before me!

Death is life...and their conflict is more bitter than any enemy behind a wall.

Oh, teach me how not to flee from death!

Oh, teach me how to preserve life!

When fate inevitably draws its line...

The last circle of silence and meeting!

Tell me before sleep: you won't wake up.

Tell me the next morning: you won't lie down.

As I leave the house: you won't return.

At the entrance: you won't go out for a walk.

Clear my mind, and I'll grasp the truth immediately,

For nature has given me reason...

Not the best, but capable of understanding everything.

"Truth is simple." In it lies justice.

Moderation does not know cunning.

Guard natural modesty,

And reach your goal.

Be healthy.
