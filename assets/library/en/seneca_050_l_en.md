# The Letter L (On Recognizing Faults)

Dear Seneca,

Five months have passed since I received your letter...

I am reading between the lines of yours:

You understand the foolishness of delusions,
You do not believe in "the circumstances of sins".

The place does not determine the temptations,
The faults, the hypocrisy.

We carry them with us wherever we go.

Grapes (do you remember - my wife's husband?),

Are blind... but she herself is unaware,

She thinks that she needs beacons...

We laugh... laughing at ourselves...

Who will say that he is greedy or stingy?

He will say that he sought support for himself...

Should I believe him, who has always been foolish?

Why should we deceive ourselves uselessly,

For the troubles do not come to us from outside:

The trouble sits inside... it is clear to whom

It is hard to treat it a hundred times.

We meet... indeed, monsters,

Not waiting for old age to see doctors.

Those who abandon their nature...

Cannot be healed by anyone's advice.

We are ashamed of the gift of good thinking,

It comes to us occasionally, unexpectedly.

Who will apply his hands to his soul when young?

He will not become corrupt, even in old age.

Hard work always benefits,
The straight branches bend with warmth,
And the spirit is as light as air...

There is no place for despair:

Let your door be long since taken by you,

Your soul, as soft as dough,

Be healed yourself.

Be healthy.

—
