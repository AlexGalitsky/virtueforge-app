# Letters from Lucilla
## Letter LV (On the Separation from Friends)

Dear Seneca,

A walk on stilts is also a chore,
You know, I was never one to be pampered.
Those who claim otherwise are lying.

The stilts are merely a consequence of my illness,
A sign of something unnatural.

Walking is more pleasant and beneficial than...

Anyone who is not sick may not even know...
My gaze fell upon Vatia's estate,
Which no one guards at the time.

There, in a moment of luxury,
They would exclaim: "You, Vatia, are the only one who knows how to live!"

But he didn't live; he hid from life,
When Segan and Gall expelled him...

One became hated through his wife,
The other was executed for his lover.

Is it living idly or being free from duty?
The distinction is different, even in appearance...

It's just that money is what we're accustomed to,
While a carefree existence is granted to the wise.

Others run away from failure with cowardice,
Envious of others and trembling like a deer,
Their desires are limited to food and sex...
And their future is marked by a heavy hand.

The estate is pleasant: the house shines brightly,
In the heart of a palm tree, a stream flows,
In caves, it's quiet, even when the wind blows...

The wall is a barrier for our eyes.

It's not beauty that adorns us from the outside...
But in our hearts, all colors are present.

Even in the most beautiful homes, there is sorrow.

But in your thoughts, you're capable of meeting me,
Like it happens in a fairy tale.

There's no place more beautiful than the reunion
In unity of soul, stronger than the journey itself...

Life may be dull sometimes, but happiness shines brighter from afar.

In separation, we find that our friends are closer to us:
A friend has an endless list of tasks...
Their other half completes this list,
Concluding their isolation.

A true friend must be in our hearts and therefore nearby!

Yes, closer than the dividing line that separates us...

Come walk with me, eat what you need...

I see you, I hear you.

Be healthy.

---
