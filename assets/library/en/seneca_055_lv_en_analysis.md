### Philosophical Context
In this letter, Lucretia expresses her separation from friends and underscores the importance of virtue in life. Seneca, as a philosopher, would understand this sentiment, as he also prioritized wisdom and well-being over material wealth.

### Essence
* Virtue is a key concept in Seneca's philosophy.
* Separation from friends is not separation from oneself, meaning we can maintain connections with others even when they are not physically present.
* A life devoid of material desires and concerns can be wiser and happier.
* Friendship and unity are stronger than material benefits.
