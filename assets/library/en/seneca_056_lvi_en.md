# The Noise
---
Lucilius greets Seneca!

I live above the bathhouse now,
Like in a crowded, noisy boat...

Imagine all the cries present:

Here are the strong men groaning with effort,

Guns loaded with lead...

Perhaps the cries are just for show,

To make people look at their faces.

A drunkard, lost in pleasure,

The belly is groaning with excess...

And even the pleasure... I hear

Even slaps and punches.

And games of soccer? As soon as they gather,

They throw it, and let's count the throws...

Until they can't anymore,

You'll go crazy from the noise and sadness...

Plus arguments, plus thefts,

Jumping into the pool, and shouting "just like that"...

But you know who my greatest enemy is?

Who pulls out his hair as a barber...

Practically screaming at clients,

And falls silent only when they're

They shout (no pause, no moment),

Like they're being put on a rat.

Merchants of sausages and pastries

Shout out their products... each one...

You'll say: I'm iron, I'm like stone,

Unless someone hits me hard...

I swear, this noise is not a distraction,

It's like a river that can flow for centuries,

Even heard about the tribe that (without laughter)

Everyone threw away, leaving the cliff.

But it's the voice, not the noise that gets into my soul,

And the noise only hits my ears.

I find it easier to listen to the constant noise,

Than the rhythm of the rowers who are straining hard.

Let the noise outside be loud and groan,

While I remain calm and unshaken.

But even on a dark, quiet night...

Desire and fear are whispering...

For the night doesn't eliminate all worries,

And in dreams they come to us...

I've met many people whose nights were more troubled than days.

One person lies alone in a spacious house,

Everything is closed. What else do you need?

But sleep refuses to come, not even a dream,

When his heart is troubled by the noise.

A drunkard, even in peace... has no peace...

He needs to wake up and get back to work,

And engage in some art... what is it?

The God of labor sent rest.

Soldiers calm down during battles,

Who are busy - can't be bothered with idle chatter.

Sometimes we escape from work into the shadows,

Not listening to the gossip's murmur...

But sometimes, even then, ambition grows,

Wasting away from failure.

(Who doesn't love themselves?)

Sorrowing that their love is limited...

Let the vices unfold their mouths

Publicly - I remain calm and composed.

The dangers of the spirit are more treacherous than lies hidden under a blanket.

"Who wasn't afraid of arrows, enemies, and swords,

Now fears just the noise of the wind,

Afraid of his own fate, thrown by open arrows,

His hand is clenched in a tight fist in sleep."

Vergilius.

Look at those happy people,
With enormous wealth on themselves:

You see their frightened faces,

What they're waiting for in the future.

"Is it better to live far from the noise?"

You're right, why should I listen to the noise of the courtyard?

Ulysses handled sirens quite cleverly.

I was only training.

Be healthy.
