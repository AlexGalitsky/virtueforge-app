Title: The Tunnel
---
# The Tunnel

Lucillia greets Seneca!

I have borne the weight of all that is bearable for an athlete:

First, I traversed the river's mire,
Then, the tunnel's dust, like a tomb.

There is nothing longer than that prison
Among the mountains (it will collapse - and shatter!).

The dust accumulates on the road and walls,
And the torches are darker than the deepest darkness.

I was crushed by the tunnel's nastiness...

You know: I am not very patient,
Far from perfection, and utterly worn out,

But here, any soul will be pierced.

But when I saw a glimmer of light,

My soul revived itself.

I began to reason: how much worse
Is this tunnel than our old homes?

There is no difference between what falls on our shoulders

And the lives of people whose existence lasts only for an instant.

How blind our fear is: it sees only the instruments

Of murder, even when its outcome is uncertain.

You think I, like any stoic,

Do not believe in the preservation of the soul,
Crushed by the weight?

It's not worth rushing...

Like a flame that cannot be extinguished,
The air does not pierce the sword,

And for the soul, there are no collapses - it's an exam,

It will find its refuge.

A soul is immortal or, like a candle,

Crushed by the breath of winds?

What is not subject to death is eternal,

The soul does not die.

Be healthy.
