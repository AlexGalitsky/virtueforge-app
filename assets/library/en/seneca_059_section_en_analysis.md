### Philosophical Context
This text is a brief and philosophical letter emphasizing the importance of finding balance in life. It suggests that, like a letter, life should be concluded with wisdom and health.

### Essence
- The author underscores the importance of achieving balance in life.
- He encourages readers to strive for wisdom and health.
- The text does not contain direct moral or ethical directives but implies that judgments should be based on personal experience and values.
- The author also highlights the importance of simplicity and straightforward expression of thoughts.

### Form of This Version
Prose.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to virtue, as it stresses the importance of being wise and healthy. This aligns with Franklin Weeks' principle of Order, which emphasizes the importance of physical activity and a balanced lifestyle in achieving moral and ethical goals.
