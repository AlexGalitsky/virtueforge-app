# Letter LX (On Insatiability)

Dear Seneca,

I am complaining, quarreling, and scolding.

My family sends their greetings with a warning:

Beware of greater dangers than enemies without number.

Evil is ingrained in us from birth,

May we pray for forgiveness into the loving inheritance,

It's time to forget the curses of our heritage,

We've had enough of our own vices already.

Can we still ask God for something that is given to us by nature?

In one feast, so much is eaten,

That it cannot fit on a single ship.

Is our gluttony greater than that of all other devouring beasts?

And are we not eating ourselves on land,

While ships from all seas bring... 

Not hunger is expensive,

Gluttony is the marble limit,

Where you can write: "He died young", 

Placing the date of death...

Be healthy.

—
