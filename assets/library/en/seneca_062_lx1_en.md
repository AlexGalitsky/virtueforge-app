# On the Willingness to Death

Dear Seneca,

I try to deny the past evil:

All my former childish transgressions are flaws

I have bound myself with a tight sea rope.

I write, I eat, I read

Everything I do now is done for the last time.

Though days pass without notice or count,

But every moment brings me joy.

I lived well, knew rest and work,

Now I only want:

To die gladly, to not be forced to do anything!

Not being a wretched slave because of obligation

Misfortune is in the face's resistance.

Who is bound by duty beforehand,

Remembers no pain.

We are not measured by years but by soul's satisfaction

From life's gains and gifts.

Then it doesn't matter: we have died, been killed...

I lived - that's enough.

Be healthy.

—
