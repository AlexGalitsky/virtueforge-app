# Letter LXIII (On the Death of Close Friends)

Dear Seneca,

I share your grief over Flaccus's passing.

Do not grieve excessively for a colleague, though I wish you to mourn his loss as well.

I do not ask that you forget your sorrow, but rather that it be a common experience among us.

It is pointless to argue about shedding tears. 

But I do not want you to remain in mourning forever.

Homer wrote: "Today we cry for the dead..."

No one deserved death more than any of us.

Tears are matched by the vainglory of those who display them publicly.

"Will I forget my friend?" 

If memory is fleeting, like sorrow...

Sorrow will pass, and in a rare moment of leisure...

You may encounter it again.

Strive to find joy, not suffering.

Sorrow was a presence in your heart, like a window.

"Remembering the deceased friend..."

Brings us pleasure, like a draught of wine.

Atal

......If you truly believe this,

The thought of living friends is sweet as honey.

But the taste of loss is bitter...

And precious to us here.

I remember them and do not weep:

Their memory is a pleasant wave...

For I knew that I would lose them in life,

And losing them, I know they were with me.

How often do we see each other in life?

How rarely do we meet our friends?

Although it seemed like the only sound was the rustling of leaves...

We waste our social gatherings on frivolity.

Look at those who cry more than anyone else...

They have not been able to express their love in life.

It is they who suffer, not just appear to be suffering.

A friend will replace another who has passed away,

If he loves and is loved by you.

But if you do not value your friends,

You will find yourself accusing yourself of sorrow.

Who could have loved only one out of many...

Was not truly in love with them.

Seek out friends, move your legs in different directions,

And hide from the fact that you forgot their friendship.

It is known that years are stronger than sorrows.

But time is a poor remedy for sorrows:

We must tear sorrow out of its roots!

Do it now, do not be afraid.

For women, there is a year of mourning...

And for men, any period of grief is suitable.

You saw an old woman who, after parting with her body,

Cried in her shawl for a month!

Recently, she has found solace...

And attracts those who are suffering to herself.

Through tears, a firm decision is taking shape:

It is not right to mourn one's own fate.

I cried over Serenus too...

One and only excuse I will find:

He was younger than me... 

But the years will not build up a chain of death.

There is no law for death,

Only a place of meeting - this common blood.

The dead are not worms of death, but rather...

They have moved ahead, and only...

We remain behind.

Be healthy.
