# Letter LXIV (On the Development of Wisdom)

Dear Seneca,

You were with us yesterday,
(With me, always, like in a boat.)

We feasted with friends from morning till night,
 Discussed many things with them,
Our thoughts ran wild in conversation...

Then we read a book (they gave it to me as a gift)
(The Book of Quintus Sextius the Elder).

(In books, some people are wise and argue,
But their wisdom is bloodless - just a faint sparkle of names.)

He lives, great, free. All you need to do
Is read him, and I am inspired by faith.

I'm ready to face Fortune herself,
To wrestle with fate and the bull,
No, I won't sit idly by... 

I've never seen such vivid feelings in anyone.
The grandeur of wise life! It's irresistible
For Sextius fills my soul so easily...

You understand: this is achievable,
Although it's very high.

I don't waste time on leisure when it's my turn,
I contemplate wisdom every moment,
It's endless to me, like the universe itself,
And I'm happy that it's with me now.

I bow down before its creation,
I thank all its creators,
Who have passed it down through generations,
And gave their hearts to it.

And those who are troubled by doubts in their heart,
Even after a thousand years,
Can achieve this wisdom,
Unlike the foolish and vain.

Imagine: wisdom was discovered in ancient times,
But applied - it's new in its time.

Evil will never be outdated,
Praise for him who can heal evil.

A philosopher deserves respect:
He is everyone's teacher, twice as wise,
It's not about being an Epicurean or a Stoic,
All his followers are equal in wisdom.

I'll mark their birthdays,
And remember their names,
To remind people of the good that comes from their mistakes,
So their souls can still feel it.

Some deserve honor for their position,
Others - for the gift of wisdom,
And choosing them after death is not difficult:
Only wisdom elevates.

Be healthy.
