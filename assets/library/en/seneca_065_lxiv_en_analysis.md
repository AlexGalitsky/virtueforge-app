### Philosophical Context
Letter LXIV from Seneca to Lucilius is a philosophical letter in which Seneca shares his thoughts on the development of wisdom and the importance of philosophy in life. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of seeking wisdom and applying it to everyday life.

### Essence
- Wisdom is an all-encompassing principle that can help people justify their actions and make reasoned decisions.
- Philosophy serves as the foundation for the development of wisdom, providing people with knowledge about the nature of reality and human existence.
- Wisdom is not confined to ancient philosophers alone but can also be discovered and applied in contemporary life.
- Philosophers who engage in the pursuit of wisdom act as mentors and inspirers for others.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Wisdom is an all-encompassing principle that can help people justify their actions and make reasoned decisions, and philosophy is the foundation for its development.

### Connection to Virtue
Wisdom is linked to virtue, as it provides people with the knowledge of how to act properly and make reasoned decisions, enabling them to exhibit virtues such as justice, honesty, and tolerance.
