### Philosophical Context
Letter LXVII is part of Seneca's philosophical work on courage and virtue, written in verse. This personal letter to Lucilius emphasizes the importance of courage and virtue in life.

### Essence
* Courage is not merely a word but an action.
* Actions taken with spirit form the foundation of courage.
* Courage demands patience, tolerance, and reason.
* Virtue can be stern, but it is a necessary condition for achieving courage.

### Form of This Version
Letter LXVII is written in verse.

### Connection to Virtue
Courage and virtue are interconnected, as courage requires a person to be virtuous. Virtue is the foundation of courage, and courage is the foundation of virtue.
