# The Letter LXVIII (On Idleness)

Dear Seneca,

I concur with your decision:

Leave your duties and quietly close your eyes.

Conceal your idleness - what is there to boast about?

For the wise, the world is open to service,
Their abode lies above the senate.

Neither divine nor human are foreign to them,
Philosophers speak of eternity.

Do not extol joyful leisure,
Say instead: "Philosophy" is "sickness".

Do not surround yourself with a secret circle,
Let people think that you are overcome by laziness.

We are not disturbed at all by your presence,
Only those who try to break through the closed door.

By hiding, you multiply your persecutions,
And invite many losses.

Leave your duties and converse with yourself... 

with anger, just as people do about you.

Engage in a struggle against your inner weakness,
And succeed in this unyielding battle.

Everyone knows that bodily weaknesses are flaws,
One's stomach is cleansed by vomiting,
People who suffer from foot pains do not drink wine,
Even though they thirst,

They use diets to get rid of cellulite.

If I had sick veins, I would have sent for you as my servant...

But the sickness that I am treating myself for is hidden:

In my heart, there is a wound and an ulcer.

Do not long for earthly glory:
"Ah, what a great man! He has conquered everything and condemned it all!"

I have only condemned myself... and the sick person,
While I found the doctor in myself...

I want you to think this as you are leaving:

"I am mistaken, taking someone else for the teacher of life...
And now I see that only laziness is there."

Accept my saying: "Idleness

is more beautiful than all the things left behind.

The worldly power - for many is a hard task,
Detestable and hated by everyone.

Perhaps they will surpass me with wealth, fame, or praise,
But their fleeting glory will soon be nothing but dust...

And I want to conquer fortune!

Ah, if only from birth one could measure one's blessings,

We would not have to worry about old age...

Even if we do not believe in experience,
It is time for us to understand it with our reason.

My age has its advantages, as all my sins
Have been tempered by time and are no longer a problem.

Who started late will still make it on time,
With the help of a blessed horse.

Perhaps young people think they are wiser than me,
But I say: All wisdom is old.

I am ready to discuss this with them later...

Perhaps they should grow up earlier.

Be healthy.
