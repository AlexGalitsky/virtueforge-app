### Philosophical Context
In this letter, Seneca underscores the importance of mental fortitude and resistance to passion to achieve happiness and well-being. This reflects his philosophical belief that virtue is the foundation for achieving happiness and freedom.

### Essence
- Mental fortitude is necessary to overcome passions and attain happiness.
- A life devoid of peace and contentment cannot bring happiness.
- Loved ones can become sources of passion rather than aids in its removal.
- Vices and passions arise within the soul and require our struggle to eliminate them.
- Ambition and the pursuit of well-being are primary principles for achieving happiness.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Mental fortitude is necessary to overcome passions and attain happiness, as a life without peace and contentment cannot bring happiness.

### Connection to Virtue
Aligns with the principle of honesty, which emphasizes the importance of battling vices and the pursuit of well-being.
