# The Letter LXX (On Self-Destruction)

Lucilia greets Seneca!

Yesterday's youth has just emerged from our hearts,
But "the cities and the shores" have disappeared
And we've seen the end for all of us.

For us, it seems like a barrier,
But it's a call to speed up: Come on!
Those who swam quickly in their youth
Bring their bodies and souls to the last refuge.

Whether you hurry or delay - the end is the same,
But it's worth living with dignity.

So, the wise person will live as long as he can,
Not for how much, but while he can still live.
He thinks: How to live? It doesn't matter how...
Always ready to rise and leave,

Leaving behind a tedious life
And the madness of Fortune's whims.

Fortune has opened the door to death:
You're in power over your own life,
But you have the strength to end it all.
In Telessor, weakness whispered:

"Hope lives both in beasts and men."

I weep for life, but not at any cost:
I won't pay with my weakness.

With Fortune's power over me,
I'll run away from death's path.

But foolishly, I'd die of fear of death.

In the prison, Socrates spent thirty days
Not because life whispered: "Believe!"
He was happy talking to his friends.
Life is not always better than longer,
And in death, everything is reversed:
Follow where it calls and go that way

Open your mouth for the sword,
You'll meet wise men who say:

"Do not commit violence against life!"

Who blocks the path to freedom
Can't talk about the law.

Life doesn't hold us back;
Evil lives in the ocean.
Are you happy? - Keep going!
Are you unlucky? - Remember my lessons,
And reap your last harvest.

Having spent all your wealth - only more will come...
Having endured all your losses - all your friends are with you...

Recognizing death's inevitability,
It's time to exit with dignity.

A German man, before the fight in the circus,
Decided to choose between two options,

Put a cloth in his mouth... and then let it go.

Let them say: Death is filth - that's for fools...
But don't focus on that!

Self-destruction, believe me, is noble,
The art of killing is terrifying.
Stealing to live - shamefully obvious,
But if life is shameful - better to die.

Don't be afraid to steal...

Be healthy.
