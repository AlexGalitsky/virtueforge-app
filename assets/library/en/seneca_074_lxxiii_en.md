# A Letter of Gratitude (LXXIII)

Lucillia salutes Seneca!

Those who are wiser than the rest do not say:

"Philosophy is a secret, from the Greeks"

The scorn of rulers and kings.

On the contrary - whoever is more grateful

For a quiet hour of leisure and peace?

It is for this reason that philosophy judges that it is only because

The leader guided them by hand.

In a state where there is no common rest,

It is impossible to live a righteous life as a philosopher.

A republic or a kingdom - what matters most?

Is it not the peaceful day and night?

Any reward given without merit

Seeks to increase its own rewards.

What is the cruelty of those who are greedy? - Their ingratitude...

Whoever is not satiated - can find no joy in anything.

The official gaze shines brightly through corridors:

"Forward and upward", never "downward" or "backward".

Whoever has "come out of the crowd" soon enough...

Does not look back at the crowd.

And who looks back at the people? - That is not someone of order.

A decent and honest man, who has retired from public service

(Although he was well-known for his work),

Will thank you for your modest portion.

The king protects all in his kingdom,

But who will pay him?

Who values the good of a peaceful life more than anyone else?

That person who enjoys it.

There are many who wear fine robes,

And are eager to start wars, just as soon as...

They have "won" half the country.

Human foolishness can distinguish between

"Ownership" and "possession".

A wise man feels gratitude for all that is given,

Not just what is given to everyone in general.

Great and true blessings do not divide themselves into small parts.

They are given - for a lifetime, not for a day or year...

One thing is shared by all, while another is enjoyed by each individual.

A wise man walks in poverty's cloak

And whispers philosophy to himself:

"Be grateful for the blessing that you have received,

And pay it back generously."

Jupiter, who owns everything, gives everything to people,

He is the one who lends everything to humanity.

A wise man does not take anything, even if they steal it,

Because he does not want anything.

"With moderation, courage, and patience

Philosophers are raised to the stars".

And the gods are not envious - the steps

Are visible only to those whose spirit strives for growth.

You are surprised that people go to God?

And God goes, scattering seeds...

And when they grow, He looks at them with strictness,

And patiently waits for their due time.

He gives us signs, like a harvest:

Is it ripe or has it withered?

I see - weeds!

But where are the grains?

That I have sown in people?!

Be healthy.
