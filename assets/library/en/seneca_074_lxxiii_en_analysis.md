### Philosophical Context
Seneca's letter to Lucilius embodies philosophical thought on the importance of gratitude and humility. This text reflects the idea that wisdom and virtue can only be attained through humility and appreciation for the benefits life offers.

### Essence
- Gratitude is a key aspect of philosophy, as it enables a person to value the blessings they receive from life.
- Humility and gratitude are necessary for achieving wisdom and virtue.
- Philosophy does not prioritize personal interests or material gains but rather the well-being of all people.
- Wisdom and virtue can only be obtained through humility and gratitude for the benefits received from life.
