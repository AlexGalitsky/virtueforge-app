### Philosophical Context
Seneca's "Letter LXXIV" (On the True Good) is part of his philosophical work on virtue and the essence of true good. In this letter, Seneca emphasizes the importance of integrity as the foundation of well-being and happiness.

### Core Message
- Integrity is the only correct path to true good.
- True good is not about having a lot of money or privileges, but rather having virtue.
- Integrity is the ability to live according to one's beliefs and not be swayed by external influences.
- To achieve well-being, one must be honest and committed to their beliefs.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: Integrity is the only correct path to true good.

### Connection to Virtue
Integrity is the foundation of virtue, and virtue is the key to well-being and happiness.

### Question to Ponder
Who sacrifices their integrity for material benefits?
