# The Style of Writing
---

Lucilla greets Seneca!

You pay me back: too plain my writing style is...

One never finds elegance forever,
In my writing - only truth itself.

If I could bring you closer,
I would show feelings without words,

Not embellish them, nor diminish

Orators have been given our ears to torment.

Let the orator, before the crowd, rant and rave...

Not with words of praise.

My words are from a faithful guard of emotions.

And emotions - in what I believe and love!

We do not heal children as we heal women,

But, with gentle caresses, too - intensity of feelings.

I advise: work on your word... less

Work only on meaning.

Let one be our ultimate goal:

How we feel, so let us live and speak!

In unity arises a sense of being,

That you have created something good.

Since eloquence has been given to us by God,

Synchronizing with beauty in the soul

May it be... but not as a path to the soul.

It is only a means for souls to improve themselves...

When going to the doctor, they do not seek eloquence

They seek knowledge, willpower, and firmness of hand.

Perhaps sometimes you may meet a handsome face,

But I have nothing to congratulate myself with, my friend.

Why do you stir up my ears with words?

There is no meaning in amusement for someone who is ill...

Pain yourself, cut it, do whatever you want...

But show me that you know your subject.

So that I can see: you have mastered it,

Not just read a thick book...

He who has achieved wisdom is blessed.

Let us divide knowledge into categories:

This first one: overcoming the sickness of the soul,

Having learned everything, being near to wisdom

They cannot fall - they may only sin.

Diseases are twisted judgments,

Urgent striving... for nothing that is not worthy of love,

And even money...

Here - there is stinginess, ambition, and every kind of shame.

In sudden passions - our weakness lies,

What can drive people to tears...

A slight cold becomes a cough,

And in continuous cases - wait for tuberculosis.

The second category is easy to identify:

They have gotten rid of the main evils of the soul,

But so that safety is not guaranteed,

They may rush down to sin.

The third group still has its share of faults:

They have expelled greed - but anger remains,

Their desires are gone, but fear is tormenting them,

And their ambition is drowning...

Look around you: how much filth

Is in the hearts of people, and shame enters homes...

Do not despise the third judgment,

And it is achievable only with effort.

You want to be "in the first category"?

And I also wish that for us both...

But we must get out of sin,

It lives in us, like a family thread.

Do not fear death: death is not evil, my dear friend.

God is not to be feared, there is no Creator from evil.

If someone has been hurt by something,

That person - is helpless as a scapegoat.

From filth - we rise up to the heights,

Where the judgment of men - is buzzing like a swarm of flies.

Those who have recognized themselves - are unbreakable.

Take control of yourself!

Be healthy.
