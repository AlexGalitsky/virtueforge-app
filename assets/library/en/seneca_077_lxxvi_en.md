# The Virtue of Humanity

Lucilius greets Seneca!

You want to know what I do every day?

I go to training with a colleague...

You ask: Is it lazy at this age?

What is age? Foolishness - not learning,

Because those who have forgotten the lessons of wisdom...

The theater and circus are good for a break,

But is philosophy's language harmful?!

Learning, while you're still getting used to something.

Even "live forever, live in eternal learning"... how to live

And I'm learning: an old man until he's almost dead

It's useful to learn to appreciate things.

The path to virtue - not joy, not suffering.

Who is virtuous - is chaste before God:

In good times and bad, they see their law.

Their duty is only a road,

Not passion that puts everything at risk.

Where else are there other blessings besides virtue?

There's the joy of life, pleasures for the body...

and boundless greed on flags...

that can't be satiated, no matter how much you want it.

Is it better to have money or honors?

That person is happier than God.

What is freedom from desire - poverty, nudity?

A madman... could believe that...

What's equal for humans and animals?

Can't be the dream of human destiny.

An animal would agree if it were free...

But this can't be.

All this is hard to accept!

Until you ask yourself:

Am I ready to give up life?

For my country, for my fellow citizens... I love them.

Let's enjoy the accomplishment of our actions

We experience a fleeting moment in life,

but anticipation - more joyful than a cup...

Tell him: The people will condemn you!

He responds: What then? Be what will be!

But honor me... I won't negotiate.

Wealth, honors - they don't give greatness,

Even if it seems so...

Take away the pedestal and you'll see the truth.

A giant is enormous from any angle.

Our flaws are not visible to us,

What we look at is only those who are more colorful.

For a fool, everything is new,

and their misfortune has its root in novelty.

For the wise, known misfortunes are already experienced.

"Ah! I didn't expect such a thing..." 

Says one doctor and calls out to others.

Another is ready for anything... and remains calm.

What's predictable - is easier.

Be healthy.
