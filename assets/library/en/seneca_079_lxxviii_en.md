**The Letter LXXVIII (On Overcoming Pain)**

Lucilla greets Seneca!

I'm familiar with the pangs of illness: swollen eyelids, a morning of misery and an evening of agony...

As I was young, I thought I could conquer illnesses with my willpower:

"I can stop the pain with my strength."

But now that I'm old, even my strongest will is no match for them.

I've had to endure so much suffering that I've often thought about ending my life.

I worried about how my father would cope with his own struggles against fate.

But then I realized that I wanted to live until the end of his journey.

It was philosophy that brought me comfort:

A cure-all remedy stronger than any medicine.

I'm grateful for its wisdom, which has helped me find peace in my problems and life...

I owe a debt of gratitude to many friends who have supported me with their attention and care.

I believed that as long as I had them by my side, my spirit would not be lost.

Try these remedies too!

Listen to what the doctor has to say.

When you're ill, money, power, and pleasure won't bring you joy or relief.

There are three things that make life hard:

Fear of death, a life without happiness, and pain.

What's more painful than fear?

Let's calmly analyze this together.

We've talked about death before:

The fear of nature was my only concern...

But I managed to cheat fate by recovering from illness without suffering the consequences.

I've been fortunate enough to avoid two major setbacks.

Illnesses may seem like a curse, but they're not unique to humans.

Death is inevitable for all of us, whether we have illnesses or not.

The pain in our joints is particularly intense...

It's as if our body is on fire and then suddenly becomes numb again.

Arthritis and gout are excruciatingly painful...

Even the first scratch can be agonizing.

Pain in our teeth, eyes, and ears is unbearable...

We lose ourselves in it, unable to think clearly.

The ignorant may find pain more unbearable than others,

Because they don't have any thoughts to distract them from their suffering.

A rational person like myself thanks God for not letting pain destroy my soul.

Let's look at pain as a temporary, annoying lump.

We're all miserable only to the extent that we believe it.

Don't share your past sufferings with friends,
Even if you want to brag about it...

Fear of future pain is growing stronger in you,

The more you try to hold onto painful memories.

In difficult times, remember:

There will be a time when you can look back on this with joy.

Pain is just a burden that God has given us.

We're like soldiers who are battered by blows to our body, hands, and face...

But we won't give up!

Why should you surrender?

Pain starts off weakly but becomes more intense:

"Ah, it hurts here... And there? It hurts even more!"

But if you behave like a fool,

Your troubles will only get stronger.

A brave warrior laughs at his tormentors,

To show them that they're not as strong as he is.

Why can't our pain be overcome by reason?

Can we not laugh away our suffering?

There's room for virtues in our sufferings,
Not just in times of war.

We all have the courage to face our troubles:

It's only when we're in those moments that we realize it.

Now, let's talk about pleasures...

We need to protect ourselves from them,

But they're also what make life worth living.

Whoever wants to enjoy food and drink will find them beautiful,

Just like a rare wine for the gourmet.

A finished meal is like frozen meat,
And luxurious indulgences are equally unappetizing.

True happiness is more precious than any pleasure.

It's brighter and more satisfying than any joy we experience.

Whether you're in pain or not, it's up to you:

To find peace with your suffering.
