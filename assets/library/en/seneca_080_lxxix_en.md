# The Letter of LXXIX (On Recognizing Virtue)

Lucilius salutes Seneca.

I eagerly await your letter: What did you see on the way to Sicily? I shall behold a neighbor in time's eye.

Places that I may not visit...

I know Scylla, with its towering cliffs,

But, along with it, Charybdis, like an open mouth.

As for her, I know little:

Is there always a whirlpool there?

And is everything that she captures carried away by the water on many miles?

To the Tavromenian shore? Or do they boast?

For those who are fed bread alone...

I eagerly await your account of adventures

Will you have found them, or were you able to reach...?

Then I shall entrust you with a commission:

To ascend Mount Etna in my honor.

Some say that Etna has become inaccessible to mariners from the distance...

Perhaps there is fire and smoke rising from it,

Or perhaps only the lazy emerge from the earth?

It is equally likely that either the fire can consume her

Or that what appears to be the sea is an illusion.

The mountain alone gives a path to fire.

Gytheum is famous for all men,

Fire roams its soil, changing the ground.

But this region is very fertile,

Everything grows there, in the face of fire.

Poetically describe the pleasure of Etna:

Ovid wrote about natural wonders behind Vergil,

North Cornelian, with mastery of poetry,

Told of the natural wonders.

They did not exhaust this pleasure,

Only sketched its outlines.

Looking at Etna carefully,

You will find the right words among your ancestors.

Following in their footsteps, in poetry more simply:

There is newness in rearranging words.

Words are not things, the first here does not boast.

Rhythm - a delight for those who know it well.

As far as I know, you, when seeing Etna,

Will open your mouth and let out tears...

You will become equal to the poets of old,

For modesty will not allow you to surpass them.

The dignity of wisdom is such:

Not to conquer those who are above us,

They are all equal under God's hand.

Those who have been conquered - whom has God rejected?

The moon does not change in size,

And the sun is one and the same.

In the whole universe, there are no other measures,

All wise people are measured by this measure.

Some have perfect behavior,

Others have clear speech,

But in them all, it's dark...

What transforms life into bliss?

It is the same in their hearts, equal.

Will Etna fall? Perhaps it will be a witness

To its destruction...

The virtue of humility does not know diminishment.

Let us try to rise to it!

The best among the worst is not bad,

Let him be content that he has left darkness behind.

My daily light is more valuable to me

Than the sun's ray breaking through the ravine.

Then my soul soars to its native heaven

And occupies a place in the heights...

With divine thoughts, I would like to live,

Leaving sin aside.

Recognition and glory are rewards for virtue,

Like a shadow of it, visible below.

It grows when envy departs...

True philosophers were not known by their names,

But those who studied them will see the light of wisdom.

Metrodor praises their friendship,

Recognizing that their names shine brightly,

And many want to remember them.

In life, however, there is silence...

Let virtue wait until it is recognized,

Not for itself, but for others, harmed by it.

Whoever believed that they lived alongside a prophet?!

But without bias, the hero of the future will be judged

By the verdict of posterity.

Why should we boast and smile?

Whoever has good deeds does not become bad.

We need to be one and the same,

Will they see us, or will they be caught off guard?

The pretence of something is of little use - what is turned inward...

Lies are obvious if you look closely!

Who can they deceive?!

Be healthy.
