### Philosophical Context
Seneca's letter to Lucilius is an exemplar of Stoic philosophy, emphasizing the importance of virtue, humility, and equality. In this letter, Seneca discusses the nature of virtue and its relationship to human life.

### Essence
- Seneca views virtue as the foundation of human well-being and happiness.
- He underscores the importance of humility, equality, and justice in achieving virtue.
- Seneca also highlights the significance of poetry and rhyme in expressing thoughts about virtue.
