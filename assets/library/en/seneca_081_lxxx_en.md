# Letter LXXX

Lucilla greets Seneca!

Today I am free. In days of heat

I sit alone, the crowd is vast,
The masses rush to watch the dice game...

I dare say, there is no disturbance:

From the spearhead, a loud cry resounds...

My thought is like an echo,

It compares the lives of mortals.

The people want entertainment and bread,

Wisdom resides in emptiness...

Everyone rejoices - the die takes flight!

Who is blessed with inner beauty?

And most importantly: how much easier

Is it to live when one's soul is built?

But, as time passes, strengthening one's back,

Souls will not find moments.

What is needed to become like that?— Desire!

A slave gives everything for freedom,

You are born freer than a bird.

Are you not less than slaves?

What do you look at? - You can't buy freedom...

What is the list of citizens? - There is no freedom in it...

Everyone is enslaved, and their rulers, and their people.

Look for the magical light in your soul.

Free yourself first from the fear of death,

From the fear of poverty:

Fortune turns to the wealthy more often,

Their gold is a source of anxiety in their hearts.

They pretend to be happy, laughing outwardly,

With hidden sorrow, compressed into a painful wound,

They play the role of kings in the eyes of their courtiers,

Even with grief, like summer flies.

Who says, "I am the master of Aegina!"?

Mocking his nose? 

He is a slave. (It's easy to check if you want.)

And his companion is an old dog.

"Be quiet, Menelaus! You have too little!

You're falling from my hand now!"

But he sleeps under a woolen blanket,

And before sleep, counts every penny.

You need a horse - take off your saddle,

You need a slave - remove your clothes...

What is the man who is elevated by law?

Uncover him... and want to change him.

The Sarmatian king will know his price

Without decorations, under the gifts of the people.

What are you weighing?— Let yourself be stripped!

Figure out yourself! 

Be healthy.
