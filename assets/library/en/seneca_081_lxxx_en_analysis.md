### Philosophical Context
This text is an excerpt from a letter written by Lucilia to Seneca, in which she expresses her nostalgia for freedom and criticizes societal behavior that she deems unethical. In this letter, Lucilia emphasizes the importance of inner freedom and moral growth.

### Essence
- Lucilia's letter to Seneca is a critique of societal behavior and underscores the importance of inner freedom.
- Lucilia highlights that people often spend years building up their external image rather than focusing on internal growth and moral development.
- She also criticizes values that prioritize personal gain and material prosperity.
- Lucilia stresses the importance of liberating oneself from the fear of death and poverty to achieve freedom.

Prose

### Connection to Virtue
Lucilia connects her critique of societal behavior to the concept of virtue, emphasizing the importance of internal growth and moral development for achieving freedom and well-being.
