**Letter LXXXII (On a Light Life and Death)**

Lucilla greets Seneca!

Fortune can deal harsh blows,

But you will not harm yourself forever.

Here is the main point that can be confirmed.

It is better to have bad things happen than to have good ones happen easily.

(Under "bad" here I consider what people say,
The weight of worries piled on their shoulders,
When there are many, they say "I'm full-mouthed".)

They envy those who live easily...

But, in the ease, one's soul will wither away.

I am glad that someone can find a strong spirit:

He laughs and breathes with excitement!

Lying among fragrant aromas

A beggar is often considered a sign of poverty.

He is a corpse, already dead by his own choice,

Like the thing caught in a trap.

Lying alone - you will go mad from boredom,

And sorrow and laughter will appear without reason.

Leisure without exercise in studies

Is like a burial shroud for all.

We cannot escape trouble,

There are always reasons to be troubled behind the wall.

Evil is not hidden anywhere,
It makes a loud noise, rolling like a wave.

Inside us and outside of us... there is only one salvation

The philosophy's wall holds it back:

A soul lives without danger inside it:

Fortune does not have enough hands to catch it.

Try to keep away from fate:

Know yourself and know nature.

You can avoid false desires,

Your reason, the source of good happiness.

He who remains calm in his days,
Smiles at death...

But when misfortune comes home to him,
And he recognizes its later praise... 

When the executioner stretches out his hand,

His loud words are not enough to help him:

This is pain! (what you said "not a burden"...)

This is death! (what you called "not scary"...)

Where is courage, and where is bravery in the heart?

You have practiced not your soul, but your tongue...

Your tongue has grown soft from seeing death,

It's been accustomed to cruelty for a long time.

Zeno used reasoning:

"There is no glory in evil,
And there may be glory in death."

This cure

Can someone forget of fear?!

No. It's not possible to speak seriously about it,
And not to make fun of the one who is going to die?!

Here, idle talk will be too late...

And whoever is so foolish as to say something like that...?

Zeno gave a clever sophism:

"What is without distinction cannot be glorious,
There is glory in death - death is not without distinction."

But this is all just words,

Not the poverty that people praise for having been corrected,

Not the exile that people praise for being strong,

Not the pain that brings glory to those who have not recognized it,

Not the death that brings glory to those who are steadfast before it.

Hail Caton! What can be said about Brutus?

Indeed, his death is more shameful than his life...

He swore by life: "I lie down!" (This is cool...)

When the executioner told him to lie down:

He almost added: Like Antioch speaking... 

With devotion... to life,

Just barely not deserving it...

All our deaths will be remembered in the future

By our descendants: who was brave, and who was cowardly.

The crowd is foolish, like little children:

Wealth, power, suffering, exile, death

Good and evil? Is there a difference between them?

This is the choice!

With this alone can you overcome it...

Everything else is of no importance,

Good and evil are just forms.

What makes someone good,
Is not the ugliness that they sing about.

The face of evil has a mark:

Love for oneself, the desire to live,

The loss of blessings, fear before darkness fade away...

These thoughts in life are like knives.

Laughing at death... is not allowed, though it should be,

But in the underworld... many things are funny:

"Ogromny pes, which guards Ork..." - us

Maybe we will never see it.

Going to death for the sake of glory - a main feat,

Like an old wolf covering its tracks.

Whoever hesitates, does not know glory.

Know: virtue is will, not duty.

The twists and turns of old tricks...

Can't send people to death.

They can only confuse their minds,
But for a while.

Here we need to act simply.

"Today I don't cheer you up,
We'll have breakfast in the underworld!" - Leonid said

"We'll be eating dinner in the underworld!"

And all were able to keep their promise...

Ctesippus knew how to talk to a warrior:

He said: "Come on! Here's your food.

I don't wait for you to come back,
You don't need to return."

Greatness does not tolerate trivialities.

For legions, meeting the fierce snake was more frightening than any enemy and battle.

They were able to break down the mountain, crush their spine,

And shoot arrows without a reason.

Big monsters are beaten with a big hammer,

What you said is correct - sharply.

But death will not stop with an acute gaze.

Do you want to meet the lion with a sword?

Be healthy.
