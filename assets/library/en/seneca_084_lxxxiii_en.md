# The Letter LXXXIII (On Intoxication)

Lucilius greets Seneca.

I am rich in hours, from alpha to omega...

I dare not look inward.

And yet, I must confess my thoughts and deeds to God.

We stand before Him naked.

Helpless in our mortal flesh.

I submit to your counsel - it is good.

About the future we dream... what awaits us?

Rare memories of the past offer little guidance.

The past's advice is often too late.

Today, I slept and indulged in pleasure,

And with the remaining time, I devoted myself to physical exercises...

I became exhausted instantly: old age brings weakness to the body.

My Faras is a young boy, not rough or unkind.

Our common crisis he has addressed:

We are both losing our teeth...

It's sad that his new ones have just begun to grow.

I took a refreshing bath,

Cold water invigorated me...

When I will only bathe in hot water,

I will understand that I am an old man.

What do I ponder? The day of yesterday

Reminded me how wise husbands speak about the most important things:

Who can trust someone who is drunk?

But they trust those who are good.

Who is good, closes the doors to drunkenness!

Is it a fight against intoxication or just wordplay?

Parody: "They don't trust sleepers,

But they trust those who are good...

Who is good - does not sleep!"

Is this true? Or what's the point here...?

Our Asclepius offered protection:

"Zeno curses drunkenness, as a sin...

Here, it's not that person who is drunk in the moment

Who has failed to overcome his desire for intoxication."

This defense doesn't work well:

The letter "G" on horseback - no use for a farmer,

Zeno looked for an opportunity to attack...

To find the truth, you can't.

For Caesar, it was like trying on a new cloak...

(That person who conquered the state),

And those who are drunk were more trusted than sober ones...

(Taking Cimber as an example, he joked about it).

Pison was always drunk, but Rome kept its doors closed,

He didn't lose his keys.

Augustus trusted him:

Do you remember who calmed the Fratii?

Later, Coscus (who came to the Senate intoxicated),

Fell asleep, and we had to remove him,

In trust, he was... (Tiberius - is he mad?)

He didn't talk about anything else.

It's not worth discussing intoxication:

Like a pot can break a bucket

With warm water inside,

A drunk person can't keep secrets.

You're better off exposing it openly,

Saying that it's a sin (read: a mistake).

A little bit - good for appetite,

Blessed is the one who has mastered this passion.

Ashamed to carry excess in his belly,

And go mad...

Until he reaches the true grave

The distance is short, very short indeed.

Even the Macedonian death was worthy:

Anger in intoxication lowered him to the bottom.

Our shame - sin.

That's it - brave warrior...

But falls weakly before wine.

Intoxication doesn't produce sins,

It shows them off,

And forces them to pay taxes

In that measure, we are all alive.

The drunkard's words are meaningless and disconnected,

Weakened legs, the whole house spins around...

The next morning - pain in the stomach from cramps,

And the mind is hard to control...

Vino (wine) is an enemy and a foe to brave men,

Who don't bend towards the abyss on the edge.

Alexander remained unharmed

After passing through battles, rivers, and seas.

He passed through places that others couldn't reach...

Wine destroyed the great king.

Perhaps one of the wild feasts can hold a full cup...

(Who drinks under the table, and who eats with his mouth open)

And he won't be able to conquer it.

Antonius... was great and noble,

A passion for Cleopatra and wine - a flaw...

Here's an example of a meal with Cicero,

Who is miserable when drunk,

The swiftness of wine brings out its ferocity,

Every day, the madness of drinking,

The fortress of reason weakens under its assault...

A wise man doesn't get drunk,

He builds his life on following rules.

Drunkards stray from their path,

And can't show a good example.

I won't even listen to them,

When they speak with false words:

A wise man does not die from poison,

Will he become sober?

Becoming drunk will make him sober?

Be healthy.
