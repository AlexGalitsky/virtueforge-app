# The Stoics and Peripatetics

Lucilia greets Seneca!

You were merciful... Now you ask for comparisons of an opponent and a colleague...

Oh, Lord, grant me strength!

"Reasoned man in all is moderate;

Moderate - steadfast in misfortune,

Not knowing sorrow, only sure in himself,

Blessed." - They say this everywhere...

Peripatetic will say: Stop, it's not necessary

To take everything too literally:

Nothing human is foreign to the wise,

There is a reason for the mind to be disturbed.

How can we understand: "Does not know sorrow"?

That one who does not give in to his will...

Sorrow - at the beginning of humanity,

In nature, like a storm or sunrise.

Then the wise man - stronger than the weakest?

A little above the lowliest, a little brighter than darkness?

With restraint, the tumultuousness of a beast,

More patient than cripples or lame people?!

So, all vices taken in order...

We will find only flattery in the words of the wise.

Or is it to seek the cause of evil in moderation?

Is he healthy who has a weakness?

In "empty" riches... there are seeds,

Different from ordinary, unsteady.

Evil, which begins small and cunningly

Grows and leads us to trouble.

Our reason is never equal to our passions,

The torrent of passion wipes it out...

Passions that consume us until the edges...

Consciousness, like a handkerchief in the nose.

Like a lion, passion - does not submit to anyone,

Even when subdued, it seizes its moment,

Seeing blood, it roars and burns with fire...

What is taming? - Where is your instrument?!

Who is possessed by passion, like an obsession,

Could not create a barrier for himself in his own soul:

We are easier to prevent the birth of vices

Than to overcome them later.

Vices cover us alive,

And reason - a useless sum.

"Moderation in vices" is simply false,

Like the slogan "do not rush to go mad".

Harshness, stinginess, and dishonesty towards duty

Are known since ancient times.

And that's why they live so long,

Because they do not change their own names.

From age to age, anyone among us is a witness,

They are like spider webs of strong thread.

The art of governance is accessible to virtue,

Vices can only be eradicated.

Giving some will to bad impulses,

You lose control over them forever:

Our fear grows when we see external pain,

Whatever the wise man says, it's all useless...

Who allowed passion to take root in himself?

Then he spends his whole life fighting with it, Don Quixote.

When things are no longer under our control,

Not even our reason can predict their outcome.

"He is not angry, being angry rarely..."

"He, though not afraid, fears sometimes"... 

Sorrow changes the soul, like a cell,

And every day - more signs of harm.

We are academics, and truth-seekers,

Left with this phrase (how to understand it?):

"Blessed can be only virtue,

But perfection in goodness is not reached."

Who thinks so, simply forgets:

There is a path for us before God,

Above all else...

Bliss cannot be divided into steps,

Blessed or not - there is no other.

Who is not blessed - repentance and penitence...

Shining bright even when everything around is dark...

The size of virtue is unimportant, its property:

A blessed soul is always full.

There is no room for vice, worry in it...

In it, pure truth shines.

"Unwise bravery is madness!" 

For troubles - not in account!

Reasoned man trembles with fear,

And what wise man can stop danger?

"What will you do under a blow to yourself?"

Not at all! He is cautious, not afraid of him.

He does not avoid danger, but takes it by the scruff...

Of his neck, enjoying it.

The trouble is in giving up our freedom!

All else - just an appearance of trouble:

Bondage, poverty, fear year after year,

Weakened by the weight of suffering's trail...

Suffering hurts and makes us worse,

Misfortune and poverty - not misfortune.

A ship has capsized in a storm, and we need

A sailor who is master of hard labor.

Peripatetic will say: "As we sail,

The storm makes us mad, the wise man - only misery."

In them there is difference in goals: first - to complete the journey,

Second - to follow the right course.

And, indeed, what does the cook promise

Us with happiness? - Only correctness of the way...

He defends himself against Neptune's artistry,

And knows that, otherwise, he would not pass.

The labor of the cook is to carry goods over long distances,

Like the work of a doctor - healing the sick.

There is a commonality in wisdom and our shared heritage,

Even closer to the wise man than to others.

Let his necessity press him

He can bring benefit to people:

Luck, insults, and lawsuits...

He cannot inflict blows on anyone.

And happiness and unhappiness - only an excuse

To show us virtue in our actions.

The path of virtue is a strict test from God,

Not the name: Christ or Allah.

There is no other way but this:

Do not confuse virtue with fame.

A sculptor could carve a statue from bone,

And bronze could shape it into form...

Even granite - he would have shown us

That beauty, which stone could hide.

So virtue, in glory and in wealth,

Or in poverty, in exile, and in the state... 

Leaves behind only what can be done.

And later, they will understand.
