# The Sophism of Wealth

Lucilia greets Seneca!

Accompanied by several slaves,

With Maximus on a cart (a friend for life)

They trample with their teeth.

A tunic and two cloaks - in exchange for a bed,

A traveling breakfast - a handful of dried mulberries,

When there is bread, they eat as an appetizer,

And, instead of... if the bread cannot be found.

Mules trot, proving that they are alive...

Such carts are not often encountered.

Someone will meet us - shame will wake up the liar,

The concept of virtue has not been grasped:

I am troubled by the opinion of travelers

I am ashamed of poverty in this present time.

All the words I spoke to the ignorant,

I find myself as the addressor...

"Another god is rich, with a great debt,

A beautiful house - he took out a loan,

A beautiful child - he did not pay him back,

And a debt - it was used as collateral for prison or money."

In the road, they eat from golden cups,

Under the city - a large landholding,

But, poor he is - all in debt... Where is it from?

It does not matter: from people, fortunes, and businesses...

Whether it is from someone, fortune, or business.

Cato was riding an old cart,

Even with sacks hanging from both sides...

You would have preferred to be a vagabond, I am sure,

That horse that was struggling in the journey Cato.

He had always predicted and called "obedience"

That life we are now coming to...

Enough... The muse of virtue is calling,

"Any good makes us better;

The musician who has known music.

Is it not beautiful? - Stoic said."

Peripatetic: To become a musician

It cannot be that someone, who was born without talent and hearing,

Can become a musician.

Look for the art above himself:

Even without instruments...

He can become a musician.

"Not good is what is able to take the most coveted,

To possess wealth can also be a thief:

In wealth, there are not usually benefits,

Only greed among people."

They will say: "There is an error in reasoning:

A grammarian, cook, or doctor... 

Stars do not capture them, but without mistakes,

Their art is better than that of the wise men."

But, their art does not have the greatness of spirit!

They cannot strive upwards,

Who has a good heart, he will be happy to misfortune.

He is not shaken by it, this is all old...

Natal (the language is so deceitful and impure,

that Natal cleansed him with his mouth)

Was rich - inherited.

Why?

Sometimes they throw away gold in the dirt.

But, virtue - is much higher than wealth,

The abilities of a grammarian, doctor:

They find joy in payment,

Not in achieving the beginning.

Money is not worth anything to Moshna,

Only what is hidden in her,

Now compare: wealthy or Stoic

In whom - an empty shell? In whom - a portrait of the country?

It is only the wisdom of man that attracts God,

The call beckons him to an unknown flight...

Heavenly things do not come to everyone,

And, even when they do, they recognize it.

"Evil does not arise from good;

A person becomes rich by being stingy:

In wealth, there is no goodness, unless not peaceful."

"The treasure of Poseidon is full of filth."

But, in saintliness - more evil than good:

There is fear, anxiety, and repentant pain.

And profit? - Not even for a year...

And after? - Choose to be stronger,

"Running after wealth is dangerous... 

Is there not only the spirit's decay?

A flock of sheep is being led by a shepherd...

You would have preferred to be a vagabond, I am sure,

That horse that was struggling in the journey Cato.

He had always predicted and called "obedience"

That life we are now coming to...

Enough... The muse of virtue is calling,

"Any good makes us better;

The musician who has known music.

Is it not beautiful? - Stoic said."

Antipater responds:

"You cannot fill a empty cup with wine,

That poor man, who feels the lack,

You can't make coats from scraps."

What to reason about poverty and wealth?

It's only an argument of words...

How to save the poor, how to help them?

And how for the wealthy to cultivate modesty?

What was taken away from all of us,

Is easily taken from one person... 

I will not remember it.

Let us say boldly (if I am not able)

openly to a handful of thieves:

Think about it, while you have been asked,

Do not boast about your wealth.

Be healthy.
