# The Freedom of Arts and Sciences

Dear Seneca,

Can we discuss the freedom of arts and sciences?

I don't need their patronage:

They cannot help or harm me.

Preparing one's mind is their concern,
And then, if they won't interfere,
That's it.

They are merely training, not work,
Worthy of devotion only in life.

The arts are called free because
A free person engages with them.

Can I prove my readiness?
Not necessary.

Freedom does not last forever.

True freedom lies in wisdom,
Which leads to freedom from the self.

But in other things, it's just an echo of feelings,
And then emptiness.

Who can truly hear their freedom?

Do you believe that they bring us good?
I don't think so.

There are no worse teachers than those who have failed.

Anyone can learn art and war
In a year.

What's the point of "enjoying" more?
Is it not just a waste of time?

A grammarian is concerned with proposing sentences,
Not creating something new.

He will say that "mother" should be "mama"
And "father" should be "papa".

If he wants to become a historian
Or poet,

He will have to learn to distinguish between
What's important and what's not.

It's not the lack of freedom that holds us back,
But our own desires and fears.

The storm of passions tempts us,
And we can't hold on to ourselves.

How can we love our country, our wife, and our brother?
Teach me how to strive for virtue!

Is it not better to aim for God
Than for worldly gain?

Was Penelope pure when Odysseus returned?
Did she know when he would come back?

Tell me about chastity,
You teach like a classic.

But how can I find harmony in my own heart?
How can I not lose myself in the twists and turns of life?

A geometer teaches us to measure the earth
But what's the point of measuring something that's already known?

Is it not just a way to calculate our desires,
To count our wealth?

What's the use of knowing how to divide land
When we can't even share it with our brothers?

Everything we learn in school
Doesn't develop our soul.

You're not the master here, you're just an outsider:

You came and will leave when your time is up.

And after a hundred generations,
Few people will remember you.

A mathematician can calculate the circumference of a circle
But can he find his way among the stars?

Can he measure the soul of another person?
How can we find our way in life?

What's the point of knowing when Mercury will appear
Or where Saturn is now?

I don't believe in astrology,
It's just a bunch of nonsense.

I won't lose hope, even if things go wrong.
I'll wait for good fortune and bad luck,
And I'll recognize them sooner or later.

I'm done with long lists of arts and sciences.

Who are the worthy ones?
The sculptors, warriors, and painters?

Then what about cooks?
What's their connection to freedom?

Is there something common between them
And our own free spirit?

Do they have a soul that's been neglected,
A heart that's been starved for attention?

I don't understand why you're so critical of art.

Philosophers use it, but it's not essential.

Just because we can't do something without it,
Doesn't mean it's necessary.

There are wise people who know more than grammar,
Their wisdom is in their actions, not words.

Wisdom needs space to grow and develop,
Not just a bunch of words on paper.

It's better to measure ourselves against virtue
Than against the world.

A philosopher doesn't need books or education,
He finds his truth by living.

But philosophers have become so humble,
They've lost sight of what's truly important.

They're more concerned with being part of a group
Than with speaking the truth.

Their words are just a way to avoid the truth,
To hide behind clever phrases.

Oh, Lord! I need the light of truth!

Not this darkness of ignorance.

Be healthy.
