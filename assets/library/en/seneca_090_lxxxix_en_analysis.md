### Philosophical Context
Seneca's letter to Lucilius is an example of philosophical correspondence, where various aspects of philosophy and its relationship to virtue are explored. The letter breaks down philosophical subjects into three parts: ethics, nature, and mind.

### Essence
- Philosophy is divided into three parts: observation (valuation), motivation (will), and action (deed).
- Ethics is the branch of philosophy concerned with moral principles and the ways of living decently.
- Observation involves the study of values and the valuation of all things.
- Motivation involves the study of motives and incentives.
- Action involves the study of human actions and their consequences.
