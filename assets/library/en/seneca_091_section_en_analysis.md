### Philosophical Context
This text is a letter from Seneca to Lucilius, in which he discusses the connection between philosophy and crafts. Seneca emphasizes the importance of understanding the human quest for wisdom and virtue.

### Essence
- Philosophy is the foundation of life.
- To understand the quest: How should one live?
- Wisdom is the ability to understand and navigate the world.
- Philosophy teaches us how to live a virtuous life.
- Wisdom does not require possessions but provides the essence of life.

### Connection to Virtue
Seneca underscores the importance of virtue and honesty in life. He asserts that wisdom is the foundation of virtue and that it helps people live a worthy life.
