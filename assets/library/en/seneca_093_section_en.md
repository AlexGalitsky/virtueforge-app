# The Letter of XSP (On a Happy Life)

Lucilia greets Seneca!

I think we agree:

Like the body - it seeks external success,
The soul seeks its own need.

From the irrational and rational parts
It seems that the soul is, and the lord
In it, all things are related, and happy
Who sees God - the path to happiness is one.

Our reason, if it possesses perfection,
Will help us choose the best way

It has revealed the essence of love: the threshold of bliss,

When giving is more pleasant than receiving.

A happy life has "quiet and will",
For those whose reason finds truth,

Which is not taught in school.

What is a noble gift, and what pleases God?

Nothing, otherwise - it comes from the source of good.

Chance takes us by the hand,
But there are no causes to lift it up.

The great Antipater wrote about this,
That external happiness is like a cake...

He seems to be dissatisfied with the sun's light,

And waits for some other spark.

For those who are dissatisfied with what is honest,

Call quiet and enjoyment:

Quiet - a known assistant of wisdom,

And enjoyment - a good for cattle.

You treat people, not men...

Is there sense in enjoying pleasures?

Not even to God?!

To animals, which have received food from the earth.

"Humanity's greatest art

There is a virtue that belongs to flesh,
Capable only of eating and drinking since eternity."

The victory of spirit - the stronghold of wisdom.

"To health and absence of suffering
I strive? "- Nature in my grasp,

I strive, but only in moderation...

I go to dinner.

I choose clean clothes,

Who - a man should be neat.

But happiness - not in things, nor in expectations

To have more, forgets the soul.

The soul's dress is our body,

Take it as I said.

I do not strive for health blindly,

Not in joy - with a dull knife...

Such sayings are from wise men:

Who have not reached the highest bliss,

When it torments them with pain and injury,

They cannot rise to the top.

However, you allow that someone can be happy and blessed,

Only the highest bliss deny.

Is he deprived of strength when climbing?

Can pleasant things be equal to unpleasant ones?

Are they outside us?

They cannot be overcome by us,

Whether it is happiness or Parnassus.

Yes, sometimes clouds cover the sun,

But this does not prevent it:

It's not what we see from the window.

(No need to explain why...)

Such virtue: when touched by misfortunes and pains

Does not weaken, but only for a moment,

Like a shadow that covers the sun.

People say that a wise man is healthy,

Although he is not unhappy, but not joyful...

I find this logic cow-like,

Where wisdom is mixed with foolishness - salad.

How can you compare reverence to contempt?

Can you equalize chance and greatness?

Is it only for those who have insight into reason?

Should we exchange the strength of healthy legs for stability?

"Water is warm and cold at the same time,
And between them - warm water."

Good luck from wisdom does not come from misfortunes...

This comparison is just a joke.

Who can resist?
