### Philosophical Context
Seneca's letter to Lucretia emphasizes the importance of happiness and virtue in life.

### Essence
- Sympathy and acknowledgment that happiness is not constant and is dependent on fate.
- A call to seek the source of happiness within one's soul rather than in external circumstances.
- Emphasis on the importance of resilience and gratitude in adverse situations.
- An invitation to emulate great men who have forsaken happiness in pursuit of virtue.
- An appeal to nature and a quest for ways to live so as not to be subject to suffering and death.

### Connection to Virtue
Virtue is the bedrock of reliable moral support in life, whereas happiness is a variable that cannot be guaranteed.

### Question to Ponder
Who can live in such a way as to not be subject to suffering and death?
