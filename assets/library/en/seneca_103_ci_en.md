# The Letter of CI (On the Unpredictability of Death)

Dear Seneca,

Every day reminds us that we are nothing.

The grand designs laid out for eternity only serve to highlight our emptiness.

You asked why I started this. 

Did you know Seneca?

A brilliant man, not a boy in matters...

Yesterday with him, he passed away... 

He was a Roman cavalryman, a strong man,

Rich and listening to my counsel...

Under the evening sun, a toad

Squeezed his throat. Death came: Hello...

He invested his money in circulation on land

And at sea, turning everything into profit...

"Ah, Melibeyus, take some grapes to plant,

Rake the grapevine as promised."

Madness - representing oneself as a hero,

planning, like "in the next year

I'll lend and demand, build,

Then calmly spend my old age..."

Believe me: even the happy ones are wrong,

Success is always slipping through their hands...

You think you're sure? Say to yourself: "Of course..."

God will give - I won't dare push him away.

Someone chases consequences after causes,

And becomes a rich man, or will become one?

We see death in someone else's bed,

And then it comes... like a brick from the roof...

It happened just like every day...

Who is surprised? That's incredibly stupid:

So, the student forgets the lesson

Read this, but don't be lazy.

Who wastes their present for nothing?

Must rely on hopes and dreams all life long.

Don't rush ahead, take simple morality:

Focused - the day goes by in two.

Who sums up each evening with life...

Isn't afraid of tomorrow's day.

Know: if reason outruns legs,

Then fear is powerless against it.

I'm troubled by the prayer of Mецената:

Ready to be crucified without hands or feet... for life

Prepared to endure torture - only not to be dissected...

To whom you can say: Hold on.

Vergilius once read him:

"Think: is death scary?"

And is it shameful to cling to life?!

The cost of freedom is bitter.

How many are willing to sacrifice a friend,

Just to live alone?

For a handful of coins... The curse of life goes around,

And in the circle, there are no good news!

That's just madness, that loud laughter of a parrot,

That blasphemy, that riot...

For many, it's a blessing that our age is short.

Forget your thirst for life.

Be healthy.
