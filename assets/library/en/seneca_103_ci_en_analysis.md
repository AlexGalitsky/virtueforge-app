### Philosophical Context
Stoicism is a philosophical school that emphasizes the importance of reason, moral virtue, and acceptance. Seneca, one of the most renowned Stoics, was a Roman statesman and philosopher.

### Essence
- Death is an inescapable aspect of human existence.
- Our lives are unpredictable, and we do not know when they will end.
- We should focus on the present and not waste time on the future or the past.
- Stoicism underscores the importance of reason and moral virtue in addressing life's challenges.
- We must be prepared for death and not try to avoid it, but instead focus on what we can control—our behavior and decisions.

### Connection to Virtue
Stoicism is linked to the idea that virtues are the foundation of a person's moral development. Seneca emphasizes the importance of virtues such as humility, justice, and self-control for achieving moral worth.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us is ready for death and does not attempt to evade it?
