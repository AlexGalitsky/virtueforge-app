# On Recognition

Lucilia salutes Seneca.

I pondered the immortality of the soul...

Your letter, interrupting my successes,

Arrived. I wish to respond hastily.

Who awakens, disrupting a dream,

Is tiresome.- I shall share your burden

And return to the former delight

Of hopes for victory over fate.

You write: "Blessings from beyond the grave

I was unable to clearly show...

Did not prove the futility of daring:

To gaze into the eyes of glory in life."

Is it foolish to try to attain

Recognition in life, rather than waiting?

Does everything wither away to dust?

Or is there something left that humanity will preserve?

Do you demand that I explain,

Perhaps it's necessary? (It seems so.)

What is one striving for? What is your reward?

What is glory? What is recognition? What is life?

Let us examine the dialectics of this issue,

And the conclusions drawn from them...

(Although, believe me: such thoughts... are in exile)

There is a goal-oriented aspect: take a person

A composite aspect: part of an argument - a word

An addition exists: a crowd of fools.

But, thank goodness, it has not been broken down into parts

In the unity of a single spirit,

The source - and virtues, and happiness...

(What dialectics divides, like madness)

"You say that blessings are only goal-oriented?

Is recognition the verdict of good men's judges?"

Then, if so, is recognition the blessing?

"Recognition - praise (sound, voice, and voice),"

"But a cough or a whistle when the chest is inflamed..."

Is recognition not a blessing?

"Honored? Are you honored, in this blessing?

If one deserves to be praised,

The praise will be his reward.

You cannot heal someone else's health."

...

Now I shall respond individually:

Who told you that blessings are not in parts?

Recognition - it is not what we thought

Where voices are like points on bones.

Recognition - the result of a vote?

For the chosen ones, an exciting game...

One recognized - enough recognition,

The same thought among all good men.

Only the man who is virtuous will recognize the truth,

And, indeed, that is the only truth.

Do not believe the crowd, even if they were glad:

They do not know this truth.

Praise - it's not a voice, it's an opinion...

Not said by wise approval

Praise, to me, is not needed.

The source of praise - the soul, praise is similar,

And, distinguish, who praised you, to that extent...

Gneius Navi wrote: "It is excellent when we are praised by a worthy man."

Cicero: "Praise nourishes art."

(The note: not praise, but recognition.)

"Praise of the people" (in it - the roots of decadence)

And art has led to ugliness.

Creates glory through many voices,

In recognition - only good voices,

Sometimes, under the shout of a crowd, or a strict order

of authorities... And what is more valuable?

Think for yourself...

"I am honored, whether I praise or not"

I am grateful to all, because I love them.

Their gratitude - it's their reward,

Which I shall share with them.

I swear that he who judges kindly will receive the blessing

Measuring what he gave.

And justice - a blessing, without condition

For both parties, look at the medal.

That is what I have done...

And what if it's not our goal?

All disputes are only a search for an adventure,

Attempts to entice each other into a trap.

Is it better to go the direct road,

Rather than wandering through the forest by choice?

The soul strives upward - given nature

How can we set limits for it?!

The soul does not accept brevity of time,

A century is not the limit of a great mind.

Even if someone is blind in his own sea,

I hope that their descendants will understand...

Like months in the womb of a mother,

A child lives, growing until birth

We grow, so that we can reach heaven.

Do not be afraid of the decisive hour,

The last for body, but not for soul!

Nature considers us all, and

When we leave this world, it will take away everything.

It's amusing to fear the new birth,

Remember how your mother used to push you:

You cried and feared injury...

Now, you must understand that

Separating yourself from something unpleasant

Will bring you a beautiful light.

Imagine the heavenly radiance,

Where the brilliance of countless stars is not counted.

And, in this new existence,

The soul will enter, for the first time - in full growth.

To one who has seen the divine light,

It's clear: life was lived in darkness,

Dirt... He remembers all those he wronged,

Those he left in fear, and poverty.

In front of God's eyes,

Your sins will be revealed immediately:

Deceit, dishonesty, and cruelty...

The man who understands with reason!

Think about the memory of great men

In their descendants - a gift.

Look at eternity - fear will be in you.

Glory is beneficial.
