Title: The Source of Misfortune
---
# The Source of Misfortune

Lucillia greets Seneca!

Why do you shudder at the thought of misfortune,
what brings us fires, collapses, and floods?

There is no malicious intent in nature.

Such things may occur, but they are rare,
perhaps ten times a century.

The storm warns us of danger,
the crackling of wood signals - the house will collapse...

But man... you do not know when he will strike,

And even if you try to defend yourself with difficulty?

Do not be afraid to believe the faces that meet you

For within them, there is only a wild soul...

No, not even a beast, when it meets another,
turns away and breathes more calmly.

Those who have seen him do not look for him again:

Because he has attacked - hunger or fear.

But man... steals from the poorest,

And rejoices in their downfall.

You are not like that, you must live differently:

Seeking to avoid his schemes,

Help others with their successes,

Try not to harm anyone.

Even if you can harm them, they will try...

Take refuge in philosophy's house,

They meet on one road,

But here there is a chance that the other side understands.

Philosophers do not boast or mock,

Only expose their own flaws.

Be wise, and do not stir up hatred

From the causes of danger.

Be healthy.
