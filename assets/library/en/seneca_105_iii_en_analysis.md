### Philosophical Context
Stoicism is a philosophical school that emphasizes personal growth, moral character, and humility. Seneca, one of the most renowned Stoics, wrote a series of letters to Lucilius discussing various topics, including the nature of evil and the ability to circumvent it.

### Essence
- There is no inherent evil in wrongdoing, but it may occur infrequently.
- Humans are constantly at risk of harm, yet they often do not know how to protect themselves.
- We are warned about potential harm, such as smoke indicating a fire.
- A person is likely to attack others due to hunger or fear.
- We should strive to circumvent evil and avoid harming others.

### Connection to Virtue
This text is linked to virtue, as it underscores the importance of humility, wisdom, and the ability to circumvent evil. It aligns with the idea that virtue is the capacity to live in harmony with nature and to refrain from harming others.
