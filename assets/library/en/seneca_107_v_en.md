# Letter to CV (On Dangers)

Lucilla greets Seneca!

Need, envy, hatred, and fear,
Are the feelings of humanity,
Drawing us into danger and collapse.

Disdain is the simplest:
It tramples people over without a second thought,
But beats those who are standing up.

Do not bring shame to hope when it cannot be taken,
Nor boast about goodness in front of fools,
Lest envy fail to understand you.

Do not offend - you will not be hated,
Offense can lead to hatred.

Who inspires fear is not strong enough to foresee,
There are no weak ones who cannot cause harm.

Disdain, if measured, lies within your power,
Is not frightening. Friendship and flattery help.

Those who have achieved honor and power in the world,

But there is a cure worse than the disease.

Do not rush ahead of all others during praise,
And know when to give a blowback at meetings.

Talk less with others,
Talk more to yourself.

A conversation can be tempting and sweet,
That makes you reveal your secrets:

The whole city will discuss your weakness,
Which was safely hidden under the bed.

It is unfair to act unjustly:
You will have to answer for it later.

Once a vessel is empty, evil loses its power...

To one who has opened it up.

Those with an unclean conscience will be punished,
Although they may have hidden their wrongdoing cleverly:

God will see that it is not possible for humans.

They will pay the price.

Be healthy.
