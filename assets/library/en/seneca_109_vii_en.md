# The Letter of VII (On the Escape of Slaves)

Lucilla greets Seneca!

You are suffering over nothing.

Have the slaves found an opportunity for escape?

True friendship is more valuable than any other treasure.

Life is like a bath, a beating, and a journey:

Beaten, deceived, and covered in dirt...

Yes, life is harsh, and many will suffer,

And those who prepare for the worst will be rewarded.

What is our life: the floor or the battlefield?

Many arrows have already hit their mark,

Some have missed. Will you say, "I would rather die"?

Will you... wait for Paracelsus to heal you?

The sorrowful worries of others will only bring joy to those who are already dead.

Who has been able to see beyond what is coming?

That person is truly great in wisdom.

He whose mind is not clouded by laziness will be happy with his lot,

And he will know that he is like a brother to the poor.

Thanks to calm reflection,

There will never be a beginner in any trouble.

The slaves have left you?- And what if they have?

Sometimes, people are beaten without being asked how they were called...

You are alive and healthy, and you have not been destroyed,

"Scratches before marriage will heal..."

A stray arrow may poison us,

But will it hit its mark? - It is the will of God.

"Equal rights" does not mean "equal power",

And therefore, we are given the right to live.

In winter, we lose something to the cold,

And in summer, we suffer from the heat...

This is a beast... and this is a man, more ferocious than any beast...

Do not argue with nature, get out of the game!

A cyclone is coming, followed by anticyclones,

Night will follow day, and darkness will follow dawn...

A bad soldier who joins the army with a scowl,

Is worse than someone who resists nature.

Like Cicero, who had the power to choose,

I have started translating poetry myself.

Will you like it? - I hope so!

And if not?- Then... I will count my sins!

"Lead me, Father, wherever you wish!"

I will follow, without hesitation or complaint...

The spirit of those who are conquered by God is great.

Whoever wants to change himself must change according to God's will.

Be healthy.
