### Philosophical Context
Letter CVII from Lucretia to Seneca is a response to his letter about the escape of slaves, in which he expresses concern over freedom and virtue. In this letter, Lucretia emphasizes the importance of fidelity and calm reflection in life, and raises questions about how one can remain innocent and good in the face of difficulties and nuances.

### Essence
- A faithful hand is worth more than the most precious friendship!
- Life is like a bathhouse, a crush, a journey: one is shoved, deceived, besmirched...
- Yes, life is rough; expect ill from many.
- Like Cicero, who had the freedom to choose, I began to translate poems on my own.
- A great spirit entrusts itself to God.
