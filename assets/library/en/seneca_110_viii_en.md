# The Letter CXXXVIII (On Philosophy and Philology)

Lucilia greets Seneca!

You do not wish to wait for books,

In which I, finally, will describe all that I see as a good spring.

Try to choose the load that is within your power,

Do not overexert yourself under the weight of stones.

The more your soul can contain itself,

The more space arises in it.

I remember the teachings of Attalus,

He was a young man for wisdom's sake:

Our goal became the benefit of both of us

One brings, and the other takes.

Philosophy is the one who brings blessings,

(On the sun, and you do not want to be scorched).

In your heart, there trembles the wisdom flag...

And, following it, you speak of good.

But many come simply to listen,

Not to learn... To have fun...

Then, you look - they repeat what someone else said in their ears,

But they do not draw conclusions, my friend!

Others are stirred by words of praise

(They are quick-witted with heart and soul):

Ready for any trouble,

To be praised by a teacher: "Good job!"

Oh, how I am familiar with the impulses of youth!

And how easily you can dissuade them...

Few reach even their doorstep,

On the path of good wishes.

It is easy to lead the way of good intentions,

There are good seeds in every soul.

With a gentle arrow, we pierce the heart with words of praise,

Then... We sink into the depths of sin.

"Poverty is stronger than wealth among the poor."

A miser is as wicked as anyone else.

The last scoundrel applauds in the theater...

If only I were not a philologist... but, a philosopher...

I have expressed simple truth in verse!

It would have conquered everyone's soul without question,

If they had forgotten their sins.

Like air passing through pipes,

It can thunder loudly over the arena.

In the strictness of poetry...

Good thoughts pass through the soul without touching it.

The rhythm of poetry - like a sharp iron stake...

Can pierce the armor of someone who is stubbornly ignorant.

Someone else argues about money...

But, the truth is simple: "Whoever wants less... will not be lacking."

He has everything that whoever wants to have, he already has.

When you hear this or something similar,

You cannot help but pay homage to it.

When it enters your soul, even more strongly...

It needs to be hammered home!

Believing in the usefulness of listening is difficult:

A speech directed towards good is beneficial.

Logic with an iron fist can lead us on the right path.

When Attalus stood up against the torrent,

I regretted: how pitiful was our human nature...

He was called "king" - like the prophets:

His words are easy to hear.

When he praised simple poverty,

I regretted that I had not been blessed...

And seeing how useless excess is to me,

I wanted to put an end to it.

Although, since then, I have held back a little

Only from eating mushrooms and fungi...

I dug my way through the "state roads" of life

With my teeth.

Since then, I do not apply any kind of pretension:

I do not want to stink... And I do not drink wine.

Since then, I have avoided baths:

In the water - there is no benefit for me.

What I could not resist...

Is not as pleasant as living modestly,

Than living with moderation.

In my youth, I studied with great enthusiasm,

And what I am doing now...

I will confess here in love... Who taught me? - He was Porphyrion, the wise.

Our Porphyrion was blessed to be a vegetarian,

Unlike Sotion, who did not eat meat.

The reasons for both are noble,

Although different...

Sotion wrote:

"Wherever there is an outlet for pleasure,

Hardness becomes our habit."

I read about it in Sotonia...

Not only did he express it, but also completed:

"How can you not see the reason,

That the soul is just a shell that changes?

In nature, nothing dies,

And souls are part of the circle of life.

A man was once human,
Then became an animal... And then again...

Whoever knew people will hardly believe

That their souls... are equal to those of animals...

If all were not like this... Then you would be moderate,

And if so... You would be without sin."

I did not eat pork and poultry for a year,

And I felt that my soul had become more agile...

In Sardinia, the Jews were exiled,

And I - from their habits.

The impulses of good are loud in my heart

In words of goodness, and in the wise example!

Whoever wanted to be a philosopher at first...

Becomes a philologist later...

Read: "Time is running out...".

A grammarian does not strive to imitate,

He notices only - the word "to run".

Whose view on philosophy is directed,

That person will shorten their life's span,

Pondering that it is not wise to delay,

Drinking wine from a cup.

And in our lives - the best is at the beginning,

Until muddiness overtakes us.

For whatever we study...

We cannot restore its original purity.

A grammarian sees:

"Illness and old age"

Next to "laziness... thinking less".

A philosopher understands that it is not wise:

"Why should you torment yourself?"

I do not condemn those who are idle:

A dog on a field looks for rabbits,

A cow searches for food on the meadow.

Take, for example, a book by Cicero

"About the state"... What will we see in it?

A wise man - injustice of the law...

A grammarian - strange and foolish kings...

A grammarian himself... writes notes:

Like "a mark" that was once called "mellifluous",

Now "melodious".

"Indeed" - now "in reality"... 

And instead of "he" - "it"... 

Ennias' works - synonymous with "work"... 

But, the main reward for him:

When he discovers where Vergilius

Took words that were "scorned by heaven's door"!

Ennias! But did Ennias write them?

No... Ennias... they were given to him by Homer.

Here, so as not to stray from the path of a grammarian,

I will say what I want you to know:

Metaphors, figures of speech - let them be...

Words that are "former words"!

Those who live "not according to their teachings"

Are enticed by a lucrative trade.

Their services are poison for our lives,

What governs us is the evil itself.

How can something improve our lives?

Who, in his own filth...?!

Many talk much... those who are supposed to rule...

But life is an ocean of dangers.

What they say - all has been said by others:

Plato, Zeno... and a long list of other names...

Can we add anything new to it?

"Let them do as they say!"

I have already said... do not give me an answer?

In another letter... I will tell you - open your mouth!

A tired person is insensitive to advice.

We are driven by curiosity.

Be healthy.
