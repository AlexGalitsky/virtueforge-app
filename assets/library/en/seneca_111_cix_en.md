# The Letter of CIX (On Helping the Wise)

Dear Seneca,

"How can a wise person help another wise person?

Who is full of good fortune, that they have a colleague to share it with?

Can someone who has overcome and surpassed others help them?"

Men are beneficial to each other, even if only to support one another's form:

A team of horses moves forward with energy and enthusiasm,

And together, they make the journey more enjoyable.

Good character fades away from idleness,

But without wise people, what is there to talk about?

The concerns of the wise are common and simple:

To help their souls discover something new,

To see the golden mean.

A foolish person can only harm another:

Where all vices come together in one place,

The most wicked person will always be ahead!

It's logical to assume that someone who is opposed will make a better argument:

With wise people, the wisest person is always at the forefront!

(As for how to distinguish between...)

People who don't like wisdom... are afraid of being part of a crowd.

There are no wise people who know everything in the world,

Even those with fiery hearts and minds.

Sometimes one person will answer your question,

And make it easy to complete the journey.

A spectator can be beneficial to a runner:

By providing a good example, they inspire others.

Just as honey is sweet and enjoyable,

But bitter if you don't appreciate its value...

(Do you want a rhyme for "smooth"?)

There's sweetness in the conversation of wise people,

Like honey that we savor and enjoy.

Is it necessary to help someone who has achieved happiness?

A wise person is always prepared to help others:

By sharing their wisdom with those around them.

Good deeds are like exercises that improve our character,

Just as a hammer strengthens the hardness of metal.

"Don't waste the present moment,

And, when with a friend, think about your path..."

Who understands this simple truth?

Has found inner peace and tranquility in their heart.

A self-centered person often sees flaws in others:

They can spot weaknesses on someone else's property.

For them, safety is what brings happiness to others:

Someone who isn't afraid will always be kind to people.

Even wise people sometimes face difficulties...

Let those who deny it know that they're lying!

Working together as a team,

Is the best way to complete a beautiful task!

The answer is... exercises!

For our wit and everything else...

How can I overcome my main doubts?

Where can I find a doctor of the soul to heal me?

Why should we teach something that's useless?

You promised me courage, but I see none...

In the midst of fires, storms, or under the iron sword,

You promised me strength... 

How can I understand the destruction of pleasures?

How can I avoid seeking glory among worlds?

How can I grow wisdom from ignorance,

By recognizing its necessity?

Be healthy.

—
