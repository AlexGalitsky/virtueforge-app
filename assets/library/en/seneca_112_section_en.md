# The Letter of SX (On True and False Bliss)

Dear Seneca,

May the blessings of the gods be upon you.

Forget about the fact that someone has given you a gift, whether it is a teacher or not. It does not matter if they are a scribe or a commoner ("Blessed with lowly station" is how they are called).

Some are called "youthful" or "divine", but all have been rewarded in both heaven and earth.

Let us look at it later, whether they have time to help with private matters.

Until then: the greatest burden of all is that you yourself created it.

And indeed, there is no greater torment than this: to be angry with oneself.

A worthy punishment - let it lie in the arms of fate.

One day, the angels will trumpet.

Who judges by names alone knows nothing but misfortune.

True happiness comes from within, not from external circumstances.

The steps to success are not happiness. The word of God is enough for a happy life.

When you have eliminated all unnecessary things, then and only then can you live with ease.

What is coarse bread? It was eaten by our ancestors to warm themselves up.

Is it not the same whether something has been digested or not?

Do not rejoice in the swine that has drowned in its own filth.

Do not rejoice in the oysters that have perished in the sea.

If you want to despise your belly, then learn to despise what is unnecessary.

The Atthal was able to clear people's eyes with his wisdom,

Recognizing himself as blind from wealth.

He counted everything that was put on display as more valuable than bread.

One day, he saw all the riches of the city and was invited to a lavish feast.

And children dressed in finery for idolatry,

The pride of those who "can do everything"!

We have gathered here to learn about jealousy?

But why did we invite you again?!

No one is great who strives to be superior through wealth.

Happiness is not found in the enjoyment of riches.

Just as spectators are not needed to enjoy the show, so too are they not needed for the display of wealth.

Since I saw the splendor of that house,

I say jokingly:

"I am familiar with the pride of children."

I address you to true riches:

To be content with little! - Bread and water

Enough is enough. And bread can be slavery.

Fortunes, always foreign to our nature.

"What if without bread?" - Not by one loaf alone,
But by the word of God, a happy man.

Look up at the sky and become closer to heaven,

For the Shepherd sees all his sheep.

Happy is not he who appears happy to others

(there are also rich people... who are thieves).

Let your humble bread be beautiful in your soul!

Your soul is the source of happiness.

Be healthy.
