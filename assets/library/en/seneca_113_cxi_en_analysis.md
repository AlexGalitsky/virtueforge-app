### Philosophical Context
Sophisms are a philosophical methodology developed in Greece, which involves a critical evaluation and analysis of all perspectives on a problem. This approach was popularized by the Latin philosopher Cicero.

### Essence
* A sophism is a critical analysis of all perspectives on a problem.
* Those who indulge in sophisms become entangled and gain nothing.
* The crowd of people who fall prey to sophisms cannot be overcome by one who sincerely seeks the truth.
* Sophisms are tricks that may lead to growth, but only for the self-interested.

### Form of This Version
Paraphrase: A sophism is a critical analysis of all perspectives on a problem, which may lead to growth, but only for those who sincerely seek the truth.

### Connection to Virtue
The alignment of sophisms with virtue can be seen as contradictory. Sophisms are often used to undermine arguments against one's own beliefs, which can result in a lack of honesty and integrity in philosophical development.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us has not succumbed to sophisms at least once in their life?
