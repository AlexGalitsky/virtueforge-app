# A Letter to a Friend on Healing

Dear Sencen,

You sent me a friend who is too rigid,
I did not infuse him with the freedom of wisdom:

He has been broken by his vices and worn down.

It is not possible to graft onto everyone:
That one is old, and that one is rotten...

Be cautious when you make an incision in the air,

There will be no result if you dig deeper underground.

A messenger cannot be given the power of reason:

He has decayed, weakened, and immediately separated from it.

The attachment to him will be rejected immediately,

I love you - not for useless endeavors...

"You are thirsty for healing!" - you believed?

Have you looked into his soul, where is it so dark?

To close the door of luxury and keep it closed,
He... had already opened the window beforehand.

"He has been tired of this life for a long time!"

Who does not get tired of excess fat?

Let us see if he will lose weight in a year,

If he wants healing?

Be healthy.
