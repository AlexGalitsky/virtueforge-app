# The Letter CXIII (On the Nature of Virtue)

Dear Seneca,

You wish to know what it means by "existence": reason, justice? Or perhaps the Greeks?

Are they mere words? Should we recognize a spark of life within them?

I say so definitively, but I will explain how one should understand.

Let us begin with virtue:

It is a state of the soul,
And therefore, even children know it.

One cannot separate existence from virtue.

The very concept of art is alive with existence,

No one among us would deny it.

There are scattered feelings within us,
At least ten in each person.

If we consider feeling to be an essence,
Then exactly that many exist within us,
Each a planet, inhabited by...

The movements of the feelings - read: exchange of matter?

I am a living being, and so is my soul,

But my body is just a dwelling place.

No one can say I have two souls,

Unless they drink all the wine in their house!

Any object is considered separate,
If it is an independent subject.

(I hope this is clear to everyone.)

One soul within me, but no more.

I said what I think and meant it,

But if you ask about virtue in passions and vices?

Then even in words, thoughts, and tears?

Do not try to solve the problem.

We see many feelings in virtue,
Their foundation - the soul.

Without virtue, one cannot live.

Can we prevent its presence?

"Feelings are like parts of a body."

Like the heads of a hydra - what is the soul?

And can each create whatever it wants?

What - frightens me?! - I am already confused...

Take justice: it is beneficial,

For everyone acts well when guided by it.

But "existence" in its name is too flattering,

All our feelings are driven by the soul.

Even those born as dogs will not become horses and pigs.

Although feelings change, however,

The one soul experiences all of them.

The soul cannot share itself,
It belongs to different beings!

(I begin to laugh,
Telling you about your foolishness.)

It is more suitable to express indignation
Than to argue.

Take two different entities:

They are different only in name.

No, the essence is different - a difference in words.

Indeed: the creator of the world with great wisdom,

Has managed to distinguish all these things from each other,
Avoiding repetition altogether.

Only if existence is alive within it,

Can it act independently.

It's not virtue - a person with an infant soul,

Influences the world with their mind.

Any feeling comes from sensations,

Then moves towards motivation, and finally

Towards agreement. A simple example of the harmony

Of the soul and body.

And can reason give consent to going for a walk?

(Although I have met... without reason,
But fortunately, it often heals them.)

Recognizing existence in goodness leads to absurd conclusions:

If goodness is courage under a flag,

Doesn't the flag hide the soul?!

(I will stop "chewing on my tongue",
Like a fool - only to start!)

Is a garment needed for the earth?

Then justice - does it want to drink?

Then we need to decide between form and content,

Between virtue or vice.

Let the circle be guided by the divine norm:

Goodness is like a ball,
And evil is like a goat's horn.

I will stop "mouthing nonsense",
Like Chrysippus showed an example of wisdom:

Rejected the "starting point" (prescribed measure)

Any talent can create, compose songs...

The soul - one, but it can change its form

According to virtue.

The question is closed. And please don't be offended:

If I speak in verse,

I would recognize Theocritus' poetry,

A work of art that is round and complete,
With six feet...

Imagine such beings facing us,

Like syllogisms or solipsism...

"Oh, the wretched glory of foolishness!

Where in thoughts - adultery, and in words - hedonism!"

Teach me not with words, but with deeds:

How to become brave, how to create strength?

So that threats do not hit me,
And I can give my weaknesses power.

Fortune may use its own weapons,

And raise or destroy...

The great Alexander, losing his friendship,

Found a new sense in it.

What are the mistakes he made?

He was conquered by lands!

Power - is it in the soul that dwells within the body's framework,
Or in the commands of powerful people?

Teach me: sacred justice!

Teach me: to forget pride and flattery!

So that generosity can shine brightly before me,

And I will be free from selfishness.

Do not take a decision based on reward,

Let the crowd of thieves search for glory...

Be just - prepare yourself for decline!

(For the wise person, it is not an effort.)
