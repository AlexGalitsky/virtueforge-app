# The Corruption of Eloquence

Lucilia greets Seneca!

What corrupts the eloquence of people?

One often finds nonsense in libraries,
Vagueness in the presentation of ideas,
And being weighed down by the significance...

(Will the essence of a speech be preserved if it is not carefully guarded?)

The ancient Greeks had a saying:
"Like what one's character is, like their speech!"

Is the government in disarray?

The eloquence of an orator will soon become effeminate!

What did he say on a public speaking engagement

Was repeated by the crowd in trivial matters.

The soul and mind are always similar in color:

A calm soul means a clear mind.

(This is especially noticeable among poets,
Who often have pretensions...)

The mind becomes affected because of the corruption of the soul.

The most terrifying thing is the wealthy "from former beggars",
Thinking, "Now is my time!"

Who has a lazy soul - cannot move their legs...

A soul in a frenzy accelerates their pace...

A mad soul jumps and flails about...

If there is a flaw in the heart, it will be reflected in the mind.

It is well known among the Muses:

"How did he live...and write here is not necessary..."

In him, what is worn - is never in place,
The rhetoric of those who are aimless and confused...

"Along the riverbanks, where the trees lean towards each other,
Look how the boats have cluttered up the current,
How, after getting drunk, they force them to retreat."

"The genius who celebrates his own festival
Is unlikely to notice it..."

He had a great gift, but wasted it on trivialities...

If he had passed it down to future generations,
At least he would have had some restraint in his speech!

Who can not see that he was unclear?!

He sat on the highest platform,

Only with his ear moving...

In times of war, when the whole city is in fear,

He walked around accompanied by a crowd

Though he was a man and more...,

Those were more courageous than his face.

He married his wife every day and went home at night,

After this life, who would not be ashamed?!

If you read his letter...

It becomes clear that he is out of shape and not humble.

His thought process is lazy, and his speech is sloppy...

The arrogance of the age - always short-lived,

Simple wisdom - is rejected by time!

There, where vice stands above honor,

Taking care of one's body is more important than other things,

They build a house that is larger than their estate,

And their estates are just chaos.

If you want to surround yourself with marble walls...

If you want to put a decorative ceiling under the floor...

The paintings of masters in elegant frames...

The feasts - for a week of indulgence.

When something seems cheap,

New words are invented,

But when it comes to conveying meaning, they fail...

They take away the essence of speech,

So that only an echo remains.

If one's thoughts are weak and dishonest,

Then the sense is a threat, arrogance, and recklessness...

If reason is not present,

Is he a poet? - A negligible parasite...

The sources of flaws are introduced into everyday life

Orators who have power over the crowd...

The great ones fade away...

The mediocre copycats follow them...

When there are many flaws in the crowd...

It's sad.

A single mistake made by one person is an example for others,

But when it happens to everyone, it becomes a plague.

In the end, it is not the words that are wrong,
But the corruption of the soul!

We should run away from this corruption!

Those who speak nonsense will be welcomed with applause

By those who are dressed in rags and wearing fur coats.

For they all judge by one reason,

What do the crowds say? - Usually, nonsense...

"But there is no speech without flaws."

I agree... But what can we praise about flaws?!

Imagine that you meet someone drunk...

Would you say: "Be forever drunk"?

There are no rules for speech,

And habits cannot last forever.

But who has directed their mind to foolishness?

And who has taken it as an example?

When there are many flaws in the crowd...

It's a tragedy.

The king of harm - and society is in harmony,

The king of harm loses his power - the contract is broken.
