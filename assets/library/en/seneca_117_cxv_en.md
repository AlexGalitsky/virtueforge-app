**The Essence of Words**

Lucilia greets Seneca!

Seek not what to write, but words:

Do not fear that your writing will be deemed "bland" in libraries.

What you write - that is what you think, and it will hold fast in deeds.

Who wants to polish their words until they shine?

That person exhausts themselves in trivialities.

Greatness - confidently bland,

But more convincing than superficiality.

For subtlety, futility is inevitable.

In words, not incendiary fire:

You know young men with beards

(As if they were stored in a trunk)... 

Without courage... their age is always short-lived,

For truth - not in hairstyles, but in hands!

I have no reason to analyze words:

Where are the words that lead?

The brilliance of speech - an ornament...

Not a man, but a refined being.

We are not given to see the soul in prosperity...

How can we see it in the shell of our bodies,

A flash of justice, courage, and reason?!

Tolerance, modesty, self-control, and kindness

And generosity - in simplicity's cloak,

The rarest good in us - humanity...

Would shine like a beacon!

But nobility - do not interrupt me.

Greatness will not be rushed.

Love for such a soul - elevates us?

Respect - teaches us the soul!

In humility, you have not rejected us:

Your face is beyond human likeness,

And your voice is not like that of men.

Ease our burden! I believe: You can help!

Be happy! Hail your star!

I worship You, not as a golden idol,

But with humility and mercy...

Not in a temple of gold,

But by will and righteous insight,

And living - simple and true.

Any one who has been consumed by love for You...

But many things hinder it,

That our gaze is blinded by brilliance,

While we ourselves are lost in darkness.

If only we had desired to purify our souls...

As we clean our eyes,

Then, in a worn-out body,

We would see the beauty of virtue...

Through poverty, weariness, and filth!

And the false light of power and wealth

Would not blind us with tired eyes...

Politics will always preach brotherhood,

But who has proven it?

Our Ariston from Chios said:

"We are all like children until the end...

Toys for our hands, foolishness - more valuable...

But we forget our Father.

He is given us a shell of marble,

That we have polished with gold,

And on its surface, we display our pride.

We catch glimpses of glory from under the roof,

And think: "Ah, what a fortunate me!"

Dignity is covered by a thin layer,

But beneath it - baseness and evil.

Money - has become our commodity.

We are convinced that there is a price for everything...

And we do not follow truth.

We praise all vices!

We have drunk from the cup of greed with children's milk,

And the entire nation worships wealth.

Even the poor curse us,

While we, in turn, curse them.

In poetry, "gold" is just a whim,

To indulge in it and revel...

Even if the age recognizes it as the best,

We have become accustomed to call it gold.

The Greeks did not mistake the image of money,

Let me recite a long line of phrases:

"If even Venus's beauty is so lovely,

She was paid for everything!"

When we heard this, the audience rose,

Under the cries of Euripides: "Recreate!"

But the heroes received their punishment...

And we - from that punishment, cannot escape.

To possess wealth - is also a test!

To watch and not forget anything...

You will see how they lament over losses,

While perceiving more than what can be!

If fortune smiles upon us,

Who has not lost - becomes bold,

What could have been ours, but was not...

No one is content with happiness,

Even when it reaches their hands.

Only the wise know: Happiness is in our power!

Everything - in the present, in the future - a fortress.

Let words flow as you wish,

And do not cherish someone else's opinion.

Who builds his soul without changing his body?

That person alone can live happily!
