### Philosophical Context
This text is part of a correspondence between Lucilius and Seneca, in which they discuss the importance of words and their impact on human life. In this letter, Lucilius emphasizes the importance of words not being merely superficial or distorted for the sake of an end, but rather being sincere and committed.

### Core Message
* Words should not be merely superficial, but should be sincere and committed.
* Words should convey a sense of humanity and virtue.
* Words should be a tool for achieving a goal, not an end in themselves.
* The human soul cannot perceive its true nature through the guise of physical characteristics alone.

Virtue and humanity are key concepts in this text. Words should be a tool for achieving a goal, not an end in themselves, which aligns with the idea that virtue is not merely the performance of certain actions, but also the internalization of these values.
