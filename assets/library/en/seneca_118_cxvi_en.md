# Letters from Lucilius
## Letter CXVI (On Passions)

Dear Seneca,

Today, let us discuss passions. They are a form of moral weakness, an unworthy thing to stir up.

Is it enough to merely suppress our passions, or should we strive to eradicate them entirely?

For those who doubt their own resolve, let us examine the mirror of passion.

I only free you from sin, taking nothing away from you:

"Desire, not craving!" - This is my lesson

Do not be deterred by the truth; do not let passion turn into an obsession...

(Many books have been written about this)

And add a touch of sharpness to our indulgences,

Be master, not slave, to your desires.

"How heavy are love's sufferings... How painful is the falsehood of strangers' opinions!"

Any vice finds justification, especially at first, when it seems so appealing...

Then, it spreads widely and consumes us. Let it arise, and you will be lost.

Like a flood, our vices sweep us away,

All we need to do is clear the way at the dam.

"There is an inherent source of passion in all things,
The mind is always full of worries."

Do not let your emotions control you; you are not for them, but they are for you.

Nature has added pleasures to necessary things: peace and hard work,

Like a feast on a weekly basis.

But if we indulge more often... People die younger.

"Allow me to grieve, allow me to fear,
A little!" - Forgive me: while you are not wise...

So, let us not try to proceed without knowing where it ends.

One young man (he was still a child) asked:

"Do I have the gift of love?"

Panaetius replied with elegance:

"How will it be with someone who is not wise?
I am not wise... but if you pursue passion,

Your soul is like an antelope that runs to meet the hunter,

Biting and scratching with its hooves.

Is love easy? We have fallen into its trap...

Is love difficult? Then we go into battle, and fall into its trap as well.

The rejected one wants to be paired,
With those who do not dare to stand up to us.

So, let us remain in our quiet place...

Do not trust the instability of your soul

A wine cup and a beautiful maiden,
What tempts us - to drink and sin?

Let us substitute "passion" for "love", as it is mentioned:

We will stand firm on solid ground,

Not wavering on dry land.

"Our passions are like a spider's web,
We cannot resist their gifts!"

The reason is not "I don't want to" or "I'm not able":

A tense spirit can achieve anything.

Be healthy.
