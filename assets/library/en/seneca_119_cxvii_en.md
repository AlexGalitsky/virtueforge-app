# The Wisdom of Being Wise

Lucilia salutes Seneca!

Your letter has brought me a mix of emotions:

"Is wisdom and being wise a good thing?" - I wish I had someone to discuss this with.

The Stoic says: "Wisdom is a good thing, but being wise is not." And so?

(Ah, patience, let me explain...)

Is the benefit a sign of a good thing?

In inaction, there is no benefit from a good thing,

And wisdom is a good thing... (Let's start a story...

"Are you trying to be wise all the time?" - What does "all the time" mean?

Is it part of the list of good things...?

I'll apply the concept of substitution:

"Unconditional" results are signs.

(I think differently, but I won't reject the axiom...)

The Stoics started my reasoning...

It doesn't mean I'll finish their ideas...

Everyone knows: there's a custom among the defeated

To appeal to mercy from the crowd,

I'm armed with conviction,

And now I'm walking on my own path.

"Running" and "to run" are different understandings,

Although they come from the same origin.

"To be beautiful" is a sign of being beautiful in one's surroundings,

"Immodesty" - evil, and "being immodest" - bottomless.

There's a good thing, an evil thing, and a third that's neutral.

Perhaps "To be wise" is not a good thing... Not worth arguing about,

Evil? - It's impolite to think so!

Saying it like that - an insult to the mind!

So what?

"Being wise" - not a good thing... Maybe

I'm not going to argue with you.

Is "evil and wisdom" - two in one?

The Peripatetic is right:

Wisdom and wisdom are one in essence.

But the dialectician is weak when it comes to:

Why do we need to distinguish them?

Let's take "control of a field" and "the owner"

There's commonality and difference...

(Ah, dialectic! - Don't be afraid of you!

Like many others, you've added nuances!)

Wisdom and wisdom are from the same nature,

But in "the field" and "the owner" - there are two things!

It's more suitable to compare a crowd than someone who has kept their wits about them...

Without applying it, wisdom is like a black flag

Flying high!

I won't repeat myself:

What can't be of benefit become not good,

Can't lose its goodness.

Trust me, I've been scolding myself for hours,

After getting tangled up in this long conversation...

Life is short! Words have another mark...

Hours steal away like a thief,

Days and years disappear,

And life slips away!

I need your help to become wiser!

I feel the weight of death pressing down on me!

Will I become wise? Or will I die sooner?

I want to be wise... But why not live?!

Be healthy.
