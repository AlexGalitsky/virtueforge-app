**Letter CXVIII (On Chastity and Happiness)**

Lucilius greets Seneca!

You want me to write more often...

Do you remember the promise? Did I blush?

I swear, I will send a letter ahead of time...

And, I am not Cicero... You are my salvation

I need it! The same principle as Atticus:

Everything that comes to mind, write it down...

I have no shortage of topics for letters,

I won't write like Cicero:

Who takes on a position with a firm handshake,

But who, for money, bypasses the law.

It's better to avoid touching someone else's property,

Sit in front of a mirror for half an hour:

We claim many things in life,

But rarely gather our voices...

It's more pleasant to achieve nothing

And avoid trying to win the favor of fortune,

By insulting your opponent,

Or making fun of the voter.

One promises a large sum of money,

(Not me, with my middlemen)

Another promises to make all women happy,

(Later, he won't even give you his hand...)

Let the consuls and magistrates be elected:

They pass by like an exciting game!

Let someone win, let someone lose,

And us - it's time to think:

It's not the one who sleeps in darkness

Who is great with a full soul...

He votes for happiness.

"Vatinian - elected, Catonian - failed":

I don't need fortune's favor...

We can write about this forever,

Without getting bogged down in trivialities!

Let thousands of people run around in circles...

Is it bad? - No - it's worse with death!

We have happiness - complaints and debts:

We want to catch the bird of happiness!

What do you think are the peaks?

Are they just steps?

We need to lift ourselves up above our feet...

Forward! For what is good? - Yes, for that, by rumor...

We fear that others... won't be able to keep up...

What we see is a mess from haste:

The crowd thinks it's good,

But we mustn't waste our time looking for something that doesn't exist!

"Nature attracts souls with its call,

The summons to itself."

If - on the contrary, it's bad?

Look: vice doesn't take long to get used to...

Truth and right don't bend to falsehood,

Happiness meets truth alone.

Who met failure once... won't come back.

"The desire for happiness is only from nature,

Because of that, we're drawn to it!"

A house that deserves an entrance will not bring us trouble,

But a house that doesn't deserve it...

Will always be a source of trouble!

Chastity - the root of all good things:

The flag of our struggle is "to be honest"!

Honesty can't be separated from truth:

We must judge everything honestly,

And bring happiness to people who are lucky.

From past evils, happiness grows:

And honesty doesn't come from evil!

"Agreement with nature" - a significant sign!

How can you deny this property?

A size that's not big, but still noticeable...

Is it not the same as a small stone?

But even if it's not large in size,

The final touch is what we see:

And when we finally see completion!

There's also continuity of change:

Look - there's movement... and then no movement...

Have you seen a stop? - No. Take a look...

My size is changing - but something new,

In harmony with nature, it sees happiness at its core!

The soul understands infinity

Without limits.

Be healthy.
