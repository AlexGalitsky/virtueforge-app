# The Letter of the 19th (On Wealth)

Lucilius greets Seneca,

I teach you: you will become rich

Without creditors, and no one to complain to!

I'll find a reliable shoulder for you...

Remember what Cato said:

"Take everything from yourself!"

To have and not want is the same,
The outcome is the same - do not suffer.

I don't teach you to refuse nature:

She will always demand her due!

But, anything beyond her reach

Is pointless, unnecessary debt.

I am hungry - I'll fill my belly!

And what's the point of filling it?

Nature will still take it away!

From a golden cup or a handful of grain:

Water is water, wine is wine.

Look at your goal - I see it right in front of me...

Then you can't miss it!

The hunger that whispers to us: "Look, something nearby!"

I'll make everything delicious, no matter what!

You look sad, your cheeks are pale...

You think: "How cruelly deceived I was:

By promising poverty, when in fact I have wealth..."

One had all he wanted - all he desired...

Another had a house full of wealth...

Who was richer? - The first.

Why is this so hard to understand?

The simplicity of the truth is lost on us.

A house that's full to the brim - what's missing?

It's not just about having something, but also about wanting it.

The desire for more and more is a never-ending cycle...

And those who try to curb it will be left with nothing but poverty.

(Since... we're almost at death's door...)

We need something to cover our eyes!

Some people get free medical care from the state...

Others can't even be buried properly!

The rich are envied by many, and everyone shouts: "Look!"

But the wise ones are happy from within.

Wealth is full of riddles for poverty...

Rome is filled with excesses...

Wealth is like a poison that affects everyone...

It's hard to measure it by natural desire,

Without paying for its extravagances!

In five words, I'll summarize:

Don't mix corruption with your desires!

You ask: how do you choose the right thing?

What price should we put on something above all else?

It doesn't matter. I just want to say:

Nature needs only food and necessities.

Horace said: "Desire is nothing when it comes to water,

No matter who gives it to us."

Whoever takes the earth without giving back...

Is like someone who takes a piece of clay and shapes it into something new.

But if you don't use your imagination, you'll never be satisfied!

The only thing nature needs from us is life.

Let's be healthy.
