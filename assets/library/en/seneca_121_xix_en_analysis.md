### Philosophical Context
In this letter, Seneca critiques the notion that wealth is the key to happiness and contentment. He argues that wealth can lead to destruction and dissatisfaction if it does not serve the benefit of the individual.

### Essence
* Wealth is not the primary source of happiness.
* Wealth can lead to ruin if it is not used for the well-being of the person.
* The idea that wealth is the key to happiness is a misconception.
* All necessity is the necessity of life and health.
* Wealth should be used to promote the well-being of the individual.

### Form of This Version
Transparent and straightforward, without any hints or parantheses.

### Connection to Virtue
Wealth is not the primary factor of virtue. Virtue lies in using wealth to promote the well-being of others and oneself.
