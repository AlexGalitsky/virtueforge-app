# The Letter XX (On the Origin of Good)

Lucilius greets Seneca!

From where does honesty and good come into us?

Is it from books we have read in libraries?

Or is it from the experience of our soul, which has seen with its own eyes?

What is the heart of honesty and good?

Is it useful to be good? - That means applying

It to all things, even to horses and wine,

And to shoes? - No, humility is worse than lying.

Where is honesty? - In understanding one's duty?

In caring for our elderly parents,

In being willing to leave home for a long time,

And in fighting for victory until the end?

I will say this: what is called good

Is not worthy of evil. How could we not know...

That our nobles abuse their wealth and power?

Let me return to the question: Where does good come from

And honesty? - Nature could not have given us

Knowledge, which is a fruit for many.

But she has given us seeds of knowledge.

I do not believe that goodness suddenly appeared

In full bloom. Only those who seek it will find it,

Even if they are lacking in heaven's stars.

How did our ancestors think that goodness came about?

Through analogy. (As the word was born, it is not a witness,

But now it is accepted, acknowledge it.)

I explain (not making concessions to future generations):

What makes brave actions shine? - People see their excellence

We have bowed down before them, not noticing the hidden flaw:

The law of nature - everyone sees its glory

Where does the brilliance of action lie?

He who did not take gold from Pyrrhus,

Refused his royal treasures.

Then he warned against the poison of the banquet,

And did not soil himself with his own vice.

He did not let wealth conquer him,

Nor was he poisoned by evil.

Behold, what is the greatness of spirit and brotherhood!

What shines through the mess, like a star!

Seneca said: Live - my blessing

And loyalty, which has troubled you!

It's not in gold that I have created images,

But in what Pyrrhus pitied, loved!

Horatius (the subject of the noble poet)

One fought against his enemy on the bridge,

Breaking it behind him.

(Perhaps you did not see this,

Or maybe you will not believe me here...)

And crying out when the beams cracked:

"Who wants to go my way?"

He jumped into the river... arrows followed him,

He came out whole, with sword and shield.

Such deeds give us an image of goodness.

On the backdrop of evil - a beautiful picture

Of an honorable and brave warrior.

Purities masquerade as good:

The lazy one scolds honest labor,

Considering himself bold... That's arrogance!

Even to the stupid it seems: He is cunning...

Perhaps pretending to be generous:

"Give" and "not keep" - I am not equal to them!

He is a critic of his own vices,

What he lost on his journey.

The brave warrior trembles before people,

Not at the crowds... Who fears poverty?

And when he is poor, he is happy

Because he is not afraid of being despised by others.

Such similarity makes us:

Look beyond the external, and consider the essence!

How often we praise a deed,

But forget that it's the person who deserves praise!

Imagine another person:

Tolerant of enemies, and kind to friends,

What will he do? - He will do what is done

And in small things, there won't be any fault.

He is wise, patient, and clever,

His spirit always reigns over his weak body,

Purposes - not harming him with vices,

He remains constant in thoughts and actions,

Whatever he starts, it will lead to the end...

(I have drawn an image that is impossible?)

But... did our weak hearts stir?

When we saw goodness shining!

We take after it:

Let us restrain our desires

And be kind to our neighbor.

Forget fear, do calmly what needs to be done,

Give away debts, and stop lying to people,

Learn to live: a quarter of life has passed...

Be prepared - not preparing yourself: you will die!

Then we understand: what is happiness in living?

Not requiring anything from life...
