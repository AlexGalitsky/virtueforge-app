### Philosophical Context
This text is a letter from Horace to Seneca, in which he poses questions about the origin of virtue and benefit.

### Essence
- Honesty and benefit are instilled within us through the experience of the soul.
- The core of honesty and benefit lies in the understanding of duty.
- It is beneficial to apply benefit in life.
- Virtue is given by nature, but it requires seeking and rational application.

### Form of This Version
prose

### Connection to Virtue
Virtue is linked to the understanding of duty and the importance of its application in life. As Seneca notes, virtue is the key to benefit and honesty.

### Question to Ponder
Who among us can truly understand virtue and its significance in life if not those who are willing to give themselves for others?
