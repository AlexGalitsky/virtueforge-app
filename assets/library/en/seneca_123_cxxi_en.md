# The Letter CXXI (On Man and Animals)

Lucilla greets Seneca.

A question is not easy to answer, you will say: I am wrong.

I present to you Archidemus, my colleague, who also understood what it means to be "good".

Not everything that is considered good by many is good.

What cultivates the soul is goodness.

One thing is in the clothes we wear today,
Another is in observing fasts...

The seventh is what is learned through training,
The eighth is the beauty of hair and body.

They study their origin there,
Here, they correct those who want to.

By studying the nature of man,
And his preference for it,

You, Seneca, do not deviate from the norms:

Whoever seeks light should shine in the window!

You will see there fields and gardens,
The glow of the sun, the movement of grass...

Not knowing one's role by nature

How can you know the true norm?

"I want to get rid of prejudices,

Forget: how I have been lucky, how I have not been lucky!

Do not torment my mind with the idea of happiness.

Words are easy to add extra syllables..."

I will do it, so they say: "Not knowing one's measure"

He criticized evil and sin...

Will see evil - examples of good behavior,

Will understand that it is impossible to transgress beyond a limit!

Is ready to interrupt prayers for God!

Do not distract God with trivialities:

We "are not talking about" prayed too much,

It was fulfilled, and it became our trouble...

We envy the movements of animals:

In them, by nature - beauty began.

There - a bee flies without tension

Its flight is not taught to anyone.

For us, movement is more frightening than adventures...

Let the dancers be light in their movements:

Their lightness is years of training,

And here - with a wave of your hand,

They are given naturally - to run faster,
To fly, to crawl, to climb up and swim!

But I do not regret it much:

Our reason is higher than their flight!

A child gets up... falls and cries...

Gets up again, without noticing the pain...

Who wants to get up? Does not pray for luck,

Wants to conquer himself!

Let's put a turtle on its back

It's uncomfortable for it, hard to breathe...

And it stretches, and turns, and moves with a wave of its body

It will turn over if you don't disturb it.

There are no animals that are tied in movements,

Even newborns, adults - all the same

Their bodily condition is an experience by nature.

"What is the state?" - Ah, what does it mean?

"Is it the soul and the body?"

- What's the point? 

Animals find their solution here,

Not through words, but naturally.

A child is trained from birth...

And who can tell us how to train him?

He "knows his state" not at all,

But feels: living being!

We have a soul, but what is it?!

Where does it come from?! 

Sometimes it troubles me more than the heart, the head, or the body.

It's quiet during the day, and it speaks at night...

It whispers: Wait! Or it shouts: Hurry!

We have a guiding beginning,

Which we call the appearance of the soul.

The living one is able to adapt

To what happens outside of him,

And our reason is a means to become stronger,

And higher than nature's essence.

While we live - we change our state,

From infant, boy, youth, old man...

And what then? - Only a few are sculpted...

But all, without exception, are mortal.

We change ourselves and our appearance,

But everyone remains himself.

Adaptation is also multifaceted,

It's an everyday and invisible struggle.

I yearn for pleasures, not pains,

I seek protection, I cry out about fate...

What is it? - All my desires, no pain

(In that - our essence), worries about ourselves.

The care of oneself is a crucial principle,

Laid down by nature in everyone.

A chicken avoids the hawk's bird,

As if it knew: death and pain.

Do not ask where this knowledge comes from,

If you have never experienced it yourself?

A dog for a chick - like a family member,

And a cat - a sign of terrible misfortune.

What is not threatened - they do not fear it,

Death is the same to all, regardless of age.

The fear of growing old cannot change,

It does not grow until others grow up.

We love safety by nature,

Like fish: hearing the sound and moving towards the bottom...

Our experience teaches us late and unclear,

What is natural to everyone at once!

The living one tries to preserve itself:

It flees from what harms it,

And seeks what is beneficial, and is restless...

In harmony with nature.

Look at bees - beautiful dwellings,

Agreement in dividing work!

Who can compare themselves to a spider?

Catches food? Or is puzzled by its web?

But how beautifully they weave the threads:

Direct lines, further - in a circle,

More often in the middle than on the edges,

And all angles are equal to each other!

And strength?! - Who has ever managed to break it?!

Nature teaches us self-preservation,

To everyone equally.

In sciences, you will not find salvation,

For those who cannot value themselves!

Squirrels that are useless for hunting,

Can adapt and thrive:

They have learned to be cunning...

You have not yet succeeded - they already reached the highest points.

Be healthy.
