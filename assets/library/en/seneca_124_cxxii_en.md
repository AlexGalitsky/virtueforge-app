# The Antithesis

Lucilia greets Seneca!

The day has shortened, and it's a shame for those who lift their eyelids,
Only in the middle of the day, when laziness is chased away.

But there's still space enough in the evening to see,
For those who catch the first light of dawn:
Birds sing sweet melodies,
And God gives them the key to discover the world.

There are, however, people like Mark Antony,
Who say, "Hold on!" - as if it were a challenge.

They're eerie, like nocturnal birds,
With a midnight cup filled with wine,
They bury themselves. It's a shame for them:
They mourn the dead during the day.

In the affairs of work, days are not long,
For the meaning and sign of life is to change it!

A day is not lacking? - Let's move the bedpost
Away from the night, just a little bit in favor of the day.

Birds are fed in the darkness, so they can fatten up,
And sleep peacefully during the day.
The bodies of the dead, relaxed and pale,
But that's the least of their sins:
Harming our health with dark thoughts is a greater evil,
And poisoning our souls with wickedness is a greater shame.

Perversity fights against nature,
To make it worse:
Those born with defects,
Come out backwards, from behind to front.

They drink wine before breakfast, filling their empty veins
With fortified wine in the morning's pre-banquet.
Young people, who often seek strength,
End up going down faster than anyone else.

After dinner, they sit and drink,
Like fathers of families in villages...

Those who don't know the joys of life,
Have only one dream: to forget their hard days.
Others put on women's clothes,
And prolong their childhood.

In men's pursuits - all their hopes are tied up...
It's more like shame than fault:
Some build a bath in the sea, thinking it's luxury,
But when they're in the bath, they're still arguing with the waves!
In an orange grove, they smell roses,
Although winter has come and gone.

When trying to reverse nature,
I see excess as a nuisance.
First, they twist and turn, then - completely gone
From their path in life:
They sleep through the morning, when the sun rises,
But spend the whole night laughing and having fun!

"Not living like everyone else!" - that's what they think is wise,
We're above the crowd and all the people!
"We have our own dawn,
Our own schedule of life, with our own desires!"

Attilius Buthus was deceived by night:
He squandered his father's inheritance,
Tiberius said, "You're late waking up!"
When he saw his pale face.

Montanus was known for being a good poet,
He loved to recite poems in front of the audience...

(He loved words like "sets" and "dawns")

To compose everywhere - now it's impossible!
His voices were loud and raspy,
The listener counted the damage!

Pinius said, "I'll listen until sunset
And demonstrate my generosity!"
Montanus read: "The sun rises slowly...
But I'm already awake!"

Var cried out: "Stop! Please speak softer,
Buthus is sleeping peacefully in his wine cellar...
He's so exhausted after a fruitless night!"

Montanus read: "A shepherd drives the flock,
And the earth is covered with silence..."
Var said, "Excuse me, I need to leave,
I have to go to Buthus' dinner party..."

Vices don't fit into the common mold,
Those without a clear conscience - their eyes pierce the light,
Why are they silent? They're already in trouble...
Their gossip is like a friendly greeting.

They should not be remembered:
Among transgressors, rogues, and thieves,
They must never repeat themselves,
And emerge from the shadows as reformers!

In the early hours of the night - accounts are kept,
At six o'clock - voices are mixed,
Before dawn - worries are calmed:

After leaving the bath - they go to dinner and sleep.

For those who follow nature's path,
Pride and modesty are their guiding principles.
Happiness comes equally to all,
But each person finds their own key to happiness or misery:
Bad reputation is a reward for bad behavior,
And common sin is not looked at in the mouth:

The more excesses there are - the more they rejoice,
Their life goes against nature.

I'll finish with today's lesson
On those who live like moles:
They spend their whole life "swimming against the current",
And many of them "reach the shore".

Be healthy.
