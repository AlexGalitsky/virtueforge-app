### Philosophical Context
Letter CXXII is part of Seneca's philosophical letter where he emphasizes the importance of virtue and opposing the antipodes. In this letter, Seneca criticizes individuals who prioritize personal interests and pleasures over the common good and morality.

### Essence
- Seneca underscores the importance of opposing the antipodes, who prioritize personal interests and pleasures over the common good and morality.
- He asserts that people who live their lives like moles, "rowing against the current," often "arrive nowhere," meaning they cannot survive in difficult circumstances.
- Seneca also highlights the importance of morality and virtue in life, stating that those who follow nature and live according to reason are the happiest and most content.
