Have you had breakfast? Why do you need dinner?

Has anyone ever had everything they wanted?

All are powerful - not wanting what there is not.

I don't need comfort, medicines, or baths,

My health comes from hard work.

Such a simple supper, after a long day of work,

Is more pleasant than the overindulgence of feasts.

He who, without preparation, overcomes obstacles

Has a heart accustomed to training:

There is no place for anger, no room for laziness.

Look at things: do we really need all this?

Why did you want to acquire it?

The light of reason does not guide us on our way,

A bad example leads us astray.

No one would be willing to serve the lust of greed,

Try me, and I will call out:

But, deceptions - are above all truths,

When they have the voice of the majority behind them.

When the roads intersect,

Everyone knows that an important guest is coming.

Are you ashamed if you are one of many?

Ah, you are not ashamed to be one!

Avoid talking to such people:

Do not listen to their words immediately.

The seeds of sin are more terrifying than thieves:

Spiritual sicknesses will grow from them.

Like after a musical lesson,

We hear the loud sound of the trumpet.

Words of flatterers and advocates of vice

Will settle in our soul for a long time.

You have not yet closed your hands to shut out their words,

When temptation tries to speak.

It begins to say - you are inclined to listen,

And it becomes bold, right before your eyes!

In the end, it convinces itself boldly:

"Good and justice are nothing! 

Whoever wants to live will 'twirl' cleverly!

There is no more happiness: Give me money!

And leave an inheritance for heirs!

Such enemies you will acquire - be careful!

Do not think about your soul or good fame,

Think about how you can live without danger!

Remember the journey of Ulysses:

He listened to the song of sirens bound.

Gold blinds us from the light of our homeland,

And turns our ship weak.

Wealth and honor attract... 

But hunger, pain, and hard work repel them.

Blessed are those who do not dream of the first,

Who know that their fears often lie.

He who goes up the mountain reaches the top,

He who goes down will return to it:

In the affairs of fools - there is no help for the soul,

And in pleasures - no one can restrain.

Pleasure-mongers spread evil and frighten us,

Those who, under the pretext of advice,

Say: "You are guilty... you rule yourself!

What is superstition? - It's just foolishness:

We should fear those we must love!

Is it not true that what we believe in idols?

There is no hope for healing with a doctor

Who wants to justify our sin.

Be healthy.
