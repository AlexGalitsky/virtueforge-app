# The Nature of Goodness

Dear Seneca,

I will tell you the stories of old,
What our ancestors have been given from time immemorial.

But, listen and reason must be joined together.

Your mind is subtle, your ears are not misled,
You always practice what is beneficial to you...

In my last letter, I say: there is no closer truth

I do not trust anyone with my thoughts.

The question is: how can we understand goodness?

Is it through reason or feeling? - Give me an answer,

Let us see: in the animal kingdom - and the truth is different,

Goodness - that is light!

Whoever values pleasure above all else,
Thinks that goodness is sensual. But we

Recognize: feelings lead to mistakes,

And minds directed towards sin.

For feelings, enjoyment are pleasant,

But, in some cases, they are just an illusion...

Why do I convince you of the opposite?

Forget gluttony, forget pain!

In them - there is no sin, when it is a matter of feeling...

But, reason pronounces its verdict:

"Goodness is in feeling!"... Until now

Who can distinguish between small things and nonsense?

Blind people, who have God's eyes closed.

Seeing the difference, understanding becomes much easier,

Than not seeing it - by touching with one's finger.

"But all art and science take their beginnings from certain feelings!"

I agree: feelings are like hands to us...

But, I do not learn goodness from them.

"Is life blessed when in harmony with nature?" 

It is called "the beginning of all blessings",

What wise men acquire after many years

No one can immediately grasp it,

Except those who have been born with it.

There is no good in a newborn baby,

And - in a tree, in an animal, unconscious...

(Is this analogy rough?... )

In what way are they different?

It is only in development that the body is formed,

A baby... grows up!

There are some who are not rational from birth,

Some who are not rational until now,

Some who have become rational with practice.

But there is no good in any of them...

Different worlds, different realms,

No good exists where there is no master of reason!

Whoever is irrational - cannot understand goodness

Whoever "is for a while" - it's hard to say immediately...

If you want to acquire it, try to find out:

What is good for your soul?

Is it not above all the gifts?!

You are not lucky, you are blessed!

Postscript

Goodness is a matter of correcting one's nature!

Seneca, I have shared with you my wisdom,

"Would you like to show me a guarantee that is greater than you?"

I do not expect praise...

My thoughts will not be influenced by your opinion:

When I see my own character in the mirror,

I say: whoever has no goodness - is wrong.

May God bless you!

Why do you train yourself every day?

God gave us strength - not more than animals!

Do you admire your reflection in the mirror?

Animal beauty is not superior to human beauty!

Do you groom your hair?

Look at the manes of lions and horses!

We run badly, naturally...

Why do we need training every day?

Now the question is: Do you want to give up everything...

What will be your downfall?!

To find goodness, try to purify your soul!

Overcome sin with your mind!

Say: Lord, I will not forget You!

Love for You - I will keep in myself!

Your highest gift - my soul and my reason!

And, for me, there is no goal other than perfection!

I know that it will not come immediately...

But, to me, it's not worth waiting for years...

Blessed is he who finds the source of all joys!

What everyone wants to take away from you?

It is your goodness!

Dares to say: "Reason - our power"!

Find out what makes you happy:

What is good for your soul?!

You are not lucky, you are blessed!
